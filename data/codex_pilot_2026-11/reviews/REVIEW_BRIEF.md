# Independent reviewer brief

Review one assigned module and its sources, blueprint, materials, rendered standard/compact guide and assessment without reading the author’s internal review or REPORT.md first. Write findings to a new review file; do not modify source/manuscript/shared code.

Independently solve every numeric task, including ones not registered as calculations.Compare outcomes and competency wording to the source crop.Check each outcome has a substantive teaching activity and observable assessment.Check every card/reference points to something actually supplied.Check teacher examples and assessment agree and that student files do not disclose the teacher key.Check scientific explanations, safety, required materials, practical fallbacks, pacing and prerequisites.Distinguish actual errors from style preferences and classroom choices.

For each confirmed finding provide severity, exact file/location/quote, evidence or recomputation, proposed correction and dependency impact.Do not claim a module clean if a source or material was unread.No fixed finding quota.Record the model and whether any school-classroom judgement remains needed.

After the fresh manuscript review, a separate comparison may inspect the baseline generators/data module and its outputs.Use the same rubric and disclose differing durations/source coverage.The Core pilot is 2.5, not the earlier pilot’s 2.4.No content superiority or cost saving follows merely from fewer reviewer findings.
