CORE MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: AREA OF POLYGONS
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only pilot • teacher review copy

Duration
12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes

Driving question
How can we defend a garden covering estimate? KICD: How do we work out the area of polygons? How do we apply the concept of the area of polygons in real-life situations?

Source learning outcomes
derive the formula for the area of a triangle given two sides and an included angle,
work out the area of a triangle given two sides and an included angle,
determine the area of a triangle using Heron’s formula,
determine the area of quadrilaterals in different situations,
work out the area of regular heptagon and octagon,
determine the area of irregular polygons in different situations,
explore the area of polygons as used in real-life situations.

Preparation and evidence
Use the companion Student Materials and Materials Teacher Key. Card identifiers are local references,not web links. Practical demonstrations require the teacher’s local risk
assessment. The standard CBE export and structured JSON are supplied separately. Independent review and classroom confirmation are recorded in the pilot report.
