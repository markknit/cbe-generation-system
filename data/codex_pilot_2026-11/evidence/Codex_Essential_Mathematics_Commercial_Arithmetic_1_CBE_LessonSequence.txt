                                  OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 2.8 • 9 lessons • teacher review copy


                                                               SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Essential Mathematics

Strand                           2.0 Measurements and Geometry

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand 2.8: Commercial Arithmetic (1)

Total Duration                   9 lessons × 40 minutes = 360 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Budget; Discounts, and commission; Profit and loss; Conversion of different currencies

Learning Outcomes                prepare budget to for use in clubs or societies,
                                 calculate discount and percentage discounts in different situations,
                                 determine commission and percentage commissions in the different situations
                                 determine percentage profit and loss made in the sale of goods,
                                 convert currencies using exchange rates tables,
                                 recognise the role of commercial arithmetic in day-to-day life.

Core Competencies                Citizenship: The learner develops ethical and digital citizenship skills when obtaining digital or printed currency exchange rates
                                 charts/tables, and working out currency conversion from Kenya Shillings to any other currencies.
                                 Critical thinking and problem solving: The learner acquires interpretation and inference skills when role play shopping scenarios
                                 where discounts are given and work out the discounts and the percentage discount.

Core Values                      Peace: the learner respects self and others by listening to others’ opinion while brainstorming brainstorm scenarios under which
                                 commissions are paid to salespersons for sale of goods and services.
                                 Patriotism: the learner displays loyalty by showing love for one's own country's currency when obtaining digital or printed currency
                                 exchange rates tables and working out currency conversion from Kenya Shillings to any other currencies.

Science & Engineering            Mathematical modelling, proportional reasoning and verification.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Financial literacy: the learner’s financial skills are enhanced when obtaining digital or printed currency exchange rates
Issues (PCIs)                    charts/tables, and working out currency conversion from Kenya Shillings to any other currencies.

Career Connections               Bookkeeping, retail, sales and financial administration.

Focus for Lessons                Prepare, calculate and justify everyday financial decisions with explicit percentage bases and exchange-rate direction.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   How can a school club make and defend an affordable financial decision? KICD: Why do we need a budget? Why are discounts
Questions                        and commissions necessary in trading activities?
 Anchoring Phenomenon                     A fictional school club can balance its first budget but wants to know whether advertised discounts and exchange offers improve its
                                          position.

 Storyline Thread                         Budget -> discounts -> commission -> profit/loss -> currency direction -> reverse conversion -> integrated decision ->
                                          communication -> independent assessment.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Illustrative quotations, sales agreements and a dealer exchange board. Figures are not real market prices or current rates.
