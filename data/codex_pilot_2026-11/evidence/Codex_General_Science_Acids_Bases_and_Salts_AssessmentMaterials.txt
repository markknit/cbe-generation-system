G10 — Independent assessment evidence
All numbers are illustrative. Aqueous solutions P, Q, R have approximate indicator pH3,
8, 7 near room temperature. A5.00 g sample ends at 5.80 g and forms a solution after
exposure to air; another 5.00 g hydrated sample ends at 4.50 g and becomes powdery,
with water the only exchanged material. A laboratory record names dilute hydrochloric
acid and magnesium; another names hydrochloric acid and calcium carbonate. Use
these data with the unit’s reaction and safe-use models.
