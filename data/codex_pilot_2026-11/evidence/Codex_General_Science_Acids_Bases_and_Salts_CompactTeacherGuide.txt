GENERAL SCIENCE GRADE 10: ACIDS BASES AND SALTS
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only pilot • teacher review copy

Duration
10 lessons × 40 minutes = 400 minutes

Driving question
Which evidence helps us explain and use these substances responsibly? KICD: Why are salts important in day-to-day life? How do salts behave when exposed to air?

Source learning outcomes
distinguish between acids and bases using the universal indicator and pH chart,
explain the role of acids and bases in biological and chemical processes,
identify products of chemical reactions of acids and bases,
classify salts according to their behaviour when exposed to air,
outline applications of salts in real-life situations,
promote proper use of salts in day-to-day life.

Preparation and evidence
Use the companion Student Materials and Materials Teacher Key. Card identifiers are local references,not web links. Practical demonstrations require the teacher’s local risk
assessment. The standard CBE export and structured JSON are supplied separately. Independent review and classroom confirmation are recorded in the pilot report.
