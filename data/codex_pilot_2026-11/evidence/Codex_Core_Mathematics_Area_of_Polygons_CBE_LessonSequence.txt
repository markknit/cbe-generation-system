                           CORE MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: AREA OF POLYGONS
                           OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 2.5 • 12 lessons • teacher review copy


                                                         SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                10

Subject                    Core Mathematics

Strand                     2.0 Measurements and Geometry

Sub-Strand                 Sub-Strand 2.5: Area of Polygons

Total Duration             12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes

Sub-Strand Content         Area of a triangle using A=1/2 ab sin C; area of a triangle using Heron’s formula; area of quadrilaterals; area of regular and
                           irregular polygons; application of area of polygons.

Learning Outcomes          derive the formula for the area of a triangle given two sides and an included angle,
                           work out the area of a triangle given two sides and an included angle,
                           determine the area of a triangle using Heron’s formula,
                           determine the area of quadrilaterals in different situations,
                           work out the area of regular heptagon and octagon,
                           determine the area of irregular polygons in different situations,
                           explore the area of polygons as used in real-life situations.

Core Competencies          Critical Thinking and Problem Solving: the learner uses trigonometric ratios to generate the formula for the area of a triangle given
                           two sides and an included angle.
                           Creativity and Imagination: the learner identifies shapes from the immediate environment that have the shapes of polygons and
                           calculates their area.
                           Learning to Learn: the learner researches and discuss the use of polygons in real-life situations.

Core Values                Patriotism: the learner uses the objects from the immediate environment and takes care of the environment.
                           Responsibility: the learner identifies objects in the immediate environment and takes care when calculating the area of regular
                           polygons.
                           Peace: the learner harmoniously discusses and works in groups using trigonometric ratios to generate the formula for the area of a
                           triangle given two sides and an included angle.

Science & Engineering      Geometric construction, derivation, measurement and independent calculation.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary   Environmental Education: the learner collects objects from the immediate environment to be used as learning materials for the area
Issues (PCIs)              of polygons.

Career Connections         Surveying, construction, landscaping and estimating.
 Focus for Lessons                        Choose and justify an area method from verified dimensions; distinguish geometric calculation from field measurement.

 Driving Question / Key Inquiry           How can we defend a garden covering estimate? KICD: How do we work out the area of polygons? How do we apply the concept
 Questions                                of the area of polygons in real-life situations?

 Anchoring Phenomenon                     A fictional school garden has triangular and irregular beds. A tempting side-times-side estimate overstates how much covering is
                                          needed.

 Storyline Thread                         Height misconception -> sine derivation -> Heron -> scale -> quadrilaterals -> diagonals -> regular polygons -> heptagon/octagon -
                                          > irregular regions -> coordinate plan -> application -> assessment.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Labelled model diagrams, scale drawings and regular-polygon designs; no measurements are claimed to be from a real school.
