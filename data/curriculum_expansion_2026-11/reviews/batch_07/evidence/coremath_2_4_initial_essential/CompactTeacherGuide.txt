CORE MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: TRIGONOMETRY 1
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
15 lessons × 40 minutes = 600 minutes
Driving question
What is trigonometry? How do we use trigonometry in real-life situations?
Source learning outcomes
determine the trigonometric ratios of acute angles from mathematical tables and calculators,
relate sines and cosines of complementary angles,
relate the sine, cosine and tangent of acute angles,
determine trigonometric ratios of special angles: 30°, 45°, 60° and 90° using triangles,
apply trigonometric ratios to angles of elevation and depression,
reflect on the use of trigonometry in real-life situations.
