CORE MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: ROTATION
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes
Driving question
How is rotation applied in real-life situations?
Source learning outcomes
determine the properties of rotation in different situations,rotate an object given the centre and angle of rotation on a plane surface and the Cartesian plane,determine the centre and angle of rotation given an object and its image,determine the order of rotational symmetry of plane figures,determine the axis and order of rotational symmetry in solids,deduce congruence from rotation,appreciate the application of rotation in real-life situations.
