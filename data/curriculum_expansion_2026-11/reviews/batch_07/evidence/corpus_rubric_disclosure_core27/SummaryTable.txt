Sub-Strand1.1: Real Numbers
Driving Question
How do we use real numbers in day-to-day activities?

Lesson 1
Can zero be even without being prime?
0 is even and neither prime nor composite;9 is odd composite.
Whole numbers can be classified by parity and divisors without making those classifications equivalent.
One counterexample disproves every odd whole number is prime.
Lesson 2
Does a decimal display classify the exact number?
A square root can be rational or irrational, depending on its exact input.
Exact definition, not the length of a device display, controls rational classification.
The exactπ is not the rational22/7.
Lesson 3
What does one divided by a number undo?
Nonzero signed numbers have reciprocals;0 does not.
Reciprocal means multiplicative inverse.
An operation and its inverse must match the intended undoing.
Lesson 4
Can mixed-number digits simply be swapped?
The sign belongs to the whole mixed number.
A reciprocal can be larger or smaller in magnitude than its nonzero input.
Converting first prevents a plausible digit-swapping error.
Lesson 5
How does a reciprocal table scale?
Multiplying an input by10 divides its reciprocal by10.
Table entries are numerical evidence with declared precision.
The supplied teaching table supports the table-reading component of sourced; calculator use is checked next.
Lesson 6
What evidence shows actual calculator use?
Devices can display approximations or error messages without changing real-number definitions.
A written result and a completed calculator action are distinct evidence.
No device provision means calculator performance remains unconfirmed; table/paper work still has its own evidence.
Lesson 7
Why can multiplying by an inverse count portions?
Both contextual quotients are exact whole counts under the stated assumptions.
Reciprocals transform division into a checkable multiplication.
A result must be interpreted using the physical quantity and assumptions.
Lesson 8
How can evidence make useful advice persuasive?
A numerical claim is more useful when its limits and evidence are visible.
Classification and reciprocals support everyday planning when interpreted correctly.
A completed written message does not by itself certify an unobserved presentation.
Lesson 9
Which shortcuts survive a counterexample?
The same words reciprocal and opposite refer to different operations.
Exact definitions, counterexamples and checks protect a numerical decision.
A finite display and an error message do not redefine the real numbers.
Lesson 10
Can an independent number audit support a decision?
Independent new inputs require the same definitions and checks.
A reliable result connects classification,inverse,precision and context.
Calculator performance is certified only by actual observation;paper correctness has its own evidence.
