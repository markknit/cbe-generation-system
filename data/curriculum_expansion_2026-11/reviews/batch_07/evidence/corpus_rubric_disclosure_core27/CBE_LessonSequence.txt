CORE MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: REAL NUMBERS
OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 1.1 • 10 lessons • teacher review copy

SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level
10
Subject
Core Mathematics
Strand
1.0 Numbers and Algebra
Sub-Strand
Sub-Strand1.1: Real Numbers
Total Duration
10 lessons × 40 minutes = 400 minutes
Sub-Strand Content
Classification of numbers; Reciprocal of numbers
Learning Outcomes
classify whole numbers as odd, even, prime and composite in different situations,
classify real numbers as rational and irrational in different situations,
determine the reciprocal of real numbers by division,
determine the reciprocal of real numbers by use of mathematical tables and calculators,
apply reciprocal of real numbers in mathematical computations,
promote the use of real numbers in day-to-day activities.
Core Competencies
Communication and Collaboration: the learner discusses with peers to identify and categorise whole numbers as odd, even, prime, composite, rational, and irrational.
Critical Thinking and Problem Solving: the learner uses the reciprocal of real numbers to work out mathematical tasks.
Digital Literacy: the learner works out the reciprocal of real numbers from calculators and other digital devices.
Core Values
Unity: the learner discusses in a group and categorises real numbers as rational and irrational numbers.
Responsibility: the learner takes care of calculators and mathematical tables when working out the reciprocal of real numbers.
Science & Engineering Practices
Classify exact quantities,model multiplicative inverses and verify a numerical decision.
Pertinent & Contemporary Issues (PCIs)
Life Skills (self-esteem): the learner participates in the discussion on the concepts of rational and irrational numbers.
Career Connections
Illustrative quantity planning,measurement and mathematical communication;no professional certification.
Focus for Lessons
Whole-numberclassification;rational/irrational realnumbers;reciprocalbydivision,mathematicaltables/calculators;applications and usefulcommunication.
Driving Question / Key Inquiry Questions
How do we use real numbers in day-to-day activities?
Anchoring Phenomenon
A fictional class supply label calls every odd count prime and tries to undo division with an additive opposite.
Storyline Thread
Classification→exactness→divisioninverse→representations→table→actualcalculator→application→advice→audit→independent evidence.
Supporting Phenomena
Exact versusdisplayednumbers,signedfraction inverses,reciprocal tables,actual device records and portion planning.

LESSON 1 (40 minutes): Can zero be even without being prime?
A. SPECIFIC LEARNING OUTCOMES
Category
Specific Learning Outcomes
Purpose
Classify whole numbers by parity and positive divisors.
Knowledge
Whole numbers0,1,2,… are all rational; prime and composite classifications require integers greater than1.
Skills
Identify factor pairs, justify parity and distinguish independent categories.
Attitudes
Respect different explanations, share approved resources responsibly and revise claims when a check disagrees.
Key Inquiry Question
Does odd imply prime, and where do0 and1 belong?
Purpose in Storyline
A classroom supply label wrongly calls all odd counts prime.
