                                 OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 1.1 • 10 lessons • teacher review copy


                                                                SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Core Mathematics

Strand                           1.0 Numbers and Algebra

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand1.1: Real Numbers

Total Duration                   10 lessons × 40 minutes = 400 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Classification of numbers; Reciprocal of numbers

Learning Outcomes                classify whole numbers as odd, even, prime and composite in different situations,
                                 classify real numbers as rational and irrational in different situations,
                                 determine the reciprocal of real numbers by division,
                                 determine the reciprocal of real numbers by use of mathematical tables and calculators,
                                 apply reciprocal of real numbers in mathematical computations,
                                 promote the use of real numbers in day-to-day activities.

Core Competencies                Communication and Collaboration: the learner discusses with peers to identify and categorise whole numbers as odd, even, prime,
                                 composite, rational, and irrational.
                                 Critical Thinking and Problem Solving: the learner uses the reciprocal of real numbers to work out mathematical tasks.
                                 Digital Literacy: the learner works out the reciprocal of real numbers from calculators and other digital devices.

Core Values                      Unity: the learner discusses in a group and categorises real numbers as rational and irrational numbers.
                                 Responsibility: the learner takes care of calculators and mathematical tables when working out the reciprocal of real numbers.

Science & Engineering            Classify exact quantities,model multiplicative inverses and verify a numerical decision.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Life Skills (self-esteem): the learner participates in the discussion on the concepts of rational and irrational numbers.
Issues (PCIs)

Career Connections               Illustrative quantity planning,measurement and mathematical communication;no professional certification.

Focus for Lessons                Whole-numberclassification;rational/irrational realnumbers;reciprocalbydivision,mathematicaltables/calculators;applications and
                                 usefulcommunication.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   How do we use real numbers in day-to-day activities?
Questions

Anchoring Phenomenon             A fictional class supply label calls every odd count prime and tries to undo division with an additive opposite.
 Storyline Thread                         Classification→exactness→divisioninverse→representations→table→actualcalculator→application→advice→audit→independent
                                          evidence.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Exact versusdisplayednumbers,signedfraction inverses,reciprocal tables,actual device records and portion planning.
