INSTRUCTIONS FOR STUDENTS
Lesson9,40 minutes:4 procedure,14 sections1–2,14 section3,4 section4 and4 checking/collection. Work independently with QE9 only; no teaching cards,peer help or worked examples. Show model definitions,factorisation and substitution checks. Calculator may check completed arithmetic. All roots requested are real; contextual restrictions must be stated separately.

1. Form and interpret an expression
For the QE9 token array define n and state its restrictions. Form and expand the total-token expression,then evaluate it for n=3. Explain why n+(n+2) does not represent the total.

Factor3t²+10t+3 and use it to solve3t²+10t+3=0. Also solve y²=4y by factorisation. State every real root,substitute each into its original equation and explain how division by y could lose one root.

3. Decide which design is feasible
For the QE9 sign define x and its restriction. Form the area equation,expand and rearrange to zero. Factor,find every algebraic root and test each in the original product equation. State feasible width,length,area and perimeter with units,and explain why any algebraic root is excluded by this context.

4. Use and limits
Explain one concrete way a quadratic model helped the sign decision. Identify one limitation or extra piece of real design information that this simplified model does not supply. Give a mathematical check that supports your decision.
