                                 OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 2.3 • 12 lessons • teacher review copy


                                                                SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Core Mathematics

Strand                           2.0 Measurements and Geometry

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand2.3: Rotation

Total Duration                   12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Properties of rotation; Rotation on different planes; Rotational symmetry; Rotation and Congruence

Learning Outcomes                determine the properties of rotation in different situations,
                                 rotate an object given the centre and angle of rotation on a plane surface and the Cartesian plane,
                                 determine the centre and angle of rotation given an object and its image,
                                 determine the order of rotational symmetry of plane figures,
                                 determine the axis and order of rotational symmetry in solids,
                                 deduce congruence from rotation,
                                 appreciate the application of rotation in real-life situations.

Core Competencies                Critical thinking and Problem Solving: the learner draws objects and their images and through construction establishes the centres
                                 and angles of rotation.
                                 Communication and Collaboration: the learner discusses using an object and its image on a plane surface to generate the
                                 properties of rotation.

Core Values                      Responsibility: the learner takes care of learning resources collected from the immediate environment to establish their axis and
                                 order of rotational symmetry.
                                 Respect: learners appreciate each other’s opinion as they discuss and draw the image under a rotation given the object, centre
                                 and angle of rotation.

Science & Engineering            Construct directed turns and test invariants,centres and symmetry.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Environmental education: as the learner responsibly collects regular solids from the immediate environment to determine the axis
Issues (PCIs)                    and order of rotational symmetry.

Career Connections               Illustrative mathematical pattern/design modelling;no real machinery or professional qualification.

Focus for Lessons                Rotation properties,plane/Cartesian constructions,centre/angle recovery,plane/solid rotational symmetry and direct congruence.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   How is rotation applied in real-life situations?
 Questions

 Anchoring Phenomenon                     A fictional pattern chart confuses a rotation with reflection and counts every cube axis as order4.

 Storyline Thread                         Properties→quarter turns→half/full turns→nonorigin centre→recover centre→identity limit→plane order→cube axes→other
                                          solids→congruence→use/digital evidence→independent final.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Fixed-pointer turns,nonorigin images,independent bisectors,identity limits,finite/continuous symmetry and labelled congruence.
