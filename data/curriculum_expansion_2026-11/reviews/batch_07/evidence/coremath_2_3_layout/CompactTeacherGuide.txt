CORE MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: ROTATION
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy

Duration
12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes

Driving question
How is rotation applied in real-life situations?

Source learning outcomes
determine the properties of rotation in different situations,
rotate an object given the centre and angle of rotation on a plane surface and the Cartesian plane,
determine the centre and angle of rotation given an object and its image,
determine the order of rotational symmetry of plane figures,
determine the axis and order of rotational symmetry in solids,
deduce congruence from rotation,
appreciate the application of rotation in real-life situations.

Preparation and evidence
Use the companion Student Materials and Materials Teacher Key. Card identifiers are local references,not web links. Practical demonstrations require the teacher’s local risk
assessment. Standard CBE documents and structured JSON are supplied with the accepted unit after content review. Consult the unit review record for independent review and
classroom confirmation status.
