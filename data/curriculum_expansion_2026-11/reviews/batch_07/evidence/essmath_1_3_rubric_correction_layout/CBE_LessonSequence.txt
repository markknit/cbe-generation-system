                                                                SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Essential Mathematics

Strand                           1.0 Numbers and Algebra

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand 1.3: Quadratic Equations (I)

Total Duration                   9 lessons × 40 minutes = 360 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Formation of quadratic expressions; Factorisation and solve quadratic equations; Applications of quadratic equation

Learning Outcomes                form algebraic expressions from different situations,
                                 form quadratic expressions and equations from real life situations,
                                 factorise quadratic expressions in different situations,
                                 solve quadratic equations by factorisation,
                                 recognise use of quadratic expressions in real life situations.

Core Competencies                Communication and collaboration: the learner collaborates with peers as they discuss with peers and work out factors of quadratic
                                 expressions.
                                 Critical thinking and Problem solving: the learner develops open-mindedness, discusses with peers and works out factors of
                                 quadratic expressions.

Core Values                      Respect: the learner displays humility by listening to others opinions while discussing areas where quadratic equations are used in
                                 real life situations.

Science & Engineering            Define variables and restrictions, build a model, factor, solve and test the interpretation.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Online safety: the learner observes online safety procedures and guidelines while searching on the application of quadratic
Issues (PCIs)                    equations using digital resources.

Career Connections               Illustrative design, display planning and area estimation; no employment or professional competence claim.

Focus for Lessons                Form quadratic expressions/equations and solve factorable examples, retaining every algebraic root before applying contextual
                                 restrictions.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   Which algebraic answers describe a possible design? KICD: How are quadratic equations applied in real life?
Questions

Anchoring Phenomenon             Two sign designs satisfy the same area equation on paper, but one answer assigns negative side lengths.
 Storyline Thread                         Variables → model equations → factors → signed pairs → non-monic grouping → zero product → feasible roots → audit →
                                          independent model.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Labelled rectangles, row arrays, inverse expansion checks and lost-zero-root audits.

                                                 LESSON 3 (40 minutes): Can multiplication be undone?
                                                             A. SPECIFIC LEARNING OUTCOMES
Category                       Specific Learning Outcomes

Purpose                        Factor common-factor and difference-of-squares expressions and verify identities.

Knowledge                      A factorisation must expand to the entire original expression; a sum of squares is not a difference of squares.

Skills                         Extract common factors, recognise a square difference and expand to verify.

Attitudes                      Use a counterexample to challenge a claim respectfully.

Key Inquiry Question           How can we know a proposed factorisation is correct for every x?

Purpose in Storyline           An unverified formula sheet introduces an extra middle term.

Safety Notes                   Use paper and pencil; printed symbols have no physical measurement meaning unless stated.

Category                        Specific Learning Outcomes

Purpose                         Factor monic quadratic trinomials with positive and negative terms.

Skills                          List signed factor pairs, choose both conditions and verify.

Attitudes                       Value a checked method over a quick unverified guess.

Key Inquiry Question            Why is the right product alone insufficient?

Purpose in Storyline            Two plausible pairs give the same constant but different middle terms.

Safety Notes                    Fictional symbolic problems use paper only.

Category                       Specific Learning Outcomes

Purpose                        Factor simple non-monic quadratics by middle-term splitting and grouping.

Knowledge                      Split coefficients sum to b and multiply to ac; grouping exposes a shared binomial.

Skills                         Split, group, extract and expand.

Attitudes                      Explain a checked sequence so a partner can inspect it.

Key Inquiry Question           Why must the split pair multiply to6 rather than3?

Purpose in Storyline           The design audit encounters a leading coefficient of2.

Safety Notes                   Use labelled working on paper; no unrelated digital search is needed.
