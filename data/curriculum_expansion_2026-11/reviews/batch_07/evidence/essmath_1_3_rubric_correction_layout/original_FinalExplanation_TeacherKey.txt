1. Form and interpret an expression
For the QE9 token array define
n and state its restrictions.
Form and expand the totaltoken expression,then evaluate
it for n=3. Explain why n+(n+2)
does not represent the total.

n is a positive integer number of rows;each contains n+2 tokens. Total n(n+2)=n²+2n,which at3 gives15 tokens. Adding rows to
tokens per row does not count each token;the count requires the product.
