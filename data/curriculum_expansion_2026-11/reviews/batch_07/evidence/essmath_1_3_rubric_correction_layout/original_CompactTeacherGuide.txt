ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: QUADRATIC EQUATIONS I
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy

Duration
9 lessons × 40 minutes = 360 minutes

Driving question
Which algebraic answers describe a possible design? KICD: How are quadratic equations applied in real life?

Source learning outcomes
form algebraic expressions from different situations,
form quadratic expressions and equations from real life situations,
factorise quadratic expressions in different situations,
solve quadratic equations by factorisation,
recognise use of quadratic expressions in real life situations.

Preparation and evidence
Use the companion Student Materials and Materials Teacher Key. Card identifiers are local references,not web links. Practical demonstrations require the teacher’s local risk
assessment. Standard CBE documents and structured JSON are supplied with the accepted unit after content review. Consult the unit review record for independent review and
classroom confirmation status.
