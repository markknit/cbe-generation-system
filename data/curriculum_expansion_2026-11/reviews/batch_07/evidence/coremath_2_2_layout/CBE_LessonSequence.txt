                        CORE MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: REFLECTION AND CONGRUENCE
                                 OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 2.2 • 13 lessons • teacher review copy


                                                               SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Core Mathematics

Strand                           2.0 Measurements and Geometry

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand2.2: Reflection and Congruence

Total Duration                   13 lessons × 40 minutes = 520 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Symmetry; Reflection; Equations of Mirror Lines; Congruence

Learning Outcomes                identify lines of symmetry in plane figures,
                                 determine the properties of reflection in different situations
                                 draw an image given an object and a mirror line on plane surface and Cartesian plane,
                                 determine the equation of the mirror line given an object and its image,
                                 carry out congruence tests for triangles,
                                 promote use of reflection and congruence in real-life situations.

Core Competencies                Learning to Learn: the learner applies the properties of reflection to generate the image of an object on a given mirror line.
                                 Creativity and Imagination: the learner locates the image of an object placed at a point in front of a mirror.

Core Values                      Responsibility: the learner takes care of the mirror used in reflecting corresponding images.
                                 Respect: the learner recognises the input of every member of the team as they work in groups to make paper cutouts of different
                                 identical shapes to identify direct or opposite congruent shapes.

Science & Engineering            Construct mirror images and test geometric equivalence using explicit evidence.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Road safety: the learner discusses with peers the applications of reflections and congruence on images formed by driving mirrors
Issues (PCIs)                    in enhancing road safety.
                                 Environment and Technology: the learner selectively and cautiously collects and observes different objects from the immediate
                                 environment and identifies the lines and planes of symmetry.

Career Connections               Illustrative mathematical design,pattern checking and model communication;no professional or driving qualification.

Focus for Lessons                Line/plane symmetry,plane reflection properties,Cartesian images,mirror equations,direct/opposite congruence,SSS/SAS/ASA-
                                 AAS/RHS and responsible uses.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   How do we use reflection in day-to-day life? Where do we use congruence in real life?
Questions
 Anchoring Phenomenon                     A fictional stencil label confuses reflection with negative enlargement and calls equal angles alone a guarantee of equal size.

 Storyline Thread                         Symmetry→plane mirror→fold/reflection properties→axis lines→diagonals→shifted equation→general slope→direct/opposite
                                          copies→SSS/SAS→ASA/AAS→RHS→use/advice→independent evidence.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Ideal symmetry lines,actual plane-mirror predictions/observations,shifted mirror equations,labelled congruent copies and fully
                                          specified triangle cases.

                         LESSON 5 (40 minutes): Why is a diagonal reflection different from a negative enlargement?
                                                             A. SPECIFIC LEARNING OUTCOMES
Category                       Specific Learning Outcomes
