CORE MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: REFLECTION AND CONGRUENCE
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy

Duration
13 lessons × 40 minutes = 520 minutes

Driving question
How do we use reflection in day-to-day life? Where do we use congruence in real life?

Source learning outcomes
identify lines of symmetry in plane figures,
determine the properties of reflection in different situations
draw an image given an object and a mirror line on plane surface and Cartesian plane,
determine the equation of the mirror line given an object and its image,
carry out congruence tests for triangles,
promote use of reflection and congruence in real-life situations.

assessment. Standard CBE documents and structured JSON are supplied with the accepted unit after content review. Consult the unit review record for independent review and
classroom confirmation status.
