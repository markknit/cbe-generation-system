                                                                SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Core Mathematics

Strand                           1.0 Numbers and Algebra

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand1.3: Quadratic Expressions and Equations

Total Duration                   14 lessons × 40 minutes = 560 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Quadratic expressions; Quadratic identities; Factorisation of quadratic expressions; Formation of quadratic equations; Solution of
                                 quadratic equations by factorisation; Application of quadratic equations to real-life situations

Learning Outcomes                form quadratic expressions from different situations,
                                 derive the quadratic identities from the concept of area,
                                 apply quadratic identities in numerical cases,
                                 factorise quadratic expressions from different situations,
                                 form quadratic equations in different situations,
                                 solve quadratic equations by factorisation,
                                 apply quadratic equations to real life situations,
                                 explore the use of quadratic equations in real life situations.

Core Competencies                Critical thinking and problem solving: the learner interprets statements to form and solve quadratic equations to get solutions to
                                 real-life cases.
                                 Communication and collaboration: the learner discusses with peers and comes up with quadratic identities.

Core Values                      Unity: the learner works with others to write quadratic identities on a chart.
                                 Respect: the learner works with peers to form and solve quadratic equations to get solutions to real-life cases.

Science & Engineering            Model area,derive identities and test algebraic and contextual solutions.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Socio-economic and environmental issues (social cohesion): the learner works with peers to form and solve quadratic equations to
Issues (PCIs)                    get solutions to real-life cases.

Career Connections               Illustrative mathematical modelling and paper design planning;no professional qualification or real construction claim.

Focus for Lessons                Quadratic expression formation,area identities,numerical identities,factorisation,equation formation,solutions by factorisation and
                                 real-life paper models.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   How do we use equations in daily life?
 Questions

 Anchoring Phenomenon                     A fictional design chart drops a cross term,loses a zero root and accepts a negative width because it solves an equation.

 Storyline Thread                         Expression→area sum→area differences→numerical use→common factor→monic→nonmonic→equation formation→zero-
                                          product→complete roots→context→exploration→audit→independent evidence.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Labelled area partitions,coefficient checks,zero products,repeated roots and changed-area model comparisons.
