                                                               SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          General Science

Strand                           2.0 Matter and Chemical Reactions

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand2.5: Rates of Reactions

Total Duration                   13 lessons × 40 minutes = 520 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Rates of reactions; Factors affecting the rates of reactions

Learning Outcomes                describe rate of chemical reactions,
                                 perform experiments involving the rate of chemical reactions,
                                 explain factors that influence the rate of reactions in biological, chemical and physical processes,
                                 appreciate the importance of manipulating factors affecting the rate of chemical and biological processes in day-to-day life.

Core Competencies                Communication and collaboration: as the learner discusses with peers the factors that affect the rate of chemical reactions.
                                 Digital literacy: as the learner uses digital devices and print media to research on the importance of optimum conditions in
                                 biological, chemical and physical processes.

Core Values                      Responsibility: as the learner carefully uses digital devices to search for information on the importance of optimum conditions in
                                 industrial processes.
                                 Respect: as the learner respects others’ opinion while discussing with peers in a group the importance of efficient industrial
                                 processes.

Science & Engineering            Plan controlled comparisons,record actual actions,analyse rates/graphs and distinguish mechanisms from unsupported
Practices                        generalisations.

Pertinent & Contemporary         Safety and security: as the learner observes safety measures when carrying out activities to investigate rates of reactions.
Issues (PCIs)

Career Connections               Illustrative process engineering,food production and laboratory quality roles.

Focus for Lessons                Rates of reactions; Factors affecting the rates of reactions

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   How do various factors affect the rate of reactions?
Questions

Anchoring Phenomenon             A fictional process poster claims every increase in heat,pressure,light or concentration always improves all reactions and that
                                 supplied data prove an experiment was performed.
 Storyline Thread                         Meaning→graph→actual-approved
                                          evidence→repeats→temperature→concentration→surface→pressure→catalyst→light→optimum→audit→independent decision.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Time-volume records,conditional supervised open-cup endpoint comparison,enzyme/gas/catalyst examples and constrained
                                          process choices.

                                                              A. SPECIFIC LEARNING OUTCOMES
Category                        Specific Learning Outcomes

Purpose                         Define rate and distinguish a measured proxy from final amount.

Attitudes                       Respect peer evidence and record results honestly.
