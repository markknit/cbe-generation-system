Sub-Strand
Sub-Strand2.5: Rates of Reactions
Driving Question
How do various factors affect the rate of reactions?

INSTRUCTIONS
FOR TEACHERS: This is the teacher reference version — each row is pre-filled with expected student responses. Use it to assess student Summary Tables, identify gaps, and prepare discussion questions. The student version has blank cells for students to complete after each lesson.

Lesson # and Title
What did I observe?
What did I learn?
How does this explain the phenomenon?
Lesson 1
What does reaction rate measure?
Define rate and distinguish a measured proxy from final amount.
Rate tracks change per time;chemical reaction involves reactant/product changes,while physical processes can also have rates.
Can equal final amounts form at different rates? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 2
How does a graph describe change through time?
Plot supplied product records and calculate interval averages.
Graph slope gives interval averages from tabulated changes;plateau does not identify a reaction cause by itself.
Does every part of a curve have the same rate? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 3
What would count as an actual approved experiment?
Carry out and record an approved apparent-rate comparison or retain not-performed status.
A reproducible operational endpoint and actual observed action are needed for experimental evidence.
Which part of a plan becomes evidence only when performed? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 4
How do repeats change a comparison?
Compute means,ranges and apparent endpoint indices with limitations.
Repeated endpoint times support a bounded comparison;1/time is not a measured volume rate or intrinsic constant.
What can six supplied times tell us? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 5
Does higher temperature always help?
Explain temperature effects with a biological counterexample and daily-use limit.
Kinetic energy and activation barriers matter;enzyme activity may decline at high temperature.
Does the highest tested condition establish an exact optimum? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 6
When does concentration affect rate?
Explain a tested concentration relation and design a controlled comparison.
Observed concentration dependence is reaction/range specific and can be confounded by changed conditions.
Does doubling concentration always double every reaction rate? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 7
How can the same amount expose more surface?
Use a geometry model to explain interface effects and physical-rate distinctions.
Surface area can change at fixed volume/mass and affect heterogeneous encounters.
Is a larger piece always more exposed overall? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 8
Which pressure change is relevant?
Explain reactant number density without unsafe pressure experiments.
Gas concentration,not total pressure alone,underlies the simple encounter argument.
Does adding inert gas always speed a reaction? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 9
How do catalysts change a pathway?
Calculate supplied catalytic rates and explain pathway/equilibrium distinctions.
Catalysts can lower activation barriers and be regenerated;they do not alter equilibrium at fixed conditions.
Does a faster pathway imply more final equilibrium product? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 10
When can light influence a process?
Interpret a photosensitive record and distinguish heat confounding and phase change.
Suitable light matters for some processes;physical evaporation and chemical product formation differ.
How can a brighter lamp change two factors? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 11
What makes a condition appropriate?
Use explicit constraints to discuss optimum conditions and actual research evidence.
Fastest need not meet a budget,safety,quality or environmental objective.
What evidence is missing from an output-only decision? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 12
Can six factor claims survive an audit?
Correct all six overclaims with condition-specific evidence.
Different factors require relevant systems,controls and bounded mechanisms.
Which conditions make each factor explanation valid? State a condition and an evidence limit.
Lesson 13
Can rate evidence support an independent decision?
Independently calculate and qualify rate evidence.
Rate conclusions require meaningful quantities,conditions and provenance.
Defend a constrained decision while distinguishing actual from supplied evidence.
