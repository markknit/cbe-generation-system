GENERAL SCIENCE GRADE 10: RATES OF REACTIONS
OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 2.5 • 13 lessons • teacher review copy

SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level
10
Subject
General Science
Strand
2.0 Matter and Chemical Reactions
Sub-Strand
Sub-Strand2.5: Rates of Reactions
Total Duration
13 lessons × 40 minutes = 520 minutes
Sub-Strand Content
Rates of reactions; Factors affecting the rates of reactions
Learning Outcomes
describe rate of chemical reactions,
perform experiments involving the rate of chemical reactions,
explain factors that influence the rate of reactions in biological, chemical and physical processes,
appreciate the importance of manipulating factors affecting the rate of chemical and biological processes in day-to-day life.
Core Competencies
Communication and collaboration: as the learner discusses with peers the factors that affect the rate of chemical reactions.
Digital literacy: as the learner uses digital devices and print media to research on the importance of optimum conditions in biological, chemical and physical processes.
Core Values
Responsibility: as the learner carefully uses digital devices to search for information on the importance of optimum conditions in industrial processes.
Respect: as the learner respects others’ opinion while discussing with peers in a group the importance of efficient industrial processes.
Science & Engineering Practices
Plan controlled comparisons,record actual actions,analyse rates/graphs and distinguish mechanisms from unsupported generalisations.
Pertinent & Contemporary Issues (PCIs)
Safety and security: as the learner observes safety measures when carrying out activities to investigate rates of reactions.
Career Connections
Illustrative process engineering,food production and laboratory quality roles.
Focus for Lessons
Rates of reactions; Factors affecting the rates of reactions
Driving Question / Key Inquiry Questions
How do various factors affect the rate of reactions?
Anchoring Phenomenon
A fictional process poster claims every increase in heat,pressure,light or concentration always improves all reactions and that supplied data prove an experiment was performed.
Storyline Thread
Meaning→graph→actual-approved evidence→repeats→temperature→concentration→surface→pressure→catalyst→light→optimum→audit→independent decision.
Supporting Phenomena
Time-volume records,conditional supervised open-cup endpoint comparison,enzyme/gas/catalyst examples and constrained process choices.

LESSON 1 (40 minutes): What does reaction rate measure?
A. SPECIFIC LEARNING OUTCOMES
Category
Specific Learning Outcomes
Purpose
Define rate and distinguish a measured proxy from final amount.
Knowledge
Rate tracks change per time;chemical reaction involves reactant/product changes,while physical processes can also have rates.
Skills
Measure or interpret change,calculate rates,control comparisons and qualify conclusions.
Attitudes
Respect peer evidence and record results honestly.
Key Inquiry Question
Can equal final amounts form at different rates?
Purpose in Storyline
Two supplied records produce the same gas amount in different times.
Safety Notes
RR3 actual work requires prior teacher product/risk/equipment approval and supervision;otherwise supplied data only. No sealed gas systems,pressure tests,medicinal tablets,crushing,dust,flames or learner heating.

B. LESSON OVERVIEW
