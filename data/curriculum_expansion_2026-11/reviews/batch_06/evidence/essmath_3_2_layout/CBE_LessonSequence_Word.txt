ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: PROBABILITY I
OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 3.2 • 10 lessons • teacher review copy

SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level
10
Subject
Essential Mathematics
Strand
3.0 Statistics and Probability
Sub-Strand
Probability I (internal sequence3.2; source table4.2)
Total Duration
10 lessons × 40 minutes = 400 minutes
Sub-Strand Content
Equally and likely events; Mutually exclusive events; Independent events (maximum of two experiments and two outcomes)
Learning Outcomes
perform experiments involving equally likely outcomes in different situations,
compute probability of equally likely outcomes,
determine the range of probability of an event,
identify mutually exclusive events in real life situations,
perform experiments of single chance involving mutually exclusive events,
calculate the probability of an event involving a single chance,
perform experiments involving independent events,
determine the probability of independent events in different situations,
appreciate the role of probability in real-life situations.
Core Competencies
Communication and collaboration: the learner evaluating successes and failures from experiments involving mutually exclusive events to work out the probability possible outcomes.
Critical thinking and problem solving: the learner develops interpretation and inference when discussing the dangers of gambling using the knowledge of probability.
Core Values
Responsibility: The learner makes correct decisions on life issues that involves gambling using the knowledge of probability.
Unity: The learner collaborates with others when carry out experiments involving probability.
Science & Engineering Practices
Perform,record,definebinaryevents,calculateandboundmodels.
Pertinent & Contemporary Issues (PCIs)
Life Skills (Decision Making): The learner makes informed decisions on life issues involving probability such dangers of gambling.
Career Connections
Illustrativemodel/data-awarenesspractices;nostakesorfinancialqualification.
Focus for Lessons
Binaryequallylikelymodels,range,singlechanceexclusivity,actualtwocomponentindependenttrialsandqualifieduse.
Driving Question / Key Inquiry Questions
Whichbinaryweightsandactualrecordsjustifyaprobabilityclaim? KICD: How do we apply probability in day to day life?
Anchoring Phenomenon
Afictionalreport assumes two labels prove fairness and calls four joint pairs four experiments.
Storyline Thread
Binaryweights → actualA → actualB → exclusivity → independencemodel → actualpair → jointcases → usefulrole → audit → independent evidence.
Supporting Phenomena
ActualAcoin/Bblinddrawandpairedrecords,separateprovidedfrequencies,binaryeventtablesandgambling-awarenesslimits.
