ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: PROBABILITY I
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy

Duration
10 lessons × 40 minutes = 400 minutes

Driving question
Whichbinaryweightsandactualrecordsjustifyaprobabilityclaim? KICD: How do we apply probability in day to day life?

Source learning outcomes
perform experiments involving equally likely outcomes in different situations,
compute probability of equally likely outcomes,
determine the range of probability of an event,
identify mutually exclusive events in real life situations,
perform experiments of single chance involving mutually exclusive events,
calculate the probability of an event involving a single chance,
perform experiments involving independent events,
determine the probability of independent events in different situations,
appreciate the role of probability in real-life situations.

Preparation and evidence
Use the companion Student Materials and Materials Teacher Key. Card identifiers are local references,not web links. Practical demonstrations require the teacher’s local risk
assessment. Standard CBE documents and structured JSON are supplied with the accepted unit after content review. Consult the unit review record for independent review and
classroom confirmation status.
