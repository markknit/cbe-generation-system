GENERAL SCIENCE GRADE 10: CHEMICAL FAMILIES
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes
Driving question
How are elements suited to their use in day-to-day life?
Source learning outcomes
identify chemical families in the periodic table,
classify elements of the periodic table into their chemical families,
investigate the physical and chemical properties of selected elements of the periodic table,
outline the uses of elements of the periodic table,
appreciate the uses of the elements of the periodic table in day-to-day life.
Preparation and evidence
Use the companion Student Materials and Materials Teacher Key. Card identifiers are local references,not web links. Practical demonstrations require the teacher’s local risk assessment.
