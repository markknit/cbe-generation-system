GENERAL SCIENCE GRADE 10: CHEMICAL BONDING
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
14 lessons × 40 minutes = 560 minutes
Driving question
How do bond types relate to physical properties of compounds?
Source learning outcomes
illustrate chemical bonding in common compounds,identify bond types in common compounds,distinguish structures of selected elements and compounds,describe the relationship between the bond types and the physical properties of elements and compounds,select appropriate materials for use based on their bond type and structure,reflect on the appropriateness of substances used in day-to-day life.
