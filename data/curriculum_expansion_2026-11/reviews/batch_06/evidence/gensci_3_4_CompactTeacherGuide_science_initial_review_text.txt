GENERAL SCIENCE GRADE 10: MAGNETISM AND ELECTROMAGNETIC INDUCTION
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
15 lessons × 40 minutes = 600 minutes
Driving question
How is the study of electromagnetic induction important in our daily life?
Source learning outcomes
describe the methods of magnetisation and demagnetisation in soft iron,describe the magnetic field patterns around a magnet,describe induced electromotive force in electromagnetism,perform an experiment on electromagnetic induction,explain factors affecting the magnitude of induced electromotive force,appreciate the applications of electromagnetic induction in day-to-day life.
