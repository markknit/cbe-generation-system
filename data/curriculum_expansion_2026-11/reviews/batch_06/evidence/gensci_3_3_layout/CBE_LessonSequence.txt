                                                                SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          General Science

Strand                           3.0 Natural Physical Science

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand3.3: Waves

Total Duration                   13 lessons × 40 minutes = 520 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Terms used in waves; The wave equation; Properties of waves; Application of properties of waves in real life.

Learning Outcomes                explain the terms used in waves,
                                 interpret the wave equation as used in science,
                                 calculate the wave characteristics using the wave equation,
                                 demonstrate the effects of properties of waves in real life,
                                 appreciate the applications of waves in real life.

Core Competencies                Learning to learn: as the learner searches for information on applications of reflection, refraction and diffraction of waves.
                                 Critical thinking and problem solving: as the learner searches for information on mitigation measures of the effects of waves on the
                                 environment (communities, ecosystems, infrastructure and operations).

Core Values                      Unity: as the learners play different roles when carrying out experiments to investigate reflection of sound waves using the echo
                                 method.

Science & Engineering            Represent spatial/time patterns,calculate relationships,compare mechanisms and evaluate evidence for applications.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Safety and security: as the learner appreciates some of the applications of waves related to safety in transport.
Issues (PCIs)                    Environmental issues: as the learner searches for information on the effect of waves on the environment (communities, marine
                                 ecosystems, infrastructure and operations).

Career Connections               Illustrative acoustics,communications,environmental assessment and optical design roles.

Focus for Lessons                Terms used in waves; The wave equation; Properties of waves; Application of properties of waves in real life.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   How are waves applicable in our day-to-day life? How do waves affect our environment?
Questions

Anchoring Phenomenon             A fictional poster confuses wavelength with period,says every night improves sound transmission,and treats hearing around a
                                 corner as proof of one cause or a clear road.
 Storyline Thread                         Terms→equation→units→boundary→reflection→echo→refraction→diffraction→corner→environment→mitigation→roads→indepe
                                          ndent evidence.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Spatial/time records,boundary speeds,quiet echo/corner options,conditional atmospheric gradients and environment/road cards.
