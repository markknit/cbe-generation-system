GENERAL SCIENCE GRADE 10: LINEAR MOTION
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
13 lessons × 40 minutes = 520 minutes
Driving question
How is free fall motion important in real life? How can the dangers of free fall in our day to day life be mitigated?
Source learning outcomes
explain terms used in linear motion,calculate variables of motion using equations of linear motion with constant acceleration,investigate the effect of gravity on bodies under free fall,appreciate the significance of linear motion and free fall in real life.
