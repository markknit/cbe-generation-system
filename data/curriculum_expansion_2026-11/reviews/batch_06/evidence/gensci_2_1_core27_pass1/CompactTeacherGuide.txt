GENERAL SCIENCE GRADE 10: THE PERIODIC TABLE
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
15 lessons × 40 minutes = 600 minutes
Driving question
How can atomic structure explain periodic patterns and help us check chemical formulae and equations?
Source learning outcomes
examine electron arrangement of an atom of an element,classify elements into groups and periods of the periodic table,explain the stability and electron affinity of atoms of elements,describe ion formation for the first 20 elements of the periodic table,formulate chemical formula of common compounds,write balanced equations of chemical reactions,appreciate the importance of the structure of the atom in the development of the periodic table.
