GENERAL SCIENCE GRADE 10: MICROORGANISMS
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes
Driving question
What evidence helps explain how microorganisms affect people and how infection risks can be reduced? KICD: How are micro-organisms important in day-to-day life?
Source learning outcomes
identify microorganisms that affect human beings,explain modes of transmission of microorganisms in human beings,identify types of infections caused by microorganisms in human beings,describe methods of control and prevention of infections caused by microorganisms,explain the economic importance of microorganisms,appreciate the effect of microorganisms in day-to-day life.
Preparation and evidence
Use the companion Student Materials and Materials Teacher Key. Card identifiers are local references,not web links. Practical demonstrations require the teacher’s local risk assessment. Standard CBE documents and structured JSON are supplied with the accepted unit after content review. Consult the unit review record for independent review and classroom confirmation status.
