# Primary-source checks for GS1.7 pass1

Checked through public web read tools during this independent review. Only narrow statements used in current cards were assessed; no diagnosis/treatment or actual preservation/culture procedure was validated.

- WHO Cholera: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/cholera — bacterium Vibrio cholerae, contaminated food/water ingestion and safe water/sanitation/hygiene supported.
- WHO Measles: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/measles — viral/airborne transmission and disease-specific vaccination supported.
- CDC Ringworm Basics: https://www.cdc.gov/ringworm/about/index.html — fungi, not worms; skin contact, objects and surfaces supported.
- WHO Malaria: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/malaria — parasites and infected female Anopheles mosquitoes supported; extension is correctly distinguished from bacteria/fungi/viruses.
- CDC Antibiotic Do's and Don'ts: https://www.cdc.gov/antibiotic-use/about/ — antibiotics do not work on viruses supported; current unit offers no personal treatment instruction.
- CDC Preventing Food Poisoning: https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/prevention/index.html — cut melon listed among riskier foods and chilling included among prevention steps. These support MI12's supplied summary; no particular food batch is thereby certified safe.
- FAO Fermented Fruits and Vegetables chapter5: https://www.fao.org/4/x0560e/x0560e10.htm — selected lactic carbohydrate-to-acid fermentation/product examples supported. This older page contains outdated/unreliable introductory cell terminology and overgeneralisations; do not use the entire chapter as a current microbiology definition handout. Current cards explicitly restrict their use to selected process information and do not reproduce those definitions.
- ASM biosafety URL supplied by author: https://asm.org/guideline/asm-guidelines-for-biosafety-in-teaching-laborator — public web read timed out. Content of that page not verified here. This is not an approval of actual cultures; MI9 expressly authorises paper review only, with any actual source project requiring a separate competent risk assessment/protocol and approval.
