## Independent scientific/task findings

- GEM1–3: existing domains, cancellation, field-driven alignment/growth and qualified soft-iron retention are sound. Electrical DC, induced magnetisation, consistent stroking and field-assisted mechanical descriptions differ appropriately. Diminishing alternating fields, relevant disturbance and suitable heat are qualified descriptions, not live procedures. Hammering alone does not determine polarity or always demagnetise; no exact-zero remanence or universal temperature claim is made. Primary cross-check: [OpenStax ferromagnets](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/22-2-ferromagnets-and-electromagnets) and [IOP magnetisation model](https://spark.iop.org/putting-model-work).
- GEM4: closed noncrossing lines, external N→S/internal S→N, tangent compass north end and qualitative density are valid school models. Optional actual mapping requires marked recorded positions; a drawing alone does not establish mapping.
- GEM5: unchanged steady configuration supplies no induction; changing linked field/relative configuration can. Appropriate moving open rod can have induced voltage while a conducting closed path is additionally needed for current. E.m.f. is energy per charge, not a force in newtons. The tasks preserve mechanical/electrical energy input. [OpenStax induction](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/23-1-induced-emf-and-magnetic-flux) supports these distinctions.
- GEM6–9: set right=+x,up=+y,out-of-page=+z. Field +x and wire along z make the active component proportional to the z-component of v×B. Independent vector checks give stationary/parallel0;up/down equal opposite;equal-speed angled with a smaller nonzero perpendicular component has smaller magnitude. Reversing field flips the sign;reversing both field and motion restores it. Meter-right/left remains a fixed-wiring convention. Active length and linked-turn factors retain their respective orientation/coil conditions; greater arbitrary loose-wire length is insufficient. Speed/field comparisons require controls and qualitative observations alone do not establish exact multipliers. [OpenStax motional e.m.f.](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/23-3-motional-emf) cross-checks the geometry.
- GEM7: actual setup drawing, all4 movements, baseline/reversal/repeats, roles/date/directions and actual or undetectable readings are required. Return-lead motion is a valid confounder. Only observed approved GEM7 can support outcome d. GEM8 supplied records/final reasoning cannot certify performance or meter sensitivity.
- GEM10/11/14: generator mechanical input and transformer changing electrical input are distinguished from a stationary magnet supplying free energy. Perfectly steady primary current does not sustain secondary induction after transients; switching remains possible. Heating is described only as a designed-device application. Each fictional claim is correctly rejected with a condition/limit. [OpenStax transformers](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/23-7-transformers) supports the changing-field distinction.
- GEM12/13: supply→switch→normally closed contact→coil→return plus mechanical armature/contact feedback produces the intended repeated attraction/open/release/reclose cycle. Soft iron's low relative retention assists release; a spring supplies return. Separate circuit and mechanical drawings are required. A disconnected coil design or off-the-shelf buzzer does not establish the source make-break project. Ordinary battery-bell action is primarily current-produced magnetism, while switching self-induction is acknowledged. [IOP model electric bell](https://spark.iop.org/model-electric-bell) independently supports this feedback principle; its particular1V/steel-blade apparatus is not adopted or claimed as approval for this draft's components.
