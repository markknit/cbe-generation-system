GENERAL SCIENCE GRADE 10: NUTRITION IN ANIMALS
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
13 lessons × 40 minutes = 520 minutes
Driving question
How do digestive structures and controlled tests support claims about food? KICD: How is the human digestive system adapted to its functions?
Source learning outcomes
describe the digestion of different types of food in each region of the digestive system,explain the adaptations of parts of the human digestive system,perform experiments to determine presence of different nutrients in food,appreciate the role played by different parts of the digestive system.
Preparation and evidence
Use the companion Student Materials and Materials Teacher Key. Card identifiers are local references,not web links. Practical demonstrations require the teacher’s local risk assessment. Standard CBE documents and structured JSON are supplied with the accepted unit after content review. Consult the unit review record for independent review and classroom confirmation status.
