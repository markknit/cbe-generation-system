                                                               SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Core Mathematics

Strand                           2.0 Measurements and Geometry

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand 2.7: Surface Area and Volume of Solids

Total Duration                   14 lessons × 40 minutes = 560 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Surface Area of Solids; Volume of Solids

Learning Outcomes                determine the surface area of prisms, pyramids, cones, frustums and spheres,
                                 calculate the surface area of composite solids,
                                 calculate the volume of prisms, pyramids, cones, frustums and spheres,
                                 determine the volume of composite solids,
                                 explore the use of the surface area and volume of solids in real-life situations.

Core Competencies                Critical Thinking and Problem Solving: the learner uses a cylinder and a cone of the same radius and height, and a substance such
                                 as sand or water, to determine the volume of a cone as a third of the volume of a cylinder.
                                 Creativity and Imagination: the learner uses a cylinder and a cone of the same radius and height, and a substance such as sand or
                                 water, to determine the volume of a cone as a third of the volume of a cylinder.

Core Values                      Unity: the learner discusses in a group and establishes the relationship between the volume of a cone and the volume of a cylinder.
                                 Respect: the learner listens to others’ opinions as they discuss in groups and work out the volumes of prisms, pyramids, cones,
                                 frustums and spheres.

Science & Engineering            Use nets/cross-sections,decompose boundaries/volumes,model capacity and verify units/limits.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Environment and technology: as the learner collects and uses containers from the immediate environment and use them to discuss
Issues (PCIs)                    and work out their volumes.

Career Connections               Illustrative packaging,storage,material planning and design contexts;no qualification/current-price claims.

Focus for Lessons                Distinguish exposed surfaces from internal volume across prisms,pyramids,cones,frustums,spheres and composites.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   Which surface or space does this decision actually require us to measure? KICD: How do we determine the surface area and
Questions                        volume of solids? Why do we determine the surface area and volume of solids?

Anchoring Phenomenon             A design team agrees on a shape but disagrees about how much covering material or storage space it needs.
 Storyline Thread                         Prism/pyramid/cone/frustum/sphere surfaces → composite boundary → prism/pointed/frustum/sphere volumes → composite space
                                          → application → critique → independent commission.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Dimensioned nets,similar-cone cuts,joined cap interfaces,cavities and ideal container estimates.
