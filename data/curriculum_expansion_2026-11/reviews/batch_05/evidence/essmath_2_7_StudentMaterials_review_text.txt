VC1 — Volume,capacity and units are different claims
Compare area(cm²),geometric volume(cm³)and usable container capacity(mL/L). Convert2500cm³toL,0.75Ltocm³and0.002m³toL. Explain why1m³is1000000cm³rather than100cm³,and why exterior volume need not equal usable capacity.
VC2 — Same base and height:why the cone is one-third
Use rightcone/cylindermodels withradius3cm,verticalheight4cm(coneslant5cm). Calculate volumes and ratio. If teacher supplies matched watertight safe plastic apparatus,perform a supervised clean-water capacity comparison with a measuringcylinder/tray;record actualmeasuredinternaldimensions/volumes and namedpour/record/checkactions. If unavailable,use geometricmodels and mark actualcapacitycomparison NOTOBSERVED. Never pour water into paperprojectmodels.
VC3 — Pyramid volume uses vertical height
Right square pyramid:base4cm by4cm,verticalheight3cm. Determine volume and idealzero-wallcapacity in mL. A right rectangularpyramid base6cm by8cm,height5cm:repeat. Explain why face slantheight cannot replace h.
VC4 — Cone frustum from similar cones
Rightcone-frustum:R5cm,r3cm,verticalheight4cm. Extend slant lines to a common apex;similar-coneheights satisfyHsmall/Hlarge=3/5andHlarge-Hsmall=4. Find both heights,subtract cone volumes and compare withV=(πh/3)(R²+Rr+r²).
VC5 — Measure an actual tapered container honestly
Teacher supplies a clean safe empty plastic container approximating a rightcircularcone-frustum. Measure internal top/bottomdiameters and perpendicular usabledepth with approved ruler/caliper;repeat and record actual readings/toolprecision. Compute an idealcapacity estimate and state wall/taper/rounding/freeboard limits. If shape/interioraccess uncertain,record what is unmeasured instead of forcing a frustum. Separateexactpractice:topdiam10cm,bottomdiam6cm,height12cm.
VC6 — Pyramid-frustum formula needs similar parallel ends
For a right square-pyramidfrustum:bottomside4cm,topside2cm,verticalheight3cm,useV=h/3*(A1+A2+sqrt(A1A2)). Derive by subtracting similarpyramids if possible. Repeat for a rectangularpyramidfrustum:bottom4cm×6cm,top2cm×3cm,height5cm. Explain why arbitrary unrelatedtop/bottomshapes do not satisfy this formula.
