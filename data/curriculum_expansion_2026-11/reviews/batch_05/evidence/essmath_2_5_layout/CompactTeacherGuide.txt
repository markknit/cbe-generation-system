ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: AREA OF A PART OF A CIRCLE
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy

Duration
10 lessons × 40 minutes = 400 minutes

Driving question
Which boundaries and measurements justify a circle-part area? KICD: Where do we use area of part of a circle in real life?

Source learning outcomes
determine the area of a sector of a circle in different situations,
determine the area of a segment of a circle in different situations,
apply the area of part of a circle in real-life situations,
appreciate the application of area of part of circular objects in real-life situations.

Preparation and evidence

assessment. Standard CBE documents and structured JSON are supplied with the accepted unit after content review. Consult the unit review record for independent review and
classroom confirmation status.
