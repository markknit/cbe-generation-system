                      ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: AREA OF A PART OF A CIRCLE
                                 OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 2.5 • 10 lessons • teacher review copy


                                                                 SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Essential Mathematics

Strand                           2.0 Measurement and Geometry

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand2.5: Area of a Part of a Circle

Total Duration                   10 lessons × 40 minutes = 400 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Area of a sector; Area of a segment

Learning Outcomes                determine the area of a sector of a circle in different situations,
                                 determine the area of a segment of a circle in different situations,
                                 apply the area of part of a circle in real-life situations,
                                 appreciate the application of area of part of circular objects in real-life situations.

Core Competencies                Critical Thinking and Problem Solving: the learner explores different options of improvising cutouts of sectors of a circle for working
                                 out their areas.
                                 Learning to Learn: the learner acquires new knowledge as they discuss with peers how to work out areas of circular objects.
                                 Citizenship; learners appreciate their immediate environment as they gather locally available circular objects

Core Values                      Unity: The learner exhibits devotion and honesty by undertaking the task while working with peers to make paper cutouts of sectors
                                 of a circle and obtain the measurements.
                                 Peace: the learner displays care and tolerance while discussing with peers and obtaining circular objects, tracing and marking out
                                 sectors and segments.

Science & Engineering            Trace,measure,partition,calculate and qualify region/application evidence.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Education for Sustainable Development: the learner uses locally available circular objects to trace and mark out sectors and
Issues (PCIs)                    segments, and work out the areas.

Career Connections               Illustrative pattern/material-design practices;no real door/site-clearance qualification.

Focus for Lessons                Sector/segment boundaries,minor/majorareas,actual safe paper models and bounded design/sweep applications.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   Which boundaries and measurements justify a circle-part area? KICD: Where do we use area of part of a circle in real life?
Questions

Anchoring Phenomenon             A fictional decorator removes a circular cap but subtracts a whole wedge and confuses majorsector with majorsegment.
 Storyline Thread                         Boundaries → sector → actualsector → minorsegment → majorcomplement → actualsegment → sweep → capapplication → audit
                                          → independent evidence.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Two-panelboundaryschematic,actualsector/segmentmodels,sectorfractions,trianglepartitions,majorcomplementsandtabletopdoor-
                                          sweepmodels.
