ES2 — Ungrouped frequency table and label meaning

ES3 — Mean from raw and weighted totals
Use ES2numericcounts;calculate sum,N and mean with raw and frequency-weighted methods. Replace8by24and recompute;explain sensitivity and why mean neednot be an observed count. Keep colourlabels out of the arithmetic.
ES4 — Median and all modes
Sort ES2numericcounts and determine median and all modes. For2,3,5,8,9find median;for1,2,3,4state whether a distinct mode exists under the stated school convention. Explain how order and frequency give different summaries.
