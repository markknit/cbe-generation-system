ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: AREA OF POLYGONS
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
11 lessons × 40 minutes = 440 minutes
Driving question
Which dimensions and shape conditions justify a polygon area? KICD: How are areas of polygons useful?
Source learning outcomes
compute area of a triangle given two sides and an included acute angle,
determine the area of a triangle given three sides using Heron’s Formula,
determine the area of a rhombus and parallelogram in different situations,
determine the area of regular pentagon and hexagon from real life situations,
appreciate the application of areas of polygons in real-life situations.
