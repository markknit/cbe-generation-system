Plant Growth and Development — Student Materials
Grade 10 • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
GD1 — Growth and development are related but distinct
Compare seed swelling immediately after water uptake,subsequent irreversible radicle elongation/new tissue,and later differentiation of leaves/flowers. Explain growth as permanent increase in size and/or biomass through cellular processes,and development as changes in organisation/differentiation/maturity. Give one useful plant-management reason to distinguish them. Can a longer etiolated seedling automatically be called healthier or heavier in dry mass?
GD2 — Dormancy is not simply a dry or dead seed
Distinguish viable dormant seed,viable nondormant seed lacking water or suitable temperature,and a dead/nonviable seed. Match possible dormancy causes to conceptual treatments:water-impermeable coat,physiological inhibition/ABA-related state,immature embryo/after-ripening requirement. Explain why safe species-specific guidance is needed before scarification,stratification or hormonal treatment;do not perform these treatments.
GD3 — Plan actual water and temperature comparisons
Use teacher-approved untreated viable common-bean seeds from one labelled lot,clean towels and ventilated covered trays. Plan three conditions:Amoist,recorded suitable room temperature;Bdry,same room conditions;Cmoist,teacher-monitored cooler environment selected for that seed lot. Use3replicate trays of10seeds per condition across the class;randomly allocate seeds. Keep seedlot,counts,container,towel,light and duration comparable,with moisture matched A/Cand no waterlogging. Define germination as radicle at least2mm;record date,actualtemperature,moisture/addedwater,counts,mould/failed setup and named roles.
GD4 — Set up and keep a genuine raw record
Only with teacher approval:set up the GD3actual trays,IDs and conditions. Each learner performs a named seed-count/label/moisture-record/check action. Use this blank dailyrecord:trayID|condition|date/time|actualtemperature|wateradded/moisturecheck|newgerminated|cumulativegerminated|ungerminated/missing|observer|exception. Adult/class monitoring continues between lessons;rotate roles and retain all readings. Do not seal a new oxygen-exclusion culture or invent expected results.
GD5 — Follow up actual conditions and separate supplied data
After the scheduled multi-day interval,use actual GD4records to compute germination percentages per tray and compare A/BandA/Cwith conditions/missingness. Do not declare every failure dead/dormant. Separate fictional seven-day data for practice:three10seedtrays each,Acounts8,9,7;B0,1,0;C3,4,2. Compute total/mean percentage by condition,identify limits,and explain why this did not directly isolate oxygen or prove light unnecessary for every species.
GD6 — Observe epigeal and hypogeal seedlings over time
Teacher prepares labelled same-age series/actual seedlings of common bean(Phaseolus vulgaris,epigeal)and pea(Pisum sativum,hypogeal)in safe transparentsideviewcontainers with a marked sowing/soil level;retains dates/conditions. Observe cotyledon positions and the elongating hypocotyl/epicotyl where resolved. Draw only visible structures and compare stages;each learner records an actual named observation/sketch/check. Do not confuse cotyledons with first true leaves or assume every bean species behaves like common bean.
GD7 — Primary and secondary growth are not old versus young
Use conceptual model:apical meristems extend typical roots/shoots;vascular cambium in many woody eudicots/gymnosperms adds secondary xylem inward and secondary phloem outward;cork cambium contributes protective tissues. Explain length versus girth and why secondary growth can occur in a plant that also continues primary growth. Do not infer every plant or every stem has the same vascular-cambium pattern.
GD8 — Germination is not all later growth
Explain roles of water,oxygen,suitable temperature,light and mineral nutrients in germination versus later plant growth/development. Fictional shoot lengths on days0,2,4,6,8,10are0,0,1,3,5,6cm. Calculate average length-change rates for each2dayinterval and overall0–10rate. Explain why length alone does not show whole-plant biomass,celltype or health,and why seed reserves can support initial germination before external mineral supply/photosynthesis.
GD9 — Hormones have interacting,context-dependent roles
Make a role table:auxin,gibberellins,cytokinins,abscisic acid(ABA),ethylene. Use conceptual examples:shootcell elongation/tropism,seed germination/stemelongation,cell division,seed dormancy/stomatal closure,fruitripening/abscission. Explain dose/tissue/developmental context and why one hormone is not always a universal growth accelerator. Do not apply or prepare hormones.
GD10 — Check three source claims safely
Use teacher-approved print or digital sources to check each:one growth-versus-development distinction,one species-specific dormancy-breaking explanation,and one hormone-role claim from GD9. Record source/title/date/claim/evidence/limit for allthree,then share one checked useful implication with peers. For digital use,no personal login/data upload,unknown download or external contact;teacher previewsaccess. Printed-only alternative remains source reasoning,not actual digital-use evidence.
