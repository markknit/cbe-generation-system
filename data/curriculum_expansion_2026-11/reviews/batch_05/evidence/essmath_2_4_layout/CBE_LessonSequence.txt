                             ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: AREA OF POLYGONS
                                 OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 2.4 • 11 lessons • teacher review copy


                                                                SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Essential Mathematics

Strand                           2.0 Measurement and Geometry

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand2.4: Area of Polygons

Total Duration                   11 lessons × 40 minutes = 440 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Area of triangle; Area of rhombus and parallelogram.; Area of a pentagon and hexagon,

Learning Outcomes                compute area of a triangle given two sides and an included acute angle,
                                 determine the area of a triangle given three sides using Heron’s Formula,
                                 determine the area of a rhombus and parallelogram in different situations,
                                 determine the area of regular pentagon and hexagon from real life situations,
                                 appreciate the application of areas of polygons in real-life situations.

Core Competencies                Creativity and Imagination: the learner acquires networking skills by undertaking group activities and exchanging new ideas while
                                 discussing discuss the applications of area of polygons.
                                 Learning to learn: the learner learns independently when measuring the sides of a tringle and works out the area using the Heron’s
                                 Formula.

Core Values                      Unity: the learner shares available resources amicably while using selected objects with the shape of a regular pentagons and
                                 hexagon when working out the area.
                                 Responsibility: the learner offers leadership to others while discussing with peers the applications of area of polygons

Science & Engineering            Measure,partition,derive,calculate and check geometric conditions and units.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Social Awareness Skills: the learner develops effective communication skills when discussing with peers the applications of area of
Issues (PCIs)                    polygons.

Career Connections               Illustrative design/material-planning practices;no site/engineering qualification.

Focus for Lessons                Included-acute-angle and Heron triangles,parallelogram/rhombus,regularpentagon/hexagon,actual safe measurements and
                                 bounded applications.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   Which dimensions and shape conditions justify a polygon area? KICD: How are areas of polygons useful?
Questions

Anchoring Phenomenon             A fictional planner applies regular-pentagon formulas to every five-sided outline and treats any adjacent side as a height.
 Storyline Thread                         Includedangle → measure → Heron → validity/measure → parallelogram → rhombus → pentagon → hexagon → application →
                                          audit → independent evidence.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Actual paper triangles,valid/degenerate sides,angle-dependent parallelograms,diagonal partitions,regular central triangles and a
                                          tile-fit limit.
