                                 OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 2.2 • 11 lessons • teacher review copy


                                                                SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Essential Mathematics

Strand                           2.0 Measurement and Geometry

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand2.2: Reflection

Total Duration                   11 lessons × 40 minutes = 440 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Symmetry and Reflection; Reflection on a plane surface; reflection on a Cartesian plane (mirror line to be x=0, y=0, y=x),

Learning Outcomes                identify lines of symmetry in plane figures,
                                 determine the properties of reflection using objects and its image,
                                 draw an image given an object and a mirror line on a plane surface,
                                 draw an image given an object and a mirror line on a Cartesian plane,
                                 draw the mirror line given an object and its image on a plane surface and a Cartesian plane,
                                 Apply reflection in real-life situations.
                                 Appreciate the role of reflection in real life situations.

Core Competencies                Learning to Learn: the learner develops relationship skills when discussing with peers the applications of reflections in real life such
                                 as use of mirrors in road transport.
                                 Creativity and Imagination: the learner acquires connection skills when making cut outs of plane figures for finding the number of
                                 lines of symmetry.

Core Values                      Unity: the learner takes turns when working with peers to make cut outs of plane figures used reflection.
                                 Responsibility: the learner takes care of instruments used to construct images formed after reflection in the mirror lines.

Science & Engineering            Construct,test matched points and justify a transformation with correspondence evidence.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Road safety: the learner discusses with peers the applications of reflections in real life such as use of mirrors in road transport.
Issues (PCIs)

Career Connections               Illustrative design,layout and geometry practices;no vehicle-operation or professional safety qualification.

Focus for Lessons                Exact symmetry,plane-surface and scoped Cartesian reflections,inverse mirror construction and limited practical applications.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   How can we construct and test a reflection before trusting its application? KICD: How is reflection important in day-to-day
Questions                        activities?

Anchoring Phenomenon             A fictional design team claims every rectangle has diagonal symmetry and every vehicle mirror gives an equal-size complete view.
 Storyline Thread                         Symmetry → properties → plain construction → x=0 → y=0 → y=x → plain inverse → Cartesian consistency → application → audit
                                          → independent evidence.

 Supporting Phenomena                     Folds,acrylic plane-mirror observation,plain-paper perpendicular bisectors,three Cartesian mirrors and an inconsistent pair set.
