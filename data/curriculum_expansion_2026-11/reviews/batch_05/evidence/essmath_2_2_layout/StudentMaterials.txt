Reflection — Student Materials
Grade 10 • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy


RF1 — Which folds really match?
Draw/cut a non-square rectangle4cm by2cm,a square3cm by3cm,an equilateral triangle
side4cm,and a scalene triangle with sides3,4,5cm. Predict each number of symmetry
lines,then fold/trace to test complete coincidence. Show every line on a labelled drawing.
A leaf may be only approximately symmetric;do not assume all natural leaves have
exact symmetry.


RF2 — Plane-mirror evidence and its limits
Use a teacher-approved stable acrylic plane mirror,one upright card marker and a
marked tabletop line. With mirror vertical on the line,place marker3cm then5cm in
front;use a second marker or teacher-supervised tracing arrangement to locate the
apparent image position. Record your actual observations and method limits. On paper
draw P3cm from line m and locate Pprime so m is the perpendicular bisector of
PPprime. Compare distances,lengths,angles and orientation of a labelled asymmetric
paper shape and its reflection.


RF3 — Construct without axes
On blank paper draw a vertical line m. Mark A2cm left of m;mark B4cm left of m and3cm
above A;mark C1cm left of m and4cm above A. Join ABC. With ruler/set square draw
perpendiculars to m and construct A,B,C images at equal perpendicular distances on its
right. Keep construction lines and label corresponding vertices. Check reflecting twice
returns ABC.


RF4 — Reflect across x=0
On the supplied equal-unit grid plot A(1,1),B(4,1),C(2,3). Construct their images in x=0
using equal perpendicular distances. Record coordinates,join both triangles and justify
which coordinate changes. Reflect D(0,-2) and E(-3,2) too.

RF5 — Reflect across y=0
On supplied grid plot P(-3,1),Q(1,1),R(-2,4). Draw images in y=0 and justify distances.
Also reflect S(2,0) and T(2,-3). Explain why changing both signs would be a different
transformation.

RF6 — Reflect across y=x
Plot A(1,2),B(4,2),C(2,5) on supplied equal-unit grid. Draw y=x and construct reflected
triangle. Check each joining segment is perpendicular to the mirror and its midpoint lies
on it. Reflect D(-2,3) and E(-2,-2). Explain why swapping coordinates works.

RF7 — Recover a mirror line on plain paper
On blank paper mark A and Aprime6cm apart horizontally. Mark B2cm above A and
Bprime2cm above Aprime. Construct the perpendicular bisector of AAprime using a
compass/ruler or measured midpoint plus set square. Verify it also bisects BBprime at
right angles. Join AB and AprimeBprime. Explain why an arbitrary connecting line is not
the mirror.


RF8 — Recover and test the Cartesian mirror
Use supplied grid. CaseU:A(1,2)→Aimage(2,1),B(1,4)→Bimage(4,1). CaseV:C(-
2,1)→Cimage(2,1),D(-1,3)→Dimage(1,3). CaseW:E(1,2)→Eimage(1,-
2),F(3,1)→Fimage(3,-2). Draw the mirror for UandV. Test whether Wfits one reflection in
the allowed lines x=0,y=0,y=x;explain using distances/midpoints.

RF9 — A safe design application
A fictional school emblem uses triangular half A(1,1),B(3,1),C(2,4) and mirror x=0. Draw
a complete bilateral emblem and justify how the construction fixes alignment/size. State
one practical benefit of symmetric layout and one limitation of symmetry as a quality test.
Use classmates’ reasons respectfully;no personal body images.

RF10 — Road mirrors:application without an unsafe demonstration
Read this scenario:an adult driver consults a correctly adjusted mirror before a
manoeuvre,but an object may still be in a blind spot. Explain a useful role of reflected
information and why the mirror alone cannot guarantee safety. Distinguish the ideal
plane mirror studied here from a convex vehicle mirror that can widen field but alter
image size. Discuss using fictional diagram descriptions only;do not enter roads,vehicles
or driving practice.


RF11 — Audit three reflection claims
Audit:(1)a non-square rectangle has four lines of symmetry;(2)in y=x,the image of(-
2,3)is(-3,2);(3)joining one nonfixed point directly to its image gives the mirror. Give
corrected statements with drawings/evidence. Then discuss why one fixed pair alone
does not determine a unique unrestricted mirror.
