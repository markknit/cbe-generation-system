ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: REFLECTION
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy

Duration
11 lessons × 40 minutes = 440 minutes

Driving question
How can we construct and test a reflection before trusting its application? KICD: How is reflection important in day-to-day activities?

Source learning outcomes
identify lines of symmetry in plane figures,
determine the properties of reflection using objects and its image,
draw an image given an object and a mirror line on a plane surface,
draw an image given an object and a mirror line on a Cartesian plane,
draw the mirror line given an object and its image on a plane surface and a Cartesian plane,
Apply reflection in real-life situations.
Appreciate the role of reflection in real life situations.

assessment. Standard CBE documents and structured JSON are supplied with the accepted unit after content review. Consult the unit review record for independent review and
