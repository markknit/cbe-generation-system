CORE MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: STATISTICS I
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
16 lessons × 40 minutes = 640 minutes
Driving question
Which data and representations justify a local decision? KICD: What is statistics?; How do we represent data?; How do we use statistics in day-to-day life?
Source learning outcomes
collect data from real-life sources,draw a frequency distribution table for grouped and ungrouped data,determine mean, mode and median of grouped and ungrouped data,represent data using histograms and frequency polygons,interpret data from histograms and frequency polygons,promote data collection, organisation and representation for informed decision making.
Preparation and evidence
Use the companion Student Materials and Materials Teacher Key. Card identifiers are local references,not web links. Practical demonstrations require the teacher’s local risk assessment. Standard CBE documents and structured JSON are supplied with the accepted unit after content review. Consult the unit review record for independent review and classroom confirmation status.
