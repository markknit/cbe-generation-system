GD1 — Growth and development are related but distinct
Compare seed swelling immediately after water uptake,subsequent irreversible radicle elongation/new tissue,and later differentiation of leaves/flowers. Explain growth as permanent increase in size and/or biomass through cellular processes,and development as changes in organisation/differentiation/maturity. Give one useful plant-management reason to distinguish them. Can a longer etiolated seedling automatically be called healthier or heavier in dry mass?

GD2 — Dormancy is not simply a dry or dead seed
Distinguish viable dormant seed,viable nondormant seed lacking water or suitable temperature,and a dead/nonviable seed. Match possible dormancy causes to conceptual treatments:water-impermeable coat,physiological inhibition/ABA-related state,immature embryo/after-ripening requirement. Explain why safe species-specific guidance is needed before scarification,stratification or hormonal treatment;do not perform these treatments.

GD7 — Primary and secondary growth are not old versus young
Use conceptual model:apical meristems extend typical roots/shoots;vascular cambium in many woody eudicots/gymnosperms adds secondary xylem inward and secondary phloem outward;cork cambium contributes protective tissues. Explain length versus girth and why secondary growth can occur in a plant that also continues primary growth. Do not infer every plant or every stem has the same vascular-cambium pattern.
