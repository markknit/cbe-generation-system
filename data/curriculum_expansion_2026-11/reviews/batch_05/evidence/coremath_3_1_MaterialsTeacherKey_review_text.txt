SI1 — Plan a real local data question
Project question:which teacher-approved classroom storage area has the greatest need for repair/replacement of ordinary paper/card supply boxes? Use safe accessible on-site boxes only. Agree what counts as a box and a recorded condition category,assign anonymous box IDs,define accessible sampling frame,avoid duplicate counts and state that inaccessible/uninspected boxes are missing,not good condition. Teacher may approve another non-personal low-risk classroom inventory. Plan one numeric measurement,for example external box length in cm,and one categorical condition record. No climbing,sharp/waste handling,people data or road visits.
Teacher key and preparation
Accept a feasible local question/operational definitions,unit,inclusion/exclusion rules,safe accessible frame and honest missing-data plan. This is a plan only;actual collection is SI2. It is not a representative whole-school/community survey by default. Parent/guardian or school staff guidance may be sought through teacher-approved normal channels;do not send messages from this workflow or require personal disclosure.
SI2 — Collect and keep an honest record
Under teacher supervision inspect/measure approved classroom boxes using the SI1 plan. Use this repeatable blank record on paper:anonymous boxID | source/location category | date | length(cm) | condition category | observer initials | duplicate/missing/uncertainty note. Retain actual raw records;each learner contributes at least one observed measure/record/check. If access is unavailable,use the supplied fictional later cards for analysis and mark actual collection NOT OBSERVED;do not invent project data.
Teacher key and preparation
Teacher observes named actual measurement/recording/checking,unit/tool use and duplicate handling. Record sampled accessible frame,uncertainty and missingness. Group artifact alone does not prove each learner collected. Counts and conclusions depend on actual records,so no universal numeric project key. Paper copy is primary evidence even if later entered digitally.

For unequal-width classes,use histogram density to discuss the highest-concentration interval and state your convention instead of equating raw frequency with bar height.
Teacher key and preparation

For unequal widths compare densities for histogram peaks;explicitly distinguish the highest-frequency interval from highest-density interval. Formula is used only for suitable equal-width interior modal class with nonzero denominator.
