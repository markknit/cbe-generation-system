ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: TRIGONOMETRY
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes
Driving question
How do reference angles and honest measurements make a triangle model useful? KICD: Why is trigonometry important?
Source learning outcomes
identify the sides of a right-angled triangle in relation to a particular angle,
determine the sine, cosine and tangent of acute angles in a right-angled triangle,
relate sines and cosines of complementary angles,
apply trigonometric ratios to angles of elevation and depression,
recognise the use of trigonometry in real-life situations.
