Trigonometry — Student Materials
Grade 10 • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
TR1 — The chosen angle changes the side names
Triangle ABC is right-angled at B,AB=4cm,BC=3cm,AC=5cm. Use the supplied labelled diagram. Relative to angle A,identify hypotenuse,opposite and adjacent(non-hypotenuse) sides;repeat relative to angle C. Explain which side name stays fixed and which switch. The diagram is a mathematical model,not a measured object.
TR2 — Three ratios from one triangle
For TR1calculate sinA,cosA,tanA and sinC,cosC,tanC using opposite/hypotenuse,adjacent/hypotenuse,opposite/adjacent. Label the reference angle each time. Explain why ratios have no length unit. Repeat with similar triangle legs8and6,hypotenuse10.
TR3 — Calculator degree mode and ratio choice
Use degree mode. Calculate sin30°,cos60°,cos30°,sin60°,tan45° to4decimalplaces. Explain why sin30=cos60 and why the degree symbol matters. Given a right triangle with an acute angle30° and hypotenuse10cm,find opposite and adjacent legs. Do not treat30°as30radians.
TR4 — Why complementary sine and cosine match
AnglesAandCofTR1sumto90°. Use the exchanged opposite/adjacent roles to explain sinA=cosC and cosA=sinC. Create table for complementary pairs20°/70°,30°/60°,40°/50°using degree mode or supplied ratios:sin20≈0.3420,cos20≈0.9397;sin30=0.5,cos30≈0.8660;sin40≈0.6428,cos40≈0.7660. Complete the complementary sine/cosine values and state rounding limits.
TR5 — Choose the known and needed side
Right-triangle cases:(a)angle30°,horizontal adjacent leg12m,find opposite vertical rise;(b)angle60°,opposite leg6m,find hypotenuse;(c)angle45°,adjacent leg5m,find hypotenuse. Draw each triangle,label right/reference angles and choose the appropriate ratio. Round lengths to2decimalplaces and keep units.
TR6 — Elevation is measured from the observer’s horizontal
Fictional level-ground survey:horizontal distance from sight position to vertical pole base12m;eye/instrument height1.5m;angle of elevation30°. Draw the right triangle and find vertical rise above sight level and total pole height. Explain why sightline length is not the horizontal baseline and why eye height is added.
TR7 — Depression uses a horizontal reference too
Fictional diagram:observer sight point9m vertically above a level target;angle of depression30°. Draw observer horizontal and target horizontal,parallel to one another,and the sightline. Find horizontal separation and sightline distance. Explain why the target’s elevation angle equals the observer’s depression angle under these parallel horizontals.
TR8 — Build and calibrate an actual simple clinometer
Use a teacher-supplied semicircular protractor/cardboard degree template,string,small blunt secured weight,tape and cardboard sight tube/open sight. Teacher prepares sharp holes/cuts. Fix sight tube along the protractor straight baseline and suspend string from its centre. Hold baseline horizontal:plumb reading should be90°. For an acute aim,angle magnitude=|90°-plumb reading|;record upward(elevation)or downward(depression)separately. Check against a teacher-fixed safe30°reference. Each learner contributes a named assembly/calibration action;record actual readings/limits. Do not sight the sun,lamps,people or outside hazards.
TR9 — Use elevation and depression safely
Teacher fixes two safe indoor wall targets,one above and one below a marked stationary accessible sight position;both away from windows/brightlights and within an unobstructed dry classroom area. Use your calibrated TR8clinometer to measure elevation and depression from that position;take3readings each and record raw plumb readings,direction,derived magnitude and spread. Teacher measures/approves a horizontal baseline and sight/target heights for an optional triangle estimate. No steps,ladders,roads,balconies or movement while sighting. Each learner performs an actual named sight/read/record/check action.
TR10 — A checked real-life use and digital support
Explain one useful school/community application of triangle ratios using a labelled model and a stated assumption. Retrieve or view a teacher-approved digital/printed trigonometry explanation,record source/title and check one ratio/complementary claim against TR1orTR4. Record actual digital action separately;printed-only alternative is source reasoning,not digital-use evidence. State one measurement limitation from the actual clinometer project.
TR11 — Audit four trigonometry claims
Correct:(1)the hypotenuse changes when choosing the other acute angle;(2)tanA=opposite/hypotenuse;(3)sin20°=cos20°;(4)a pole’s height is always d tanθwithout any observer-height/datum adjustment. Use a labelled right triangle or a complementary relation,and give one safe practical implication.
