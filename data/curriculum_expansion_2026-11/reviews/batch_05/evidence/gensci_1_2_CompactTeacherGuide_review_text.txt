GENERAL SCIENCE GRADE 10: THE CELL
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes
Driving question
What can a cell image establish,and how do its structures support an organism? KICD: What makes up an organism?
Source learning outcomes
explain the differences between a light and an electron microscope.describe the plant and animal cell under an electron microscope,compare plant and animal cell structures under light and electron microscope,explain the functions of the components of plant and animal cells,construct a concept map of the levels of cell organisation,appreciate the function of the cell as a basic unit of life.
