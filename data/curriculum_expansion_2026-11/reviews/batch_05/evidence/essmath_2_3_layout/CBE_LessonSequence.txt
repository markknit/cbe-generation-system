                                 ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: TRIGONOMETRY
                                 OpenAI-only evaluation • Sub-Strand 2.3 • 12 lessons • teacher review copy


                                                                SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Essential Mathematics

Strand                           2.0 Measurement and Geometry

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand2.3: Trigonometry

Total Duration                   12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Sines and Cosines of complementary angles; Application of trigonometric ratios (angle of elevation and depression)

Learning Outcomes                identify the sides of a right-angled triangle in relation to a particular angle,
                                 determine the sine, cosine and tangent of acute angles in a right-angled triangle,
                                 relate sines and cosines of complementary angles,
                                 apply trigonometric ratios to angles of elevation and depression,
                                 recognise the use of trigonometry in real-life situations.

Core Competencies                Self-efficacy: The learner makes observations and establishes the relationships between the sine and cosine ratios.
                                 Learning to learn: The learner learns collaboratively while making a simple clinometer using locally available materials and using it
                                 as a learning resource.

Core Values                      Love: The learner demonstrates a caring attitude to others when using a simple clinometer to measure the angle of elevation and
                                 depression of different objects in the immediate environment.

Science & Engineering            Label,form ratios,compare complementary relationships,measure and qualify a model.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Self-esteem: The learners acquire self-esteem while making a simple clinometer using locally available materials and use it as a
Issues (PCIs)                    learning resource.

Career Connections               Illustrative surveying/design practices;no engineering certification or unsafe field access.

Focus for Lessons                Acute right-triangle sine/cosine/tangent,complementary sine/cosine,elevation/depression and actual safe clinometer project.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   How do reference angles and honest measurements make a triangle model useful? KICD: Why is trigonometry important?
Questions

Anchoring Phenomenon             A fictional survey report uses the wrong adjacent side and reports rise above eye level as total pole height.

Storyline Thread                 Sides → ratios → degree mode → complements → choose ratio → elevation → depression → build → use → application/source
                                 → audit → independent model.
 Supporting Phenomena                     Labelled3–4–5triangle,similar ratios,degree benchmarks,complement table,paper surveys and actual calibrated indoor readings.
