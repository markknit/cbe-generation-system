                                                                  SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Essential Mathematics

Strand                           3.0 Statistics and Probability

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand3.1: Statistics I

Total Duration                   11 lessons × 40 minutes = 440 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Ungrouped data; Application of graphs of data

Learning Outcomes                draw a frequency distribution table for ungrouped data
                                 determine mean, mode and median of ungrouped data,
                                 represent data using bar graphs, line graphs and pie charts,
                                 interpret data from bar graphs, line graphs and pie charts,
                                 appreciate the importance of data organization and interpretation in making effective decisions

Core Competencies                Critical thinking and problem solving: the learner develops interpretation and inference skills when collecting data.
                                 Digital literacy: the learner develops the skills of connecting and interacting with digital technology when using digital devices to
                                 draw graphs and charts to represent the data collected.

Core Values                      Integrity: the learner displays honesty while working in groups and collecting data such making frequency distribution tables.
                                 Love: the learner is considerate of the other’s when working with peers to extract information from graphs and charts draw
                                 conclusions

Science & Engineering            Collect,organise,summarise,represent and interpret data with explicit variable/frame limits.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Life Skills (Decision Making): the learner makes informed decisions when identifying patterns from the graphs and charts and
Issues (PCIs)                    drawing conclusions.

Career Connections               Illustrative data-recording/planning practices;no statistical or school-resource certification.

Focus for Lessons                Ungroupedfrequency/mean/median/mode,bar/line/pieconstruction/interpretation,actualsafeproject and allthree actualdigitalgraphs.

Driving Question / Key Inquiry   Which records,summaries and graphchoices justify a useful decision? KICD: How do we use statistics in day-to-day life?
Questions

Anchoring Phenomenon             A fictional stockreport averagesarbitrarycolourcodes and draws a time trend through unorderedcategories.

Storyline Thread                 Actualrecord → tables/types → mean → median/mode → bar → line → pie → graphaudit → actualdigitalgraphs → projectdecision

 Supporting Phenomena                     Actualpaperstockcounts/timepoints,separatenumeric/categorydata,means/ties,barbaseline,lineorder,piewhole and
                                          actualdigitalverification.
