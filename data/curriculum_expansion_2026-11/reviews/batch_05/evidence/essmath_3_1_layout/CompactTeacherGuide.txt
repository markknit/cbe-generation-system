ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: STATISTICS I
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy

Duration
11 lessons × 40 minutes = 440 minutes

Driving question
Which records,summaries and graphchoices justify a useful decision? KICD: How do we use statistics in day-to-day life?

Source learning outcomes
draw a frequency distribution table for ungrouped data
determine mean, mode and median of ungrouped data,
represent data using bar graphs, line graphs and pie charts,
interpret data from bar graphs, line graphs and pie charts,
appreciate the importance of data organization and interpretation in making effective decisions

Preparation and evidence
Use the companion Student Materials and Materials Teacher Key. Card identifiers are local references,not web links. Practical demonstrations require the teacher’s local risk
assessment. Standard CBE documents and structured JSON are supplied with the accepted unit after content review. Consult the unit review record for independent review and
classroom confirmation status.
