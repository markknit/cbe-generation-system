SF1 — Cone net:arc length must fit the base
Right cone:r3cm,verticalheight4cm,slant5cm. On blank paper construct a radius5cm sector of216°(equivalently remove144°from a full circle)and a separate radius3cm base disk;make/label the lateral net and disk with safe teacher-approved tools. Verify sector arc equals base circumference. Calculate lateral and total closed surface area;state which pieces a cone without base has. A labelled schematic is supplied,not physicalprintscale.
SF2 — Square-pyramid net:four faces and a base
A right square pyramid has base side4cm and face slantheight3cm. Use supplied net:central4cmsquare and four outward isosceles triangles,eachbase4cm/perpendicularfaceheight3cm. Make/label a safe paper model or record accessible template adaptation. Compute lateral and closed total area;explain why faceheight is not verticalpyramidheight.
SF3 — A rectangular base gives two face heights
Right rectangular pyramid:base8cm by6cm,verticalheight4cm. Derive face slantheight to each8cmedge and each6cmedge. Draw a net with centralrectangle,two8-base triangles and two6-base triangles;label corresponding heights. Compute lateral and closed area.
SF4 — Sphere area is not a flat exact net
Ideal sphere radius3cm:use surfacearea4πr²and compute. A second sphere diameter10cm:determine radius first and area. Explain why paper facets/orange-peel analogies approximate rather than form a perfectly flat exact sphere net. Contrast surfacearea units with volumeunits.
SF5 — Hemisphere:which circular face is included?
Ideal hemisphere radius4cm. Compute curvedsurface only,then closed total including its circular cutface. Compare with a zero-thickness open dome/bowl model that counts only the curved outer surface. Explain why a real thick bowl’s inner/outer/rim areas require more dimensions and an explicit counted-surface statement.
SF6 — Conical frustum:the end disks are separate
Right conical frustum has top radiusR5cm,bottomradiusr3cm and slantlengths4cm. Verify a feasible verticalheight,then compute curvedlateralareaπ(R+r)s. Compute totalclosedarea,open-topbucketarea withbottompresent,andopen-both-endsarea. Make a counted-face list before adding disks.
SF7 — Square-pyramid frustum:four trapezoids
Right square-pyramid frustum has bottomsquare side6cm,topsquare side2cm,and each trapezoidalfaceperpendicularslantheight5cm. Draw a net partition into fourtrapezoidsplusendfaces. Compute lateral,closedtotal,open-topwithbottom,andopen-bothareas. Explain why a cone-frustum formula does not fit.

SF9 — Measure a safe spherical approximation
Teacher supplies a clean soft ball approximating a sphere and an approved empty plastic dome/hemisphere if available.
