ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS GRADE 10: SURFACE AREA OF SOLIDS
Compact teacher guide • OpenAI-only curriculum draft • teacher review copy
Duration
12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes
Driving question
Which nets,faces and measurements justify a solid’s surface area? KICD: How solids used in real life?
Source learning outcomes
determine the surface area of cones and pyramids from the nets,
calculate the surface area of a sphere and a hemisphere,
determine the surface area of frustums of cones and pyramids,
apply the surfaces area of cones, pyramids and frustums in real life situations,
appreciate the application of surface area of solids.
