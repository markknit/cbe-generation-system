CP3 — Make and measure an actual sector cut-out
Trace a teacher-approved clean circular lid or draw a circle on safe card. Mark centre using a known compass centre or two chord perpendicular bisectors;draw an acute sector,cut with approved blunt scissors or use an accessible prepared template. Each learner measures actual radius and central angle with ruler/protractor and computes area;record tool precision and whether making/cutting was actually performed. Compare measured fraction to whole circle.
