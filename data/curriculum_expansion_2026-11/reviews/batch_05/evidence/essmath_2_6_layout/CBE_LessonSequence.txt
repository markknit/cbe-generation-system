                                                                SUB-STRAND OVERVIEW
Grade Level                      10

Subject                          Essential Mathematics

Strand                           2.0 Measurement and Geometry

Sub-Strand                       Sub-Strand2.6: Surface Area of Solids

Total Duration                   12 lessons × 40 minutes = 480 minutes

Sub-Strand Content               Surface area cone, pyramid (square or rectangular base), sphere, frustum; Applications on area of solids

Learning Outcomes                determine the surface area of cones and pyramids from the nets,
                                 calculate the surface area of a sphere and a hemisphere,
                                 determine the surface area of frustums of cones and pyramids,
                                 apply the surfaces area of cones, pyramids and frustums in real life situations,
                                 appreciate the application of surface area of solids.

Core Competencies                Critical Thinking and Problem Solving: the learner uses a cylinder and a cone of the same radius and height to relate volume of a
                                 cove to the volume of a cylinder.
                                 Creativity and Imagination: The learner work with peers to model frustums of cones and pyramids both open and closed.

Core Values                      Social Justice: the learner accords equal opportunities when discussing the uses of pyramids and frustums are used
                                 Responsibility: the learner observes safety precautions of others while using locally available materials to model frustums of cones
                                 and pyramids.

Science & Engineering            Construct nets,match boundaries,list faces,measure and qualify ideal models.
Practices

Pertinent & Contemporary         Environment and technology: The learner collects and uses locally available materials to model frustums of cones and pyramids for
Issues (PCIs)                    use as a learning resources.

Career Connections               Illustrative material/design practices;no professional construction/site approval.

Focus for Lessons                Cone/square/rectangular-pyramid nets,sphere/hemisphere,open/closed conical/pyramidfrustums,actual safe models/diameters and
                                 bounded applications.

                                                              A. SPECIFIC LEARNING OUTCOMES
Category                       Specific Learning Outcomes

Purpose                        Determine cone surface area from a compatible sector-and-disk net.

Knowledge                      Lateral arc must match base circumference;slantheight and counted disks determine area.

Skills                         Construct/label a net and verify arc/area geometry.

Attitudes                      Offer equal participation,share materials fairly and report actual model/measurement work honestly.
