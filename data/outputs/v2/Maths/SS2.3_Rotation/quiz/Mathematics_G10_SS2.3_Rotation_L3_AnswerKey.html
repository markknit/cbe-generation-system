Q1. A learner is asked to sketch triangle ABC after a 90° anticlockwise rotation about a point O outside the triangle. Her sketch shows the triangle in exactly the same orientation as the original, only shifted to a new place. Which transformation has she drawn?
  1. A translation (slide)
  2. A reflection (mirror flip)
  3. A rotation about O
  4. An enlargement
Correct answer: A
A shape that keeps the same orientation and is only moved to a new position has been slid, which is a translation. A rotation turns the shape about O, so its orientation changes.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Step 3 of 'The Clock Construction Method' (measure the distance with the compass)
Q2. In the construction method, the compass is opened to the distance OA and an arc is drawn through A before the image A′ is marked. Why must OA′ be equal to OA?
  1. Because rotation preserves the distance of every point from the centre
  2. Because the image must be larger than the object
  3. Because the protractor needs a ray of the same length
  4. Because the compass cannot be changed once it is set
Correct answer: A
Every point turns on a circular path about the fixed centre, so its distance from the centre does not change. The compass arc shows this circle, which is why OA′ = OA.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Step 4 of 'The Clock Construction Method' (measure the angle with the protractor)
Q3. You are constructing the image of vertex A of a triangle about centre O through 90° anticlockwise. You have drawn a ray from O through A. Where should the protractor be placed to measure the 90° angle?
  1. Centre of the protractor at O, with its baseline along the ray from O through A
  2. Centre of the protractor at the point where the compass arc meets the paper edge
  3. Centre of the protractor at O, with its baseline along the horizontal edge of the paper
  4. Centre of the protractor at A, with its baseline along side AB
Correct answer: A
The angle of rotation is measured at the centre O from the ray OA to the new ray OA′. So the protractor's baseline must lie along the ray from O through A, not along a horizontal or a side of the shape.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: independent practice with quadrilateral PQRS, centre O inside the shape, rotated 180° clockwise
Q4. Quadrilateral PQRS is rotated 180° clockwise about a point O inside it. Vertex P is 5 cm from O. Which statement about the image P′ is correct?
  1. P′ is 10 cm from O, on the opposite side of O from P
  2. P′ is 5 cm from O, on a line through O at right angles to OP
  3. P′ is 5 cm from O, on the same ray as P
  4. P′ is 5 cm from O, on the opposite side of O from P, in line with P and O
Correct answer: D
Rotation keeps the distance from the centre at 5 cm. A half-turn of 180° sends P to the opposite side of O along the same straight line through P and O.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Example B – rectangle with the centre at one corner of the shape, rotated 90°
Q5. A rectangle is rotated 90° about one of its own corners, and that corner is the centre of rotation. What happens to the corner that sits on the centre?
  1. It moves 90° along an arc around the other corners
  2. It stays in the same place and is its own image
  3. It moves to the opposite corner of the image
  4. It moves to the middle of the rectangle
Correct answer: B
The centre of rotation is invariant. A point at distance 0 from the centre does not move, so the corner on the centre is its own image.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Example A – equilateral triangle, centre outside, rotated 270° anticlockwise
Q6. A learner rotates an equilateral triangle 270° anticlockwise about a centre O outside the triangle. Which single rotation about the same centre O gives exactly the same image?
  1. A rotation of 90° anticlockwise
  2. A rotation of 180° clockwise
  3. A rotation of 90° clockwise
  4. A rotation of 270° clockwise
Correct answer: C
A full turn is 360°, and 360° − 270° = 90°. Turning 270° anticlockwise ends in the same place as turning 90° clockwise.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: constructing the minute hand position at 12:45 (270° clockwise) on the clock model
Q7. On a clock model, the minute hand starts at 12:00. It turns clockwise about the centre of the clock face until it shows 12:45. Through what angle has the hand turned?
  1. 45°
  2. 270°
  3. 180°
  4. 90°
Correct answer: B
One hour is a full turn of 360°. 45 minutes is 45/60 of an hour, so the angle is 45/60 × 360 = 270° clockwise.
