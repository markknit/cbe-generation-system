Q1. A telecom mast in Garissa is 45 m tall and stands at a right angle to level ground. A support wire is anchored on the ground 45 m from the base of the mast. What angle does the wire make with the ground?
  1. 45°
  2. 30°
  3. 60°
  4. 90°
Correct answer: A
The opposite side (45 m) equals the adjacent side (45 m), so tan θ = 45/45 = 1, which gives θ = 45°, a special angle.

Q2. A road on a Kericho tea plantation rises at an angle of 35° to the horizontal. The road gains a vertical height of 20 m. What is the horizontal distance along the flat ground, to the nearest 0.1 m?
  1. 28.6 m
  2. 11.5 m
  3. 34.9 m
  4. 14.0 m
Correct answer: A
The vertical height is opposite the angle and the horizontal distance is adjacent, so tan 35° = 20 ÷ adjacent. Adjacent = 20 ÷ tan 35° ≈ 20 ÷ 0.7002 ≈ 28.6 m. Using sine gives the road length (34.9 m), not the horizontal distance.

Q3. A student stands 40 m from the base of a transmission mast in Nakuru. The angle of elevation to the top of the mast is 50°. Using tan 50° ≈ 1.1918, what is the height of the mast, to the nearest 0.1 m?
  1. 33.6 m
  2. 47.7 m
  3. 25.7 m
  4. 30.6 m
Correct answer: B
The 40 m is adjacent and the height is opposite, so use TOA: tan 50° = height ÷ 40. Height = 40 × tan 50° ≈ 40 × 1.1918 ≈ 47.7 m.

Q4. A ramp at Kisumu County Referral Hospital makes an angle of 12° with the ground. The horizontal distance along the ground is 6 m. What is the length of the ramp surface (the hypotenuse), to the nearest 0.01 m?
  1. 1.28 m
  2. 28.88 m
  3. 5.87 m
  4. 6.13 m
Correct answer: D
The 6 m is adjacent and the ramp is the hypotenuse, so use CAH: cos 12° = 6 ÷ hypotenuse. Hypotenuse = 6 ÷ cos 12° ≈ 6 ÷ 0.9781 ≈ 6.13 m.

Q5. A ramp at Mombasa port is 8 m long and makes an angle of 15° with the horizontal. What vertical height does the ramp rise, to the nearest 0.01 m?
  1. 7.73 m
  2. 2.07 m
  3. 30.91 m
  4. 2.14 m
Correct answer: B
The ramp length is the hypotenuse and the height is opposite the 15° angle, so use SOH: sin 15° = height ÷ 8. Height = 8 × sin 15° ≈ 8 × 0.2588 ≈ 2.07 m.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: class question after the pair presentation, 'Does the answer make physical sense?'
Q6. A learner works out that a ramp 8 m long, rising at 15° to the horizontal, has a vertical height of 30.9 m. Which statement explains why this answer cannot be correct?
  1. The height is the opposite side, which must be shorter than the 8 m hypotenuse.
  2. The learner should have used tan 15° instead of sin 15°, because tan always gives heights.
  3. The height of a ramp must always be a whole number of metres.
  4. Angles of 15° always give heights greater than 30 m.
Correct answer: A
The hypotenuse is the longest side of a right-angled triangle, so the vertical height cannot be more than 8 m. The learner divided by sin 15° instead of multiplying by it.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: extension pair problem, Problem 4 (Kericho road, inverse tangent)
Q7. A road in a Kericho tea plantation rises 18 m vertically over a horizontal distance of 54 m. What is the angle of inclination of the road to the nearest 0.1°?
  1. 18.4°
  2. 0.3°
  3. 71.6°
  4. 18.0°
Correct answer: A
The vertical rise is opposite and the horizontal distance is adjacent, so tan θ = 18 ÷ 54 ≈ 0.3333. Then θ = tan⁻¹(0.3333) ≈ 18.4°. Using 54 ÷ 18 would wrongly give 71.6°.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model Building: the decision-rule box (SOH if O and H, CAH if A and H, TOA if O and A)
Q8. In a right-angled triangle you know the length of the hypotenuse and the size of one acute angle. You want to find the side adjacent to that angle. Which ratio should you use?
  1. Tangent, because it uses the adjacent side
  2. Any of the three ratios, because they always give the same answer
  3. Cosine, because it links the adjacent side and the hypotenuse
  4. Sine, because it uses the hypotenuse
Correct answer: C
CAH: cos θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse involves exactly the two sides in the problem. Sine uses the opposite side and tangent uses the opposite and adjacent sides, so neither fits.
