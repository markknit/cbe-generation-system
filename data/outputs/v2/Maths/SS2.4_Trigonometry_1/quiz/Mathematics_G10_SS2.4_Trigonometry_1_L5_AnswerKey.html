Q1. In Construction A, a pair draws an isosceles right-angled triangle with both legs 6 cm long. What is the exact length of the hypotenuse BC?
  1. 6√2 cm (about 8.49 cm)
  2. 12 cm
  3. 36 cm
  4. 6 cm
Correct answer: A
By Pythagoras, BC² = 6² + 6² = 72, so BC = √72 = 6√2 ≈ 8.49 cm. Adding the legs (12 cm) is a common error.
Use after: Observe phase — Construction B: equilateral triangle of side 8 cm bisected from P to M
Q2. In Construction B, an equilateral triangle PQR has sides of 8 cm. A line is drawn from P to the midpoint M of QR, making a right angle at M. What is the exact length of PM?
  1. 4√3 cm (about 6.93 cm)
  2. 4 cm
  3. 4√2 cm (about 5.66 cm)
  4. 8 cm
Correct answer: A
In triangle PQM, QM = 4 cm and QP = 8 cm. So PM² = 8² − 4² = 48, giving PM = √48 = 4√3 ≈ 6.93 cm. This is longer than QM but shorter than QP.
Use after: Explain phase — Part A: Exact derivation, 45° triangle (legs = 1 unit, hypotenuse = √2)
Q3. An isosceles right-angled triangle has two equal legs of 1 unit and a hypotenuse of √2 units. What is tan 45°?
  1. 1
  2. 1/2
  3. 1/√2
  4. √2
Correct answer: A
tan 45° = opposite/adjacent = 1/1 = 1, because both legs are equal. Answering √2 confuses the hypotenuse with the ratio of the legs.
Use after: Explain phase — Part A: Exact derivation, 30-60-90 triangle (short leg = 1, long leg = √3, hypotenuse = 2)
Q4. A 30-60-90 triangle has sides 1, √3 and 2 (the hypotenuse is 2). The shortest side, 1, is opposite the 30° angle. What is sin 60°?
  1. √3
  2. 1/√3
  3. 1/2
  4. √3/2
Correct answer: D
The side opposite 60° is √3 and the hypotenuse is 2, so sin 60° = √3/2. 1/2 is sin 30°, which comes from mixing up which leg is opposite which angle.
Use after: Explain phase — Part A: limit arguments for 0° and 90° using the first and last rows of the trig tables
Q5. A learner writes tan 90° in the special-angles table. Which statement is correct?
  1. tan 90° = √3, because 90° is the largest special angle
  2. tan 90° = 0, because the opposite side shrinks to 0
  3. tan 90° = 1, because the angle is a right angle
  4. tan 90° is undefined, because the adjacent side approaches 0 and division by zero is undefined
Correct answer: D
tan θ = opposite/adjacent. As θ approaches 90° the adjacent side approaches 0, so the ratio cannot be calculated and tan 90° is undefined.
Use after: Explain phase — Part B: Mast application (60° angle of elevation, 40 m horizontal distance)
Q6. A surveyor in Nakuru measures the angle of elevation to the top of a Kenya Power mast as exactly 60° from a point 40 m from the base. Using tan 60° = √3, what is the height of the mast?
  1. 40√3 m (about 69.3 m)
  2. 40/√3 m (about 23.1 m)
  3. 20√3 m (about 34.6 m)
  4. 20 m
Correct answer: A
tan 60° = height/40, so height = 40 × tan 60° = 40√3 ≈ 69.3 m. Dividing by √3 uses the ratio upside down.
Use after: End of lesson — End of lesson: using the special-angles reference card on the Nakuru mast problem
Q7. At another mast near a school in Nakuru County, the angle of elevation to the top is exactly 45° from a point 40 m from the base. What is the height of this mast?
  1. 40 m
  2. 40√2 m (about 56.6 m)
  3. 20 m
  4. 40/√2 m (about 28.3 m)
Correct answer: A
tan 45° = 1, so height = 40 × tan 45° = 40 × 1 = 40 m. At 45° the two legs of the triangle are equal, so the height equals the horizontal distance.
