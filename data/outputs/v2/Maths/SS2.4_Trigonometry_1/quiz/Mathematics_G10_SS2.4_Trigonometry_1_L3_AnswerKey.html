Q1. A pair of learners use mathematical tables and find that sin 20° = 0.3420. What is the value of cos 70° from the same tables?
  1. 0.3640
  2. 0.9397
  3. 0.6580
  4. 0.3420
Correct answer: D
20° and 70° are complementary (20° + 70° = 90°), so cos 70° = sin 20° = 0.3420. 0.9397 is cos 20° and 0.3640 is tan 20°.

Q2. Learners complete a table for the pairs 20°/70°, 30°/60°, 42°/48° and 15°/75°. In every row the sine of one angle equals the cosine of the other angle. Which equation correctly states this pattern?
  1. sin θ = cos(180° − θ)
  2. sin θ = sin(90° − θ)
  3. sin θ = cos θ
  4. sin θ = cos(90° − θ)
Correct answer: D
The table shows the sine of an angle equals the cosine of its complement, so sin θ = cos(90° − θ). Writing sin θ = sin(90° − θ) is the common direction mix-up.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: labelling the same right-angled triangle from corner A and then corner B
Q3. In a right-angled triangle ABC the right angle is at C. Angle A = θ and angle B = 90° − θ. The hypotenuse is c, the side opposite A is a and the side next to A (adjacent to A) is b. What is sin(90° − θ), that is sin B?
  1. b/a
  2. a/c
  3. a/b
  4. b/c
Correct answer: D
From corner B, the side opposite B is b, so sin B = b/c. From corner A, cos θ = b/c, so sin(90° − θ) = cos θ.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Practice (A) – express cos 70° as a sine
Q4. Which expression is equal to cos 70°?
  1. sin 70°
  2. sin 110°
  3. sin 20°
  4. sin 40°
Correct answer: C
Using cos θ = sin(90° − θ): cos 70° = sin(90° − 70°) = sin 20°.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Practice (B) – solve sin(2θ + 10°) = cos(θ + 20°)
Q5. Solve for θ if sin(2θ + 10°) = cos(θ + 20°). Both angles are acute.
  1. θ = 30°
  2. θ = 10°
  3. θ = 50°
  4. θ = 20°
Correct answer: D
Sine equals cosine only when the angles are complementary, so (2θ + 10°) + (θ + 20°) = 90°. Then 3θ = 60° and θ = 20°. Setting the angles equal gives the wrong answer θ = 10°.
Use after: DQB phase — DQB: what today adds to the driving question (sin 50° = cos 40° for the Nakuru mast)
Q6. A mast stands upright on level ground. From a point on the ground the angle of elevation of the top of the mast is 50°. In the right-angled triangle formed by the mast, the ground and the line of sight, the angle at the top of the mast (between the mast and the line of sight) is 40°. Which statement is correct?
  1. sin 50° = tan 40°
  2. sin 50° = cos 40°
  3. sin 50° = cos 50°
  4. sin 50° = sin 40°
Correct answer: B
The 50° and 40° angles are complementary, so sin 50° = cos(90° − 50°) = cos 40°. The same triangle viewed from the top swaps the roles of opposite and adjacent sides.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model Building: annotating the Nakuru mast diagram with the 40° complementary angle
Q7. A Kenya Power engineer could use either the 50° angle at the base or the 40° angle at the top of the mast to describe the same mast height situation. Why is this possible?
  1. The two acute angles add up to 90°, so the sine of one equals the cosine of the other and the ratios use the same sides with swapped roles
  2. The two angles are equal, so all their ratios are the same
  3. Tangent of 50° equals tangent of 40°, so either angle gives the same answer
  4. The hypotenuse is always the same as the height of the mast, so the angle does not matter
Correct answer: A
The acute angles of a right-angled triangle are complementary. The side opposite one angle is adjacent to the other, so sin 50° = cos 40° and cos 50° = sin 40°.
