Q1. A square of side 10 cm has a small square of side 4 cm cut from one corner. What is the area of the L-shaped piece that is left?
  1. 84 cm²
  2. 96 cm²
  3. 36 cm²
  4. 116 cm²
Correct answer: A
The area left is the large square minus the small square: 10² − 4² = 100 − 16 = 84 cm².
Use after: Observe phase — Observe, Steps 3 and 4: cutting the L-shape into two rectangles and joining them into one long rectangle
Q2. An L-shape is made by cutting a square of side b = 4 cm from a corner of a square of side a = 10 cm. It is cut along a straight line into two rectangles (10 cm by 6 cm and 4 cm by 6 cm). The 4 cm by 6 cm rectangle is turned and joined to the other to make one long rectangle. What are the sides of this long rectangle?
  1. 10 cm by 14 cm
  2. 4 cm by 10 cm
  3. 6 cm by 6 cm
  4. 6 cm by 14 cm
Correct answer: D
The width is a − b = 10 − 4 = 6 cm and the length is a + b = 10 + 4 = 14 cm, because the 10 cm and 4 cm sides join end to end. The area 6 × 14 = 84 cm² matches the L-shape.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: writing why the L-shape area equals the rectangle area
Q3. Why does the long rectangle (a − b) by (a + b) have the same area as the L-shape a² − b²?
  1. The same pieces of paper were only rearranged, so the area did not change
  2. The L-shape and the rectangle have the same perimeter, so the areas must be equal
  3. Cutting a shape always makes its area bigger
  4. The rectangle has area a × b, which is the same as a² − b²
Correct answer: A
No paper was added or removed, only moved. Area is conserved when pieces are rearranged, so a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b).
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: mental calculation of 52² − 48² using the identity
Q4. Use a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b) to work out 52² − 48² without a calculator. What is the answer?
  1. 16
  2. 2704
  3. 400
  4. 4
Correct answer: C
52² − 48² = (52 − 48)(52 + 48) = 4 × 100 = 400. Direct check: 2704 − 2304 = 400.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: contrasting cases, is 52² − 48² the same as (52 − 48)²?
Q5. Kamau says, "52² − 48² is the same as (52 − 48)², so the answer is 16." Which statement is correct?
  1. Kamau is right: a² − b² and (a − b)² are always equal
  2. Kamau is wrong: 52² − 48² = 400, but (52 − 48)² = 16, so a² − b² is not the same as (a − b)²
  3. Kamau is right, but only when a and b are both even numbers
  4. Kamau is wrong: 52² − 48² = (52 + 48)² = 10 000
Correct answer: B
52² − 48² = 2704 − 2304 = 400, while (52 − 48)² = 4² = 16. The correct identity is a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b), not (a − b)².
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: linking the identity to the garden numbers, 12² − 9²
Q6. Mama Wanjiru's garden is 9 m wide and 12 m long. Use the identity to find 12² − 9², the area of a 12 m square minus the area of a 9 m square.
  1. 63 m²
  2. 9 m²
  3. 27 m²
  4. 108 m²
Correct answer: A
12² − 9² = (12 − 9)(12 + 9) = 3 × 21 = 63 m². Check: 144 − 81 = 63.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: adding the third panel and identity to the class cumulative model
Q7. Which statement correctly gives the third identity found by cutting a small square from a large square?
  1. a² − b² = (a + b)²
  2. a² − b² = (a − b)²
  3. a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)
  4. a² − b² = a² − 2ab + b²
Correct answer: C
The L-shape a² − b² is rearranged into a rectangle with sides (a − b) and (a + b). The other options are the wrong product or belong to other identities.
Use after: End of lesson — Model: exit ticket, use a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b) to find 63² − 37²
Q8. Use a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b) to find 63² − 37².
  1. 2600
  2. 26
  3. 676
  4. 3600
Correct answer: A
63² − 37² = (63 − 37)(63 + 37) = 26 × 100 = 2600.
