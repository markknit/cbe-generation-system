Q1. Two metal containers are made from exactly the same area of iron sheet. Which statement is correct?
  1. They must hold the same amount of grain because the same amount of metal was used.
  2. The taller container always holds more grain.
  3. They can hold different amounts of grain because the shape of the container also affects its volume.
  4. Volume depends only on how thick the iron sheet is.
Correct answer: C
Surface area is the sheet used to cover a solid, while volume is the space inside. Different shapes with equal surface area can have different volumes, as with Mzee Kiplagat's drum and box.

Q2. A water tank is a cylinder of radius 2.1 m and height 4 m with a hemispherical dome on top and a flat base. Use pi = 22/7. What is the total outer surface area to be covered with sheet (wall + base + curved dome)?
  1. 94.38 m²
  2. 66.66 m²
  3. 108.24 m²
  4. 80.52 m²
Correct answer: A
Wall = 2 x 22/7 x 2.1 x 4 = 52.8 m²; base = 22/7 x 2.1 x 2.1 = 13.86 m²; dome = 2 x 22/7 x 2.1 x 2.1 = 27.72 m². Total = 52.8 + 13.86 + 27.72 = 94.38 m². The circle between the cylinder and dome is inside the tank and is not covered.

Q3. A water tank is a cylinder of radius 2.1 m and height 4 m with a hemisphere of the same radius on top. Use pi = 22/7. How many litres of water can the tank hold when full? (1 m³ = 1,000 litres)
  1. 19,404 litres
  2. 74,844 litres
  3. 74.844 litres
  4. 55,440 litres
Correct answer: B
Cylinder = 22/7 x 2.1 x 2.1 x 4 = 55.44 m³. Hemisphere = 2/3 x 22/7 x 2.1³ = 19.404 m³. Total = 74.844 m³, and 74.844 x 1,000 = 74,844 litres.

Q4. A grain silo has a cone-shaped roof of base radius 2.1 m, vertical height 2.8 m and slant height 3.5 m. Use pi = 22/7. What is the curved surface area of the roof (the sheet needed for the roof)?
  1. 18.48 m²
  2. 12.936 m²
  3. 23.1 m²
  4. 46.2 m²
Correct answer: C
The curved area of a cone uses the slant height: pi x r x l = 22/7 x 2.1 x 3.5 = 23.1 m². The vertical height 2.8 m is used for the volume, not the curved area.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Pair B (Rift Valley silo) volume = cylinder + cone
Q5. A grain silo is a cylinder of radius 2.1 m and height 6 m with a cone roof on top. The cone has the same radius, a vertical height of 2.8 m and a slant height of 3.5 m. Use pi = 22/7. What is the total volume of grain the silo can hold?
  1. 121.968 m³
  2. 96.096 m³
  3. 99.33 m³
  4. 83.16 m³
Correct answer: B
Cylinder = 22/7 x 2.1 x 2.1 x 6 = 83.16 m³. Cone = 1/3 x 22/7 x 2.1 x 2.1 x 2.8 = 12.936 m³, using the vertical height. Total = 83.16 + 12.936 = 96.096 m³.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Question Sort, worked task (ii) painting cost of the tank
Q6. The outside of a water tank has a total surface area of 94.38 m². A painter charges KES 150 per m² and the whole outside is painted. What is the cost of painting?
  1. KES 11,226.60
  2. KES 17,424
  3. KES 12,078
  4. KES 14,157
Correct answer: D
Painting covers the outside, so it depends on surface area: 94.38 x 150 = KES 14,157. The other amounts come from using the silo's area, the tank's volume or a surface area with the base left out.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Question Sort into SURFACE AREA or VOLUME
Q7. Which of these questions about a grain silo needs a VOLUME calculation rather than a surface area calculation?
  1. How much will it cost to paint the outside of the silo?
  2. How much grain can fit inside the silo?
  3. How much iron sheet is needed to make the cone roof?
  4. How much sheet is needed for the flat floor?
Correct answer: B
Grain fills the space inside the silo, so the question is about volume (capacity). Sheet, paint and cost are about covering the outside, which needs surface area.
