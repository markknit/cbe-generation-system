Q1. Wanjiru the metalsmith used exactly 30,800 cm² of iron sheet to make each closed container for Mzee Kiplagat. What does the 30,800 cm² tell us about a container?
  1. The area of iron sheet needed to cover the outside of the container
  2. The space inside the container
  3. How much maize the container can hold
  4. The height of the container
Correct answer: A
30,800 cm² is an area, the amount of sheet covering the outside (surface area). It does not tell how much the container holds.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Task A (rectangular prism net, 5 cm x 4 cm x 3 cm)
Q2. A closed cardboard box measures 5 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm. Its net has 6 rectangular faces. What is the total surface area of the box?
  1. 60 cm²
  2. 47 cm²
  3. 12 cm²
  4. 94 cm²
Correct answer: D
The faces come in 3 equal pairs: 5 x 4 = 20, 5 x 3 = 15, 4 x 3 = 12. TSA = 2(20 + 15 + 12) = 94 cm². The 60 is 5 x 4 x 3, which is not a surface area.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Task B (triangular prism net, 3-4-5 cm ends, length 10 cm)
Q3. A triangular prism has right-angled triangle ends with sides 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm. The prism is 10 cm long. Its net has 2 triangles and 3 rectangles. What is its total surface area?
  1. 60 cm²
  2. 120 cm²
  3. 126 cm²
  4. 132 cm²
Correct answer: D
Each triangle is 1/2 x 3 x 4 = 6 cm², so two give 12 cm². The rectangles are 30 + 40 + 50 = 120 cm². TSA = 12 + 120 = 132 cm². Choosing 120 forgets the triangles, and 126 counts only one triangle.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: looking for a pattern linking the two bases and the rectangles around them
Q4. The 3 rectangles in the net of a triangular prism (ends 3 cm, 4 cm, 5 cm; length 10 cm) are laid side by side to make one long rectangle. What are the dimensions of this long rectangle?
  1. 7 cm by 10 cm
  2. 5 cm by 10 cm
  3. 12 cm by 10 cm
  4. 12 cm by 6 cm
Correct answer: C
The widths 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 cm add up to the perimeter of the triangle, and the height of the strip is the length of the prism, 10 cm.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: applying the formula to Mzee Kiplagat's box (50 cm x 50 cm x 129 cm)
Q5. Mzee Kiplagat's closed box has a square base 50 cm by 50 cm and a height of 129 cm. Using TSA = 2 x base area + perimeter of base x length, what is the total surface area of the box?
  1. 30,800 cm²
  2. 322,500 cm²
  3. 25,800 cm²
  4. 15,400 cm²
Correct answer: A
Base area = 50 x 50 = 2,500 cm², so 2 x base = 5,000 cm². Perimeter = 200 cm, so 200 x 129 = 25,800 cm². TSA = 5,000 + 25,800 = 30,800 cm². The 25,800 leaves out the two bases.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: distinguishing surface area (covering) from volume (space inside)
Q6. The box has a total surface area of 30,800 cm². Does this number tell us how much maize the box can hold?
  1. No, because the box has a different surface area from the drum
  2. Yes, because surface area and volume are the same thing
  3. No, it only tells the area of sheet covering the box; the space inside is the volume, which we have not found yet
  4. Yes, because more sheet metal always means more maize
Correct answer: C
Surface area is the material covering a solid and volume is the space inside. They are different quantities, and the box's volume has not been calculated yet.
Use after: DQB phase — DQB: sorting questions into Surface area, Volume, and Shape and design categories
Q7. On the Driving Question Board, a group is sorting questions into 'Surface area questions' and 'Volume questions'. Which question belongs under 'Volume questions'?
  1. How much maize does the drum hold?
  2. How much iron sheet covers the drum?
  3. What is the perimeter of the box's base?
  4. How many faces are in the net of the box?
Correct answer: A
How much the drum can hold is about the space inside, which is volume. The other questions are about sheet, perimeter or faces, which relate to surface area.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: first draft of the chart 'Why does the drum hold more maize?'
Q8. At the end of this lesson, the class knows the box's surface area is 30,800 cm². Which statement best describes what the class can say about why the drum holds more maize?
  1. It is because the box has a smaller surface area than the drum
  2. It is because the drum uses more iron sheet than the box
  3. It has not been explained yet; we still need the drum's surface area and both volumes
  4. It is because the drum is round
Correct answer: C
This lesson found only the box's surface area. The drum's surface area and the volumes are investigated in later lessons, so the puzzle is still open. The other choices are guesses with no evidence yet.
