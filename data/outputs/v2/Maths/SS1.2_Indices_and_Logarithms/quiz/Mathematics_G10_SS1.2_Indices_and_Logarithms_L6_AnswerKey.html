Use after: Predict phase — Predict: learners predict log(1024 × 8) from the side-by-side worked examples
Q1. A tea farmer is told that log(1024) ≈ 3.0103 and log(8) ≈ 0.9031. Using the product law of logarithms, what is log(1024 × 8)?
  1. 3.9134
  2. 3.0103 × 0.9031 = 2.7186 and then doubled to 5.4372
  3. 0.9031
  4. 2.7186
Correct answer: A
The product law says log(ab) = log a + log b, so log(1024 × 8) = 3.0103 + 0.9031 = 3.9134. Multiplying the logs (2.7186) is the common error.
Use after: Observe phase — Part A: Deriving the Laws (Step 3 of the discovery worksheet)
Q2. Suppose log M = a and log N = b, so M = 10^a and N = 10^b. Then M × N = 10^(a + b). What is log(M × N)?
  1. a − b
  2. a × b
  3. a + b
  4. 10^(a + b)
Correct answer: C
log(M × N) = log(10^(a+b)) = a + b, which equals log M + log N. This is the product law.
Use after: Observe phase — Part A: Deriving the Laws (red flag: confusing log of a product with the product of logs)
Q3. A learner claims that log(M × N) = log M × log N. Test the claim with M = 10 and N = 10, where log 10 = 1. Which statement is correct?
  1. The claim is wrong: the correct law is log(M × N) = log(M + N).
  2. The claim is wrong: log(10 × 10) = log 100 = 2, but log 10 × log 10 = 1. The correct law adds the logs: 1 + 1 = 2.
  3. The claim is correct: both sides equal 2.
  4. The claim is correct: both sides equal 1.
Correct answer: B
log 100 = 2 but 1 × 1 = 1, so multiplying logs fails. Adding the logs (1 + 1 = 2) matches the product law.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: Reading Antilogarithm Tables (guided lookup antilog(2.7482))
Q4. Using the antilogarithm table, what is antilog(2.7482)?
  1. 5 600
  2. 5.600
  3. 560.0
  4. 56.00
Correct answer: C
The mantissa .7482 gives the digits 5600. The characteristic 2 means the answer is in the hundreds, so antilog(2.7482) ≈ 560.0.
Use after: Explain phase — Problem 1 (Multiplication): maize trader at Wakulima Market
Q5. A maize trader at Wakulima Market buys 346 bags, each weighing 91.5 kg. From the tables, log 346 = 2.5391 and log 91.5 = 1.9614. Using logarithms, what is the total mass of maize?
  1. About 3 166 kg
  2. About 4.5005 kg
  3. About 31 660 kg
  4. About 316 600 kg
Correct answer: C
Add the logs: 2.5391 + 1.9614 = 4.5005. The antilog of 4.5005 is about 31 660, and the characteristic 4 puts it in the tens of thousands.
Use after: Explain phase — Problem 2 (Division): fertiliser depot in Nakuru
Q6. A fertiliser depot in Nakuru shares 7 820 kg of CAN fertiliser equally among 34 farms. From the tables, log 7820 = 3.8932 and log 34 = 1.5315. Using logarithms, how much does each farm receive?
  1. About 2 300 kg
  2. About 23 kg
  3. About 230 kg
  4. About 5.4247 kg
Correct answer: C
Division means subtracting the logs: 3.8932 − 1.5315 = 2.3617. The antilog of 2.3617 is about 230, and the characteristic 2 gives hundreds.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model Building: adding the multiplication chain (multiply → log → add → antilog) to the concept map
Q7. Which list gives the correct order of steps for multiplying two large numbers using logarithm tables?
  1. Multiply the numbers, then find the log of the answer, then add 1
  2. Find the log of each number, add the logs, and stop because the sum is the product
  3. Find the log of each number, add the logs, then use the antilog table to get the product
  4. Find the log of each number, multiply the logs, then use the antilog table to get the product
Correct answer: C
The chain is: look up each log, add the logs, then convert back with the antilog table. Leaving out the antilog step is a common gap.
Use after: End of lesson — Extension / Take-home (Problem 3, Combined): annual tea revenue for a Kericho estate
Q8. A Kericho tea estate produces 2 480 kg of tea per week for 52 weeks. The tea is sold at Ksh 118 per kg. Using logarithms, log 2480 = 3.3945, log 52 = 1.7160 and log 118 = 2.0719. What is the annual revenue?
  1. About Ksh 15 220 000
  2. About Ksh 1 522 000
  3. About Ksh 7.1824
  4. About Ksh 152 200 000
Correct answer: A
There are three factors, so add three logs: 3.3945 + 1.7160 + 2.0719 = 7.1824. The antilog of 7.1824 is about 15 220 000, and the characteristic 7 gives an 8-digit number.
