Q1. A plane mirror forms an image of a lorry that is 8 m long. How long is the image of the lorry?
  1. 8 m, because the image is the same size as the object
  2. It depends on how far the lorry is from the mirror
  3. 4 m, because images in a mirror are smaller
  4. 16 m, because the image is behind the mirror as well
Correct answer: A
Property I: a plane mirror image is the same size as the object, so the image is also 8 m long. Objects that are far away only look smaller to the eye. The image does not actually change size.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Step 4, measuring the six distances and recording them in the results table
Q2. In the matatu arrow investigation, a group measures the distance from point A to the mirror line and gets 35 mm. What distance should they measure from the image point A' to the mirror line?
  1. 17 mm
  2. 35 mm
  3. 0 mm
  4. 70 mm
Correct answer: B
Property II: an object point and its image point are equidistant from the mirror line, so A' is also 35 mm from it, within about 2 mm of measurement error.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Step 5, using a set square to check whether AA', BB' and MM' are perpendicular to the mirror line
Q3. A group joins A to its image point A' with a straight line and uses a set square to check the angle where AA' meets the mirror line. What result should they record?
  1. 90°
  2. 45°
  3. 60°
  4. 180°
Correct answer: A
Property III: the line joining an object point to its image point meets the mirror line at 90°, so AA' is perpendicular to the mirror line.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: tracing paper fold along the mirror line to check that the object and image coincide
Q4. A group folds tracing paper along the mirror line and the object arrow lands exactly on top of the image arrow. Which mathematical word describes two shapes that match exactly like this?
  1. Congruent
  2. Enlarged
  3. Parallel
  4. Similar but larger
Correct answer: A
Shapes that have exactly the same size and shape are congruent. The exact fit when folding shows that the image is congruent to the object.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: whole-class consolidation of Property II (equidistance), using the lorry 20 metres in front of the mirror glass
Q5. A lorry is 20 m in front of the glass of a plane mirror. The image appears as far behind the glass as the lorry is in front of it. How far apart are the lorry and its image?
  1. 40 m
  2. 0 m
  3. 10 m
  4. 20 m
Correct answer: A
The image is 20 m behind the glass because object and image distances are equal. The lorry and its image are 20 + 20 = 40 m apart.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Property IV (lateral inversion) with the reversed AMBULANCE card held up to the plane mirror
Q6. The word AMBULANCE is painted back to front on the front of an ambulance. Why is this done?
  1. A plane mirror reverses left and right, so a driver reads the word correctly in the rear-view mirror
  2. A plane mirror makes images smaller, so the word must be painted larger
  3. The word is reversed so that the image is not congruent to the object
  4. A plane mirror makes the image appear closer, so the word must be painted back to front
Correct answer: A
Property IV: the image in a plane mirror is laterally inverted (reversed left to right). Reversed letters therefore read correctly in the mirror image.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model Building: annotating the class diagram with the perpendicular bisector caption, the equal-distance arrows and the square symbol at the point where AA' meets the mirror line
Q7. On a diagram, the segment AA' joining an object point A to its image point A' is 60 mm long. The mirror line is the perpendicular bisector of AA'. Where does AA' meet the mirror line?
  1. 30 mm from A, at an angle of 90°
  2. 30 mm from A, at an angle of 45°
  3. 60 mm from A, at an angle of 90°
  4. 20 mm from A, at an angle of 45°
Correct answer: A
A bisector cuts the segment in half, so the meeting point is 60 ÷ 2 = 30 mm from A. A perpendicular bisector also meets the segment at 90°.
