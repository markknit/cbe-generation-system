Q1. A school plan is drawn to Scale 1 : 200. A classroom block measures 4 cm long on the plan. What is the real length of the block?
  1. 8 m
  2. 80 m
  3. 0.8 m
  4. 0.02 m
Correct answer: A
Real length = 4 cm × 200 = 800 cm = 8 m. Learners who divide by 200 get 0.02 cm, and those who forget to convert from cm to m get 800.
Use after: Predict phase — Predict: part (b), real area of the football pitch from the plan
Q2. On a plan drawn to Scale 1 : 200, a football pitch measures 25 cm by 15 cm. What is the real area of the pitch?
  1. 1 500 m²
  2. 7.5 m²
  3. 15 000 m²
  4. 150 m²
Correct answer: A
Plan area = 25 × 15 = 375 cm². A.S.F = 200² = 40 000, so real area = 375 × 40 000 = 15 000 000 cm² = 1 500 m². Equivalent check: the real pitch is 50 m by 30 m.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Card A, Survey of Kenya map (1 : 50 000, Kisumu to Ahero), real area of the rice paddy
Q3. On a Survey of Kenya map drawn to Scale 1 : 50 000, a rectangular rice paddy measures 2 cm by 3 cm. What is the real area of the paddy? (Hint: 1 cm on this map is 500 m in real life.)
  1. 15 km²
  2. 0.75 km²
  3. 1.5 km²
  4. 3 km²
Correct answer: C
Map area = 2 × 3 = 6 cm². 1 cm² on this map is 0.25 km², since 1 cm is 0.5 km. Real area = 6 × 0.25 = 1.5 km². Using the linear scale factor on an area, rather than squaring it, gives the wrong answer of 3 km².
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Card B, architectural plan, real volume of the square water tank and number of 20-litre jerricans
Q4. A square water tank on a plan drawn to Scale 1 : 200 has a 3 cm × 3 cm base and a height of 2 cm on the plan. How many 20-litre jerricans can the full tank fill? (1 m³ = 1 000 litres)
  1. 7 200
  2. 360
  3. 72 000
  4. 720
Correct answer: A
Real size is 6 m × 6 m × 4 m, so the volume is 144 m³ = 144 000 litres. Dividing by 20 gives 7 200 jerricans. Equivalently, V.S.F = 200³ = 8 000 000 and 18 cm³ × 8 000 000 = 144 m³.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Card C, packaging design, mandazi box scaled by L.S.F = 1.5
Q5. A mandazi factory in Eldoret enlarges its box design with a length scale factor of 1.5. The original box needs 600 cm² of cardboard. How much cardboard does the new box need?
  1. 900 cm²
  2. 1 350 cm²
  3. 2 025 cm²
  4. 1 800 cm²
Correct answer: B
Surface area scales by A.S.F = k² = 1.5² = 2.25, so the new area is 600 × 2.25 = 1 350 cm². Using 1.5 gives 900, and using 1.5³ gives 2 025.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Synthesis Question 1, the two water tanks (2×, 4× or 8×)
Q6. Two water tanks have exactly the same shape. The second tank is twice as tall as the first. How many times as much water does the second tank hold?
  1. 8 times
  2. 2 times
  3. 6 times
  4. 4 times
Correct answer: A
Volume scales by V.S.F = k³ = 2³ = 8. Doubling the length does not double the volume. Many learners say 2 or 4 because they use k or k².
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Synthesis Question 2 (homework), Eldoret maize silo model and real curved surface area
Q7. A scale model of a maize silo has a length scale of 1 : 30. The model's curved surface area is 50 cm². What is the curved surface area of the real silo?
  1. 135 m²
  2. 0.15 m²
  3. 45 m²
  4. 4.5 m²
Correct answer: D
Area uses A.S.F = 30² = 900, so real area = 50 × 900 = 45 000 cm² = 4.5 m². Multiplying by 30 gives 0.15 m², and cubing 30 gives 135 m².
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: Project Planning Sheet calculation table (L.S.F, A.S.F, V.S.F and expected volume of Model 2)
Q8. A group plans two similar cuboid models using a length scale factor k = 2. Model 1 has a volume of 50 cm³. What is the expected volume of Model 2?
  1. 400 cm³
  2. 200 cm³
  3. 300 cm³
  4. 100 cm³
Correct answer: A
Volume scales by V.S.F = k³ = 2³ = 8, so Model 2 has volume 50 × 8 = 400 cm³. Using k gives 100 and using k² gives 200.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: group planning check on A.S.F, with the cylinder example (radius 3 cm, k = 2)
Q9. A group builds two similar cylinders. The smaller has radius 3 cm and the larger is made with a length scale factor k = 2. What is the ratio of the curved surface area of the smaller cylinder to that of the larger cylinder?
  1. 1 : 6
  2. 1 : 2
  3. 1 : 4
  4. 1 : 8
Correct answer: C
The larger radius is 6 cm. Curved surface area scales by k² = 4, so the ratio is 1 : 4. Using 1 : 2 confuses area with length, and 1 : 8 confuses area with volume.
