Q1. A quadrilateral ABCD is enlarged to give a bigger quadrilateral A'B'C'D'. A learner draws straight lines through A and A', B and B', C and C', and D and D', extending each line far in both directions. What will the learner find?
  1. The lines from A and B meet at one point, and the lines from C and D meet at a different point
  2. The four lines all meet at one single point
  3. The four lines go in different directions and never meet
  4. The four lines are parallel to each other
Correct answer: B
In an enlargement, every line through a vertex and its image passes through the same fixed point, the centre of enlargement. Learners who predicted divergence or separate meeting points should revisit the construction.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: measuring corresponding sides and calculating image ÷ object for each pair
Q2. A side PQ of a triangle measures 4 cm. In its enlargement, the matching side P'Q' measures 10 cm. What is the linear scale factor (L.S.F)?
  1. 40
  2. 0.4
  3. 6
  4. 2.5
Correct answer: D
L.S.F = image length ÷ object length = 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5. Dividing the other way round (4 ÷ 10 = 0.4) is a common reversal.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: measuring OA, OA', OB, OB' and comparing these ratios with the side ratios
Q3. O is the centre of enlargement. Point A is 3 cm from O and its image A' is 6 cm from O. A side AB of the object is 4 cm long. How long is the matching side A'B' of the image?
  1. 7 cm
  2. 8 cm
  3. 2 cm
  4. 12 cm
Correct answer: B
L.S.F = OA' ÷ OA = 6 ÷ 3 = 2, and the same scale factor applies to every length. So A'B' = 4 × 2 = 8 cm.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: writing and agreeing on a class definition of 'centre of enlargement'
Q4. Which statement best describes the centre of enlargement?
  1. The vertex of the object that is closest to the image
  2. The point where the diagonals of the enlarged image cross
  3. The middle point of the object shape
  4. The single fixed point through which all ray lines joining corresponding vertices of the object and its image pass
Correct answer: D
The centre of enlargement is the one fixed point where all lines through corresponding vertices of the object and image meet. It is not necessarily inside either shape.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: applying L.S.F = image length ÷ object length to the 10 m classroom block on the Kisumu school plan
Q5. On a school plan, a classroom block that is 10 m wide in real life measures 5 cm. What is the L.S.F from the real building to the plan?
  1. 200
  2. 1/200
  3. 1/2000
  4. 1/20
Correct answer: B
Change to the same units: 10 m = 1000 cm. L.S.F = 5 ÷ 1000 = 1/200. It is less than 1 because the plan is smaller than the building.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: practice problem (a), the football pitch on the 1:200 plan
Q6. A football pitch is 90 m long. It is drawn on a plan with scale 1 : 200 (L.S.F = 1/200). What length does the pitch have on the plan?
  1. 0.45 cm
  2. 450 cm
  3. 45 cm
  4. 18 000 cm
Correct answer: C
90 m = 9000 cm. Plan length = 9000 × 1/200 = 45 cm. Multiplying by 200 instead gives the wrong, much larger length.
Use after: End of lesson — Explain: practice problem (b), the door on the plan (homework), used as an end-of-lesson check
Q7. A door on a plan with scale 1 : 200 measures 0.45 cm wide. How wide is the real door in metres?
  1. 0.00225 m
  2. 9 m
  3. 0.9 m
  4. 90 m
Correct answer: C
Real width = plan width ÷ L.S.F = 0.45 × 200 = 90 cm = 0.9 m. The real door must be bigger than its drawing, so the answer must be more than 0.45 cm.
