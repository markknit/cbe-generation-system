Use after: Explain phase — Explain: groups draft and the class agrees one class definition of Chemistry
Q1. After comparing their drafts, a class in Kisumu agrees on one definition of Chemistry using evidence from their tray of substances. Which definition did they agree on?
  1. Chemistry is the science that studies living things and how they grow.
  2. Chemistry is the science that studies only dangerous liquids used in laboratories.
  3. Chemistry is the science that studies matter: its composition, properties, changes and uses.
  4. Chemistry is the science that studies forces, motion and energy.
Correct answer: C
All four columns of the evidence chart (composition, properties, changes, uses) describe matter, so Chemistry is the science that studies matter and these four aspects. The other options describe a misconception or other sciences.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: four-column evidence chart (Composition, Properties, Changes, Uses)
Q2. A group fills in a four-column evidence chart with the headings Composition, Properties, Changes and Uses. Which entry belongs in the Changes column?
  1. Sulphur burns with a blue flame and gives a sharp, choking-smelling gas.
  2. Sand sinks in water and stays visible.
  3. Iron filings are pulled out of a mixture by a magnet.
  4. Paracetamol tablets are used to relieve pain and fever.
Correct answer: A
Burning sulphur forms a new substance (sulphur dioxide), so it is a change. Magnetism and sinking are properties, and relieving pain is a use.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: mixture M data (9 g in, 8.4 g recovered)
Q3. In the tray investigation, mixture M of iron filings, sand and salt had a mass of 9 g. After separating it with a magnet, filtration and evaporation, the learners recovered 2.9 g of iron, 2.8 g of sand and 2.7 g of salt. What mass was lost during the separation?
  1. 0.5 g
  2. 1.1 g
  3. 0.6 g
  4. 8.4 g
Correct answer: C
Mass recovered = 2.9 + 2.8 + 2.7 = 8.4 g. Mass lost = 9 - 8.4 = 0.6 g.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: challenge case (a) why salt dissolves but sand does not
Q4. Learners test the definition 'Chemistry is the science that studies matter: its composition, properties, changes and uses' on this question: why does table salt dissolve in water but sand does not? Which answer uses the definition correctly?
  1. Sand is not matter, so it cannot dissolve.
  2. Both dissolve if stirred long enough, so the difference is only in the stirring.
  3. Salt and sand are different substances with different properties (such as solubility) that come from what each is made of.
  4. Sand is heavier than salt, and all light solids dissolve in water.
Correct answer: C
Each substance has its own properties, such as solubility, which come from its composition. Sand is matter, and in the tray test it did not dissolve however it was mixed.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: challenge case (c) Wanjiru's question about antacid F and paracetamol G
Q5. Wanjiru asks why an antacid tablet relieves heartburn while a paracetamol tablet relieves pain. The antacid fizzes in dilute vinegar, but paracetamol does not. Which explanation is correct?
  1. The antacid reacts with and neutralises excess stomach acid, while paracetamol relieves pain and fever by a different action and does not neutralise acid.
  2. Both tablets neutralise acid, but paracetamol does it more slowly.
  3. Paracetamol relieves heartburn by reacting with stomach acid, and the antacid relieves pain by melting in the body.
  4. The antacid is matter but paracetamol is not, so they act differently.
Correct answer: A
Fizzing in vinegar shows the antacid reacts with acid, which is why it relieves heartburn. Paracetamol does not fizz and does not neutralise acid; it relieves pain and fever in a different way.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: why taking too much of either medicine is dangerous (paracetamol maximum 4 g in 24 hours)
Q6. A paracetamol label says an adult may take a maximum of 8 tablets of 500 mg each in 24 hours. What is this maximum in grams? (1 g = 1000 mg)
  1. 1.6 g
  2. 0.4 g
  3. 4 g
  4. 40 g
Correct answer: C
8 tablets x 500 mg = 4000 mg. Dividing by 1000 gives 4 g. Taking more can damage the liver.
Use after: End of lesson — Model Building: exit ticket on the class definition of Chemistry and tray evidence
Q7. A learner says, 'Chemistry only studies dangerous liquids in a laboratory.' Which is the best reply based on the class definition of Chemistry?
  1. She is wrong, because Chemistry only studies medicines.
  2. Chemistry studies all matter, including ugali, tea from Kericho and the water in Lake Victoria.
  3. She is right, because Chemistry cannot study solids such as sand or sugar.
  4. She is right, because chemists only work with dangerous liquids.
Correct answer: B
Chemistry studies the composition, properties, changes and uses of all matter, not only dangerous liquids or laboratory work, and not only medicines.
