Use after: Predict phase — Predict: individual and group predictions on what happens if a person takes far more tablets than the label says
Q1. Wanjiru has a bad headache. She says, "If the label says 2 paracetamol tablets, I will take 8 at once so the pain goes away faster." What is the best response?
  1. Paracetamol cannot harm the body, because it is a medicine.
  2. Taking far more than the label says can damage the liver, so she should follow the label.
  3. She is right, because more tablets always mean faster relief.
  4. Eight tablets is safe if she drinks a lot of water.
Correct answer: B
More is not always better. Paracetamol taken above the label dose can damage the liver, so the label dose must be followed.
Use after: Observe phase — Part A: teacher demo of F and G in dilute vinegar
Q2. A teacher drops an antacid tablet F into dilute vinegar (an acid) and it fizzes. Paracetamol tablet G in dilute vinegar does not fizz. What does this evidence suggest?
  1. G neutralises stomach acid better than F.
  2. Fizzing shows that F is poisonous and G is safe.
  3. F reacts with acid, so it can neutralise excess stomach acid, while G does not react with acid.
  4. F and G are the same substance because both are white tablets.
Correct answer: C
F fizzes with acid, so it reacts with acid and can neutralise excess stomach acid. G does not react, so it does not work by neutralising acid. It relieves pain by a different action.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: data card and calculation of the maximum daily mass of paracetamol
Q3. A paracetamol label says: "500 mg per tablet. Maximum 8 tablets in 24 hours." What is the maximum mass of paracetamol an adult may take in 24 hours, in grams? (1000 mg = 1 g)
  1. 40 g
  2. 4 g
  3. 400 g
  4. 0.4 g
Correct answer: B
8 x 500 mg = 4000 mg. Since 1000 mg = 1 g, 4000 mg = 4 g.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: calculation of tablets taken at 2 tablets every 4 hours in 24 hours
Q4. A paracetamol tablet contains 500 mg. The label maximum is 8 tablets (4000 mg) in 24 hours. A person takes 2 tablets every 4 hours for the whole 24 hours. By how many mg does this go over the label maximum?
  1. 2000 mg
  2. 6000 mg
  3. 1000 mg
  4. 4000 mg
Correct answer: A
There are 24 ÷ 4 = 6 doses, so 6 x 2 = 12 tablets, which is 12 x 500 = 6000 mg. The excess is 6000 - 4000 = 2000 mg.
Use after: Explain phase — Building the group explanation on why too much of either tablet is dangerous (data card facts on antacid and paracetamol)
Q5. A person takes antacid tablets all day after every meal for several weeks. What is the most likely effect of this antacid overuse?
  1. Liver damage, as with too much paracetamol
  2. No effect, because antacids are always harmless
  3. Upset body salts, with constipation or diarrhoea
  4. Faster relief from pain and fever
Correct answer: C
Antacid taken too often can upset the body's salts and cause constipation or diarrhoea. Liver damage is the effect of paracetamol overdose.
Use after: Explain phase — Sorting the six Kenyan scenario cards into correct use or misuse (key terms: drug, prescription, dosage, substance abuse)
Q6. A learner takes 10 paracetamol tablets of 500 mg in one day because a headache is severe. The label maximum is 8 tablets in 24 hours. Which statement is correct?
  1. This is following a prescription, because paracetamol is a medicine.
  2. This is correct use, because the learner is only taking tablets and not alcohol.
  3. This is substance abuse, because the amount is harmful and above the label maximum.
  4. This is correct use, because a severe headache needs more medicine.
Correct answer: C
Substance abuse means using a substance in a harmful way or amount. 10 tablets is 5000 mg, which is more than the 4000 mg maximum and can damage the liver.
Use after: Explain phase — Effects of substance abuse: fact cards on alcohol, miraa, bhang and tobacco
Q7. Which statement about alcohol, miraa (khat), bhang (cannabis) and tobacco for school-age learners is correct?
  1. A small amount is a safe dose for a learner.
  2. They only affect the body, not learning or safety.
  3. They are safe if a friend gives them to you.
  4. They are not medicines for learners, and there is no safe dose for a learner.
Correct answer: D
These substances are not medicines for learners. They can harm the body and mind, learning, safety, family and community, and there is no safe dose for a learner.
Use after: Model Building phase — Part A: model revision, adding a section on safe use to class model v2
Q8. A group adds a safe-use section to the class model about medicines like antacid F and paracetamol G. Which statement should it include?
  1. Properties decide what a medicine does, and the dose decides whether it helps or harms.
  2. The more tablets a person takes, the faster the medicine works.
  3. Tablets that look the same work in the same way, so the dose does not matter.
  4. A medicine is safe at any dose if it is bought from a shop.
Correct answer: A
The model links properties to how a medicine works, and dose to safety. Labels, prescriptions and correct dosage keep people safe.
