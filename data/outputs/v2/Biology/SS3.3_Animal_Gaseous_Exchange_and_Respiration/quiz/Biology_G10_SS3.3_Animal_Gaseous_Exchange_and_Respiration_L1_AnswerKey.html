Q1. During the lesson, learners counted their pulse using the radial artery. Where should you place your fingers to find this pulse?
  1. Two fingers on the outside of the elbow
  2. One hand flat on the chest, over the ribs
  3. Two fingers (index and middle) on the inside of the wrist, below the thumb
  4. Your thumb pressed hard on the side of the neck
Correct answer: C
The radial pulse is found with the index and middle fingers pressed gently on the inside of the wrist below the thumb. A flat hand on the chest was used for counting breaths, not pulse.
Use after: Predict phase — Predict: posting resting breathing and pulse rates on the class data table
Q2. In a Grade 10 class, most learners had resting breathing rates between 12 and 20 breaths per minute and resting pulse rates between 60 and 90 beats per minute. Which pair of resting values fits this class pattern?
  1. 6 breaths per minute and 30 beats per minute
  2. 16 breaths per minute and 76 beats per minute
  3. 16 breaths per minute and 150 beats per minute
  4. 40 breaths per minute and 76 beats per minute
Correct answer: B
Only 16 breaths per minute and 76 beats per minute lie inside both class ranges (12–20 and 60–90). The other pairs have at least one value far outside the range.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: 2 minutes of step-ups, then re-measuring pulse rate and comparing with the resting value
Q3. Before exercise, a learner's resting pulse was 80 beats per minute. Immediately after 2 minutes of vigorous step-ups it was 128 beats per minute. By what percentage did the pulse rate increase?
  1. 160%
  2. 37.5%
  3. 48%
  4. 60%
Correct answer: D
Increase = 128 − 80 = 48 beats per minute. Percentage increase = 48 ÷ 80 × 100 = 60%. This is within the 50–80% rise seen in the class.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: whole-class data table analysis of resting and post-exercise values
Q4. The class posted resting and post-exercise values for every learner on the board after the 2 minutes of step-ups. Which statement best describes the pattern in the whole-class data?
  1. Both rates rose for every learner, but by different amounts
  2. Pulse rate rose for everyone, but breathing rate stayed the same
  3. Breathing rate rose for everyone, but pulse rate stayed the same
  4. Both rates rose by exactly the same amount for every learner
Correct answer: A
The class data showed that both breathing rate and pulse rate increased for everyone, which makes it a pattern. The size of the increase differed from learner to learner.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: comparing written predictions with actual observations in groups of four
Q5. Wanjiru predicted her breathing rate after the step-ups would be 26 breaths per minute. She then measured 30 breaths per minute. By how many breaths per minute was her prediction different from her measurement?
  1. 30
  2. 4
  3. 2
  4. 56
Correct answer: B
The difference between measured and predicted values is 30 − 26 = 4 breaths per minute, so her prediction was close.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: groups propose explanations, which the teacher records without judgment (the BURNING clue)
Q6. After the step-ups, some learners felt a burning sensation in their thigh muscles even though they were breathing hard the whole time. At this point in the unit, which statement is best supported by what the class has done so far?
  1. The burning only happens to learners who did not breathe hard
  2. The burning proves that the heart is too weak to pump blood
  3. The class has observed the burning but has not yet explained it, so it becomes a question to investigate
  4. The burning has been proven to be caused by tired lungs
Correct answer: C
The class has only observed the burning sensation and has not yet gathered evidence to explain it. The sensible next step is to record it as a question on the Driving Question Board and investigate it in later lessons.
Use after: DQB phase — DQB: writing one 'biggest unanswered question' and posting it on the Driving Question Board
Q7. Which of these would make the best question to post on the Driving Question Board after the step-ups activity?
  1. What colour sticky notes did we use?
  2. What is the name of the wrist artery used for pulse?
  3. How many learners are in our class?
  4. Why did my legs burn even though I was breathing in plenty of air?
Correct answer: D
A good Driving Question Board question is a genuine 'Why' or 'How' question about the phenomenon, not a recall or routine question. Only the question about the burning legs asks about a puzzle the class observed.
