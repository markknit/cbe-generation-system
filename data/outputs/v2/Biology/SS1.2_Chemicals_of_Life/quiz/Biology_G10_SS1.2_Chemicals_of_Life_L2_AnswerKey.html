Use after: Predict phase — Predict: learners list the chemicals they think are in each food and compare in pairs
Q1. Wanjiru's lunch includes ugali made from maize flour. A learner says, "Ugali is just one chemical." Which statement is correct?
  1. Ugali is made of many chemicals, mainly starch (a carbohydrate) together with water and small amounts of other substances.
  2. Ugali is one chemical called starch and nothing else.
  3. Ugali contains no chemicals because it is a natural food.
  4. Ugali contains only minerals such as iron and calcium.
Correct answer: A
Foods are mixtures of many chemicals. Ugali is mostly starch, a carbohydrate, plus water and other substances, so the idea that a food is only one chemical is a misconception.
Use after: Observe phase — Station A: Nutrient data (meal nutrient approximations)
Q2. A meal card for Wanjiru's lunch says: ugali about 70 g carbohydrate; beans about 23 g carbohydrate and 9 g protein; cooking oil about 10 g fat. How much carbohydrate do the ugali and the beans give together?
  1. 102 g
  2. 79 g
  3. 80 g
  4. 93 g
Correct answer: D
Add only the carbohydrate amounts: 70 g + 23 g = 93 g. The 9 g of protein and the 10 g of fat are not carbohydrate.
Use after: Observe phase — Station C: Burn test demonstration
Q3. Mr Otieno holds a small piece of dry maize flour in a flame and it turns black. He then holds table salt in the flame and it does not turn black. What does the black residue from the maize flour show?
  1. The maize flour contains mineral salts such as sodium.
  2. The maize flour contains iron.
  3. The maize flour contains water only.
  4. The maize flour contains carbon.
Correct answer: D
Charring leaves black carbon, so the maize flour contains carbon. Salt does not blacken because it contains no carbon, which supports the idea that organic compounds contain carbon.
Use after: Observe phase — Station B: Element cards (matching elements to foods in the meal)
Q4. Starch, found in ugali, is made of carbon, hydrogen and oxygen. Protein, found in beans, milk and nyama choma, contains those elements and always one more. Which element is it?
  1. Calcium (Ca)
  2. Nitrogen (N)
  3. Sodium (Na)
  4. Iron (Fe)
Correct answer: B
Proteins contain nitrogen as well as carbon, hydrogen and oxygen. Calcium, iron and sodium are mineral elements found in mineral salts, not part of every protein.
Use after: Explain phase — Classification Chart: sorting nutrients into Organic and Inorganic columns
Q5. On the classification chart, water is placed in the Inorganic column even though every living thing contains it. Why?
  1. Water is a liquid, and organic compounds must be solids.
  2. Water contains hydrogen and oxygen but no carbon, so it has no carbon bonded to hydrogen.
  3. Water contains no hydrogen.
  4. Water is not found in foods such as milk and sukuma wiki.
Correct answer: B
Organic compounds contain carbon bonded to hydrogen. Water (H and O only) has no carbon, so it is inorganic, even though living things need it.
Use after: Explain phase — Claim-Evidence-Reasoning statement: the chemical most likely to cause the glucose rise
Q6. A learner writes a claim about the chemical in Wanjiru's lunch most likely to cause her blood glucose to rise. The meal data show: ugali about 70 g carbohydrate; beans about 23 g carbohydrate and 9 g protein; cooking oil about 10 g fat. Which claim is best supported by this evidence?
  1. Protein, because beans contain protein.
  2. Carbohydrate, because the meal contains much more carbohydrate (70 g in the ugali alone) than protein or fat.
  3. Fat, because cooking oil is used to cook the meal.
  4. Mineral salts, because sukuma wiki contains iron and calcium.
Correct answer: B
The largest amount in the meal is carbohydrate, with 70 g in the ugali alone, so it is the best-supported suspect. The other groups are present in much smaller amounts, and the lesson gives no evidence linking them to the glucose rise.
Use after: End of lesson — Exit slip after the class model: naming organic and inorganic chemicals in Wanjiru's meal
Q7. Which pair of substances from Wanjiru's lunch are both inorganic?
  1. Starch (from ugali) and water
  2. Vitamin C (from sukuma wiki) and iron salts
  3. Water and calcium salts (from milk)
  4. Protein (from beans) and calcium salts
Correct answer: C
Water and mineral salts such as calcium salts are inorganic because they do not contain carbon bonded to hydrogen. Vitamins, starch and proteins are organic.
