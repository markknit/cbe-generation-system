Q1. An amoeba is about 0.1 mm across. A cluster of human leg muscle cells lies several centimetres deep below the skin. For which one is diffusion alone enough to supply oxygen, and why?
  1. The amoeba, because oxygen only has to diffuse a very short distance to reach all parts of the cell
  2. Both, because diffusion works at the same speed over any distance
  3. Neither, because every living thing needs a heart to move oxygen
  4. The muscle cells, because they are made of many cells and so have more surface area in total
Correct answer: A
Diffusion is fast over very short distances, so a tiny amoeba can be supplied directly from its surroundings. Cells several centimetres below the skin are too far away for diffusion to supply them in time.
Use after: Observe phase — Activity A: SA:V ratio modelling with 1 cm, 2 cm and 3 cm cubes
Q2. In Activity A, a group makes a cube with sides of 3 cm. Using Surface Area = 6 × side² and Volume = side³, what is the SA:V ratio of this cube?
  1. 6
  2. 2
  3. 18
  4. 0.5
Correct answer: B
Surface area = 6 × 3² = 54 cm². Volume = 3³ = 27 cm³. SA:V = 54 ÷ 27 = 2.
Use after: Observe phase — Activity A: SA:V ratio modelling ('As the cube gets bigger, what happens to the SA:V ratio?')
Q3. A 1 cm cube has an SA:V ratio of 6. A 3 cm cube has an SA:V ratio of 2. What does this suggest about a large animal compared with a small one?
  1. The two animals have the same SA:V ratio, so both can rely on diffusion
  2. The large animal has less surface area for each unit of volume, so diffusion alone cannot supply all its cells
  3. The large animal has more surface area for each unit of volume, so diffusion is faster in it
  4. The large animal has a smaller volume, so its cells are closer to the surface
Correct answer: B
As size increases, volume grows faster than surface area, so SA:V falls. A large body has less surface for each unit of volume, and many of its cells are far from the surface, so it needs a transport system.
Use after: Observe phase — Activity B: Pulse rate and breathing rate measurement (count for 30 seconds and multiply by 2)
Q4. In Activity B, a learner counts 45 pulse beats in 30 seconds immediately after star jumps. What is the pulse rate in beats per minute (BPM)?
  1. 75 BPM
  2. 45 BPM
  3. 90 BPM
  4. 22.5 BPM
Correct answer: C
The count was for 30 seconds, so multiply by 2 to get beats per minute: 45 × 2 = 90 BPM.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: CER discussion, 'how long would oxygen take to diffuse from his skin to that muscle cell?'
Q5. Kipchoge's quadriceps muscles are about 8 cm below the skin. Why could oxygen NOT reach these muscle cells by diffusion alone from the skin during a marathon?
  1. The skin is waterproof, so no oxygen can ever enter the body
  2. Diffusion only moves carbon dioxide, not oxygen
  3. The muscles are pumping too hard for oxygen to enter them
  4. Diffusion time increases greatly with distance, so it would take hours instead of the seconds the muscles need
Correct answer: D
Diffusion time grows with the square of the distance. It is fast over fractions of a millimetre but takes many hours across several centimetres, far too slow for working muscle.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: annotating the body-outline diagram linking each transport function to a Kipchoge marathon scenario
Q6. At km 30 of a marathon, Kipchoge's leg muscles release a lot of heat. His blood carries this heat to other parts of the body so he does not overheat. Which transport function is this?
  1. Delivery of nutrients
  2. Immune defence
  3. Transport of hormones
  4. Thermoregulation (redistributing heat)
Correct answer: D
Moving heat made by working muscles around the body so that it can be lost is thermoregulation, one of the five transport functions.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: cumulative model revision, sentence stem 'Without a transport system, Kipchoge's muscle cells would ________ because ________'
Q7. Complete the sentence: 'Without a transport system, Kipchoge's muscle cells would ________ because ________.'
  1. stop needing oxygen, because a large body has a high SA:V ratio
  2. run out of oxygen and glucose and build up CO₂, because diffusion is too slow over the large distance from the skin
  3. work normally, because the heart only pumps harder and is not needed to move substances
  4. get too much oxygen, because diffusion is fast over long distances
Correct answer: B
Deep muscle cells need a constant supply of oxygen and glucose and removal of CO₂. Diffusion over a large distance is too slow to do this, so a transport system is needed.
