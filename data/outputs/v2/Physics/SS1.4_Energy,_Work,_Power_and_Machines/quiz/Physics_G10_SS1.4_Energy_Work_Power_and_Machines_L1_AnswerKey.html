Q1. Mama Kavata lifts a full 20 kg bucket from the water at the bottom of an 8 m deep well to the ground at the top. What happens to the energy stored in the bucket because of its position as it is lifted?
  1. It increases, because the bucket is higher
  2. It stays the same, because the bucket is the same bucket
  3. It decreases, because the bucket is moving away from the water
  4. It is zero all the time, because the bucket is not falling
Correct answer: A
The higher the bucket is lifted, the more energy it stores because of its position. This is the prediction the lesson aims to test against the evidence.
Use after: Predict phase — Predict: question 'Will the energy gained be different for Mama Kavata and for Mwende?' and the prediction tally on the board
Q2. Mama Kavata lifts the 20 kg bucket 8 m by hand and finds it exhausting. Her daughter Mwende raises the same bucket the same 8 m using the pulley system and finds it easy. Which statement about the bucket at the top of the well is correct?
  1. The bucket has no stored energy for Mwende because she did not lift it by hand
  2. The bucket has less stored energy for Mwende because it felt easier to lift
  3. The bucket has the same stored energy at the top in both cases because it rose the same height
  4. The bucket has more stored energy for Mwende because the pulley gave it extra energy
Correct answer: C
The bucket is the same and rises the same 8 m, so it gains the same stored energy either way. Feeling easier is about effort, not about the energy the bucket gains.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: PhET Energy Skate Park, Task 2 (pause at the top, middle and bottom of the track)
Q3. In the PhET Energy Skate Park simulation, a skater is released from the highest point of the track. At the moment the skater is at that highest point, which statement is correct?
  1. The skater's potential energy is greatest
  2. The skater has no energy at all
  3. The skater's potential energy and kinetic energy are both greatest
  4. The skater's kinetic energy is greatest
Correct answer: A
At the highest point the skater is highest and has the most potential energy. The skater is about to start moving, so kinetic energy is low there.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: PhET Energy Skate Park, Task 2 table (Position / Potential energy / Kinetic energy / Speed)
Q4. In the PhET Energy Skate Park simulation, the skater slides down and reaches the lowest point of the track, moving fastest. What do the energy bars show at the lowest point?
  1. Potential energy is greatest and kinetic energy is lowest
  2. Potential energy and kinetic energy are both zero
  3. Kinetic energy is greatest and potential energy is lowest
  4. Kinetic energy is lowest because the skater is at the bottom
Correct answer: C
At the bottom the skater is lowest, so potential energy is lowest, and fastest, so kinetic energy is greatest. Potential and kinetic energy trade off as the skater moves.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: vocabulary introduced after learners described the skater (potential and kinetic energy)
Q5. Which pair of statements correctly describes potential energy and kinetic energy?
  1. Potential energy is energy because of motion; kinetic energy is energy stored because of position
  2. Potential energy is energy stored because of position; kinetic energy is energy an object has because it is moving
  3. Potential energy is the force used to lift an object; kinetic energy is the effort a person feels
  4. Potential energy is energy only for living things; kinetic energy is energy only for machines
Correct answer: B
Potential energy is stored because of position (such as a bucket high in the well) and kinetic energy is present because of motion. Energy is not the same as force or effort.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: linking to the bucket using the bucket PE table (0, 400, 800, 1200, 1600 J)
Q6. A bucket is lifted in an 8 m deep well. Its stored energy at each height is: 0 m (water) = 0 J, 2 m = 400 J, 4 m = 800 J, 6 m = 1200 J, 8 m (ground level) = 1600 J. Which pattern does this table show?
  1. The higher the bucket, the less potential energy it has
  2. The potential energy stays the same at every height
  3. The higher the bucket, the more potential energy it has
  4. The potential energy is greatest at the water
Correct answer: C
The energy rises from 0 J to 1600 J as the height rises from 0 m to 8 m. Higher position means more stored potential energy.
Use after: DQB phase — Explain and DQB: 'What might the pulley be doing?' and the 'We still wonder...' list on the Driving Question Board
Q7. Mwende finds the pulley makes lifting the bucket feel easier, even though the bucket rises the same 8 m. At this stage of the lesson, which statement is the best one to make about why the pulley makes it easier?
  1. The bucket stops having potential energy once it is on the pulley
  2. The pulley makes the bucket lighter, so it stores less energy
  3. The pulley adds extra energy to the bucket, so there is less to lift
  4. We do not know yet; it is an open question for our Driving Question Board to investigate
Correct answer: D
This lesson only establishes that a higher bucket stores more potential energy. How the pulley helps, where the energy comes from and whether energy is lost are still open questions for later lessons.
