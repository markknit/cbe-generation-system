Q1. Mama Wanjiru pushes with the same 40 N force on a sharp knife and on a blunt knife. The sharp knife cuts a tomato but the blunt knife only squashes it. Which statement best explains why?
  1. The sharp knife has a smaller edge area, so the same force gives a greater pressure.
  2. The sharp knife carries more force because it is sharper.
  3. The sharp knife is heavier, so it pushes down with more force.
  4. The blunt knife has a smaller edge area, so it gives a greater pressure.
Correct answer: A
Both knives get the same 40 N, so force is not the difference. Pressure P = F/A, so the smaller edge area of the sharp knife gives the greater pressure.
Use after: Observe phase — Station A: Siphon
Q2. At a siphon station, a tube full of water joins a higher bucket to a lower basin, and water flows from the bucket to the basin. The outlet end is then raised until it is at the same level as the water surface in the bucket. What happens, and why?
  1. The flow gets faster because the outlet is now closer to the bucket.
  2. The flow stops because atmospheric pressure disappears when the tube is raised.
  3. The flow stops because there is no longer a difference in depth between the two water levels.
  4. The flow continues because air always pushes water through a tube.
Correct answer: C
A siphon needs atmospheric pressure and a difference in liquid level. When both levels are equal there is no depth difference, so the flow stops. Atmospheric pressure does not disappear.
Use after: Observe phase — Station B: Dam diagram (100 kPa at 10 m, 300 kPa at 30 m)
Q3. A dam holds water 30 m deep. Take the density of water as 1,000 kg/m3 and g = 10 N/kg. What is the pressure of the water at the base of the dam wall?
  1. 30 kPa
  2. 3,000 kPa
  3. 300 kPa
  4. 100 kPa
Correct answer: C
P = rho g h = 1,000 x 10 x 30 = 300,000 Pa = 300 kPa. This is 3 times the 100 kPa at 10 m depth, so the wall is thicker at the bottom.
Use after: Observe phase — Station C: Hydraulic jack model (two syringes and Mzee Otieno's jack)
Q4. Mzee Otieno's hydraulic jack has a small piston of area 5 cm2 and a large piston of area 100 cm2. He pushes the small piston with an effort of 50 N. Using F1/A1 = F2/A2, what load can the large piston lift?
  1. 2,500 N
  2. 1,000 N
  3. 250 N
  4. 50 N
Correct answer: B
The pressure is transmitted equally, so F2 = F1 x A2/A1 = 50 x 100/5 = 1,000 N. The area ratio is 20, so the force is 20 times bigger.
Use after: Explain phase — Part 2: Final Explanation (Claim-Evidence-Reasoning) of the knife phenomenon
Q5. A sharp knife edge has a contact area of 5 x 10^-6 m2. Mama Wanjiru pushes it with a force of 40 N. What pressure does the edge exert on the food?
  1. 8 MPa
  2. 0.2 MPa
  3. 80 MPa
  4. 0.8 MPa
Correct answer: A
P = F/A = 40 / 0.000005 = 8,000,000 Pa = 8 MPa.
Use after: Model Building phase — Final class model: threshold line at 2 MPa with the sharp and blunt knife pressures
Q6. Tomato and meat give way (are cut) at a pressure of about 2 MPa. A sharp knife exerts 8 MPa and a blunt knife exerts 0.8 MPa, both with the same 40 N force. Which conclusion is correct?
  1. The sharp knife cuts the food because 8 MPa is less than 2 MPa.
  2. Both knives cut the food because both are pushed with 40 N.
  3. The blunt knife only squashes the food because it is pushed with less force than the sharp knife.
  4. The blunt knife only squashes the food because 0.8 MPa is less than 2 MPa.
Correct answer: D
Food is cut only when the pressure is above 2 MPa. The sharp knife gives 8 MPa, which is above it, and the blunt knife gives 0.8 MPa, which is below it. The force is the same on both.
Use after: Explain phase — Part 1: 'Because...' chain for the water pump
Q7. Atmospheric pressure is about 101 kPa (101,000 Pa). Using P = rho g h with water density 1,000 kg/m3 and g = 10 N/kg, about how high can atmospheric pressure alone push water up a pipe?
  1. 1,010 m
  2. 10.1 m, which is about 10 m
  3. 101 m
  4. 1 m
Correct answer: B
h = P/(rho g) = 101,000 / (1,000 x 10) = 10.1 m, so about 10 m at most. Deeper boreholes need pumps that push the water up from below.
Use after: Explain phase — Part 1: 'Because...' chain for the bicycle pump (outlet blocked with a finger)
Q8. A learner blocks the outlet of a bicycle pump with a finger and pushes the handle in. The handle gets harder to push. What is the best explanation?
  1. The volume of air increases, so its pressure rises.
  2. The air in the barrel is squeezed into a smaller volume, so its pressure rises.
  3. The air in the barrel becomes lighter, so it pushes back harder.
  4. Atmospheric pressure outside the pump suddenly becomes bigger.
Correct answer: B
Pushing the piston in reduces the volume of the trapped air, so its pressure rises and pushes back harder on the piston. This is also why the valve opens when the pressure is higher than the tyre pressure.
