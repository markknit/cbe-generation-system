Q1. The Moon is about the same distance from the Sun as the Earth. Its surface reaches about 127 °C by day and falls to −173 °C at night. Earth's day-to-night temperature swings are much smaller. What is the best explanation for this difference?
  1. Gases in Earth's atmosphere absorb infrared energy from the surface and send some back down, slowing heat loss
  2. Earth's atmosphere reflects all sunlight straight back into space
  3. Earth is much closer to the Sun than the Moon is
  4. The Moon receives no sunlight at all during the day
Correct answer: A
Greenhouse gases in the atmosphere absorb the infrared energy emitted by the surface and re-emit some of it back toward the surface, so Earth cools slowly at night. The Moon has no atmosphere, so heat escapes quickly.
Use after: Observe phase — Steps 1–3 of the PhET investigation: two-column data table (Atmosphere Condition | Equilibrium Surface Temperature)
Q2. In a simulation, a student records the equilibrium surface temperature for an atmosphere with no greenhouse gases, then with a small amount, then with a large amount of greenhouse gases. What pattern should the data table show?
  1. The temperature rises at first and then drops back to the starting value
  2. The temperature stays the same because gases do not affect sunlight
  3. The temperature rises as the greenhouse gas concentration increases
  4. The temperature falls as the greenhouse gas concentration increases
Correct answer: C
More greenhouse gas means more infrared is absorbed and re-emitted back toward the surface, so the equilibrium surface temperature rises with concentration.
Use after: Observe phase — Step 4: 'Photon Absorption' tab – firing IR photons at CO₂, CH₄ and N₂
Q3. A student fires one infrared photon at a CO₂ molecule, then at a CH₄ molecule, then at an N₂ molecule. Which result is correct?
  1. Only N₂ absorbs the photon because it is the most common gas in air
  2. All three molecules absorb the photon equally
  3. None of the molecules absorb it because infrared passes through all gases
  4. CO₂ and CH₄ absorb the photon, but N₂ does not
Correct answer: D
CO₂ and CH₄ are greenhouse gases and absorb infrared photons. N₂ does not absorb infrared, so not all gases trap heat equally.
Use after: Explain phase — Direct instruction: shortwave radiation absorbed by the surface and re-emitted as longwave infrared
Q4. Sunlight reaches the ground in Trans-Nzoia and warms the soil. In what form does the warm Earth's surface mainly give out this energy?
  1. Shortwave ultraviolet radiation
  2. It does not give out energy; it keeps all of it
  3. Longwave infrared radiation
  4. Shortwave visible light, as the Sun does
Correct answer: C
The Sun's radiation is mainly shortwave. Earth's surface is much cooler, so it re-emits the absorbed energy mainly as longwave infrared radiation.
Use after: Explain phase — Revisiting 'Our Current Ideas' misconceptions: ozone layer vs greenhouse gases
Q5. A student says, 'The ozone layer is the gas that causes the greenhouse effect.' Which response is correct?
  1. Correct, ozone and CO₂ absorb the same type of radiation, so they are the same process
  2. Incorrect, the ozone layer absorbs ultraviolet radiation from the Sun, while the greenhouse effect is caused by gases such as CO₂ and CH₄ absorbing infrared
  3. Correct, ozone absorbs infrared from the Earth's surface and this is the main greenhouse effect
  4. Incorrect, ozone reflects infrared back to space so Earth stays cool
Correct answer: B
Ozone absorbs ultraviolet from the Sun. The greenhouse effect involves infrared from the surface being absorbed and re-emitted by gases such as CO₂, CH₄ and water vapour. These are two different processes.
Use after: Explain phase — Direct instruction: GHG molecules absorb and re-emit IR (back-radiation)
Q6. A CO₂ molecule in the lower atmosphere above Kenya absorbs an infrared photon from the ground. What happens next?
  1. The energy is destroyed, so it cannot warm anything
  2. The molecule stores the energy for ever and never re-emits it
  3. The molecule re-emits the energy in all directions, including back toward the surface
  4. The molecule turns the infrared into shortwave radiation that goes up into space
Correct answer: C
Greenhouse gas molecules re-emit absorbed infrared in all directions. The part sent back toward the surface is back-radiation, which raises surface temperature.
Use after: Model Building phase — Updating the Atmospheric Model: enhanced greenhouse effect and Lewis Glacier annotation
Q7. More charcoal is burned in Nairobi and more cattle are kept in Laikipia, so greenhouse gas levels rise. Which chain of events best explains how this can melt the Lewis Glacier on Mount Kenya?
  1. More greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit more infrared back toward the surface, so surface temperature rises and the ice melts
  2. More greenhouse gases make the ozone layer thinner, and this directly heats the air and melts the ice
  3. More greenhouse gases let more infrared escape to space, which warms the mountain
  4. More greenhouse gases bring the Sun closer to Mount Kenya, so the ice gets more heat
Correct answer: A
More greenhouse gases mean more infrared is absorbed and re-emitted back toward the surface. This increases back-radiation, which raises temperature (the enhanced greenhouse effect) and melts glacier ice.
