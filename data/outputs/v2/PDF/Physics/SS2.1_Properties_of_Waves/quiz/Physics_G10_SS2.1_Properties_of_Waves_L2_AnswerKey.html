Use after: Observe phase — Station 1 (Shadows): lining up three cards with holes and shifting the middle card
Q1. In an investigation, three cards each have a small hole at the same height. A torch shines at the first card and a screen is placed behind the third card. Light reaches the screen when the three holes are in a straight line. The middle card is then moved sideways so that its hole is off the line. What happens, and what does it show?
  1. The light bends around the card and still reaches the screen, showing light can curve
  2. A dimmer spot of light still reaches the screen, showing light spreads in all directions around corners
  3. The light reaches the screen but is brighter, showing light needs a bigger hole
  4. No light reaches the screen, showing light travels in straight lines
Correct answer: D
Light passes only when the holes are aligned. When one hole is shifted the straight path is blocked, which is evidence for rectilinear propagation.
Use after: Observe phase — Station 2 (Pinhole camera): drawing lines from the top and bottom of the object through the pinhole to the screen
Q2. A pinhole camera is made from a tin with a small hole at one end and tracing paper at the other. It is pointed at a bright candle flame. The image of the flame on the tracing paper is upside down. Which explanation is correct?
  1. The tracing paper turns the image over
  2. Light from the top of the flame travels in a straight line through the pinhole and lands at the bottom of the paper, and light from the bottom lands at the top
  3. Light bends upwards after passing through the hole
  4. The pinhole stores the light and releases it in reverse order
Correct answer: B
Straight rays from the top and bottom of the object cross at the pinhole, so the top of the object lands on the bottom of the screen. This supports straight-line travel of light.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: agreeing on rectilinear propagation and defining a ray
Q3. In Physics, what is a ray?
  1. The actual wave itself, which we can touch and see
  2. A straight line with an arrow that shows the direction in which wave energy travels
  3. A narrow shadow made by a torch behind an object
  4. A curved line showing how a wave spreads out in all directions
Correct answer: B
A ray is a model: a straight line with an arrow showing the direction of energy travel. It is not the wave itself.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: pairs calculate how many times faster light is than sound
Q4. The speed of light is 3.0 x 10^8 m/s and the speed of sound in air is 340 m/s. About how many times faster is light than sound?
  1. About 340 times
  2. About 88 000 times
  3. About 8 800 times
  4. About 880 000 times
Correct answer: D
3.0 x 10^8 / 340 is about 880 000, which is roughly 10^6 times faster.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: drawing the Hell's Gate situation as a ray diagram, 85 m + 85 m = 170 m, 170 m / 340 m/s = 0.50 s
Q5. Wanjiru shouts towards a cliff at Hell's Gate that is 85 m away. She hears her echo 0.50 s later. The speed of sound is 340 m/s. Which statement best explains the 0.50 s delay?
  1. The sound follows a curved path, which makes the journey slower
  2. The sound takes 0.50 s to travel just the 85 m to the cliff, and returns instantly
  3. The cliff stores the sound for a while and then releases it
  4. The sound travels 85 m to the cliff and 85 m back, a total of 170 m, and this takes 0.50 s at 340 m/s
Correct answer: D
The sound travels out and back: 85 m + 85 m = 170 m. Time = 170 / 340 = 0.50 s. The delay is travel time, not storage by the cliff.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: comparing light and sound speeds and explaining why we see lightning before hearing thunder
Q6. During a storm you see a lightning flash far away and hear the thunder a few seconds later. Why do you see the flash first?
  1. Light is louder than sound, so it is noticed first
  2. Sound travels in a curved path, so it travels a longer distance than light
  3. Light travels much faster than sound, so it reaches you almost instantly while sound takes time
  4. The lightning makes the thunder only after the flash has ended
Correct answer: C
Light travels at about 3.0 x 10^8 m/s and sound at 340 m/s, so light arrives almost instantly and sound arrives later.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: using the same ray idea for Ngong Hills (170 m, 1.00 s) on the class echo model
Q7. A learner at Ngong Hills shouts towards a cliff 170 m away. The speed of sound is 340 m/s. How long is it before the learner hears the echo?
  1. 0.25 s
  2. 1.00 s
  3. 0.50 s
  4. 2.00 s
Correct answer: B
The sound travels 170 m to the cliff and 170 m back, a total of 340 m. Time = 340 / 340 = 1.00 s. The value 0.50 s is only the one-way time.
