Use after: Explain phase — Claim-Evidence-Reasoning (CER) statement about the bent straw
Q1. Amina puts a straw in a glass of water and it looks broken at the water surface. Which statement best explains why?
  1. Water magnifies the straw so the part under water looks closer.
  2. The straw really bends when it is put into water.
  3. Light from the straw changes speed and direction as it passes between water and air.
  4. Light reflects off the water surface and bounces back to the eye.
Correct answer: C
Light travels at different speeds in water and in air, so it changes direction at the surface (refraction). The straw is not physically bent, and this is not reflection.
Use after: Explain phase — Complete the sentence: 'Light is slower in water (2.26 x 10^8 m/s) than in air... so it bends ___ the normal when entering water'
Q2. Light travels at 3.0 x 10^8 m/s in air and 2.26 x 10^8 m/s in water. A ray of light goes from air into water at an angle. What happens to it?
  1. It speeds up and bends away from the normal.
  2. It slows down and bends toward the normal.
  3. It slows down and bends away from the normal.
  4. It keeps the same speed and does not bend.
Correct answer: B
2.26 x 10^8 m/s is less than 3.0 x 10^8 m/s, so light slows down entering water. A wave that slows down bends toward the normal.
Use after: Explain phase — Compare sound: 'Sound goes from 340 m/s in air to 1500 m/s in water, so it speeds up and bends ___ the normal'
Q3. Sound travels at 340 m/s in air and about 1500 m/s in water. A sound wave moves from air into water at an angle. What happens to it?
  1. It speeds up and bends toward the normal.
  2. It speeds up and bends away from the normal.
  3. It keeps the same speed but changes direction.
  4. It slows down because water is denser, and bends toward the normal.
Correct answer: B
Sound is faster in water (1500 m/s) than in air (340 m/s), so it speeds up and bends away from the normal. The idea that denser water always slows waves is a misconception, because it applies to light but not to sound.
Use after: Observe phase — Station 2: Apparent depth (coin in the cup) and the data card with the Lake Victoria pool
Q4. A Lake Victoria shallow pool has a real depth of 1.33 m. Water has a refractive index of 1.33, so the apparent depth equals the real depth divided by 1.33. How deep does the pool look when viewed from directly above?
  1. 1.33 m
  2. 1.00 m
  3. 0.75 m
  4. 1.77 m
Correct answer: B
Apparent depth = real depth / 1.33 = 1.33 / 1.33 = 1.00 m. Rays from the bottom bend away from the normal as they leave the water, so the pool looks shallower than it really is.
Use after: Observe phase — Station 1: Bent straw, viewing from directly above (along the normal)
Q5. A learner looks straight down at a straw standing in a cup of water, along the normal to the water surface. What does the learner see, and what happens to the light speed?
  1. The straw looks bent, because light changes speed in water.
  2. The straw looks straight, but light still changes speed in water.
  3. The straw looks bent, because light changes direction but not speed.
  4. The straw looks straight, because light does not change speed along the normal.
Correct answer: B
Light entering along the normal is not bent, so the straw looks straight. Its speed still changes in the new medium, because bending depends on the angle of approach but the speed depends on the medium.
Use after: Observe phase — Station 3: Offline video of a ripple tank with water waves moving from deep to shallow water
Q6. Water waves in a ripple tank move from deep water into shallow water, where they travel more slowly. The frequency stays the same. What happens to the waves?
  1. The wavelength becomes shorter but the wave fronts stay straight in their original direction.
  2. The wavelength becomes shorter and the wave fronts bend.
  3. The wavelength becomes longer and the wave fronts bend.
  4. The wavelength stays the same and the wave fronts bend.
Correct answer: B
Speed = frequency x wavelength. With the frequency fixed and the speed smaller, the wavelength is shorter. The part of the wave front that enters the shallow water first slows down first, so the wave front swings and changes direction.
Use after: Model Building phase — Adding refraction to the class echo model: the reflected wave and the refracted wave at a boundary
Q7. A shout from Hell's Gate hits a cliff face and part of the sound returns as an echo. The rest goes into the rock. Which statement correctly describes what the wave does at this boundary?
  1. The returning part is reflected and stays in the air with i = r. The part entering the rock is refracted, with a new speed and direction.
  2. All of the sound is reflected, so none of it can be refracted.
  3. The returning part is refracted, and the part entering the rock is reflected.
  4. Both parts are refracted, because every wave changes direction at a boundary.
Correct answer: A
Reflection sends a wave back into the first medium with i = r, which makes the echo. Refraction is the part that goes on into the second medium at a different speed and direction.
