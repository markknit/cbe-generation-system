Use after: Predict phase — Predict: learners write what they think an echo is and share predictions with a partner
Q1. Wanjiru and her friends shout towards a cliff at Hell's Gate and, after a short delay, hear their own voices come back. Which statement best describes what an echo is?
  1. Wind that carries the voice back to the listeners
  2. A brand new sound that the cliff makes by itself
  3. Sound that comes back to the listener after meeting a surface
  4. A sound that the shouter's ears make after the shout
Correct answer: C
At this stage learners only need to recognise that an echo is sound coming back after meeting a surface. The other choices are common ideas, such as the wall making the sound or the wind carrying it back, which the class will test later.
Use after: Predict phase — Predict: individual written predictions, 'Our first ideas' recorded on the board
Q2. Before any teaching, Mr. Mwangi asks learners to write their own prediction about the echo. Why is it useful to write predictions first?
  1. So that learners with the same ideas as the teacher get the best marks
  2. So that later evidence can be used to confirm, challenge or revise our ideas
  3. So that the teacher can mark each prediction right or wrong straight away
  4. So that the class never needs to change its ideas during the unit
Correct answer: B
Committing to an idea in writing lets learners compare it with evidence later and revise it. There are no right or wrong answers at this stage.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: studying the Echo Table for the four trip sites
Q3. In the Echo Table, Hell's Gate has a cliff 85 m away and an echo delay of 0.50 s. Ngong Hills has a valley wall 170 m away and an echo delay of 1.00 s. Which statement correctly describes what the table shows for these two sites?
  1. Both sites have the same distance and the same delay
  2. The Ngong wall is closer and its echo delay is longer
  3. The Ngong wall is farther away but its echo delay is shorter
  4. The Ngong wall is farther away and its echo delay is longer
Correct answer: D
Ngong has the larger distance (170 m compared with 85 m) and the larger delay (1.00 s compared with 0.50 s). Learners only describe this pattern and do not calculate anything yet.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: studying the Echo Table, comparing site C with sites A and B
Q4. The Echo Table records what was heard at four sites. Which description matches the Kisumu school hall, where the far wall is 12 m away?
  1. A clear echo after a delay of 1.00 s
  2. A clear echo after a delay of 0.50 s
  3. No echo heard at all
  4. A loud, muddy, drawn-out sound with a very short delay of 0.07 s
Correct answer: D
The table records the Kisumu hall as a loud, muddy and drawn-out sound with a delay of 0.07 s. This is different from the clear echoes at Hell's Gate and Ngong Hills and from the no-echo result in Nairobi.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: pairs discuss why the Kisumu hall sounds muddy and the Nairobi field gives no echo
Q5. On the open grounds of a Nairobi school, the nearest hard wall is 300 m away and learners hear no echo after shouting. At this point in the unit, which statement is the most scientific?
  1. The wind always blows the sound away, so there is no echo
  2. The grass swallows the sound, so the matter is settled
  3. The sound simply disappears once it is outside
  4. We have observed that no echo is heard there, but we still need evidence from later lessons to explain why
Correct answer: D
The explanation has not been taught yet. Ideas such as 'grass swallows sound' or 'sound disappears' are first ideas to be tested, not conclusions, so the honest position is to describe what was observed and plan to investigate.
Use after: DQB phase — DQB: learners write 2 or 3 questions on sticky notes and place them on the class Driving Question Board
Q6. Which of these is the best question to add to the class Driving Question Board about the echo phenomenon?
  1. How many learners went on the trip?
  2. Why does the Kisumu school hall sound muddy while the Hell's Gate cliff gives a clear echo?
  3. Who in the group shouted first at the cliff?
  4. What is the name of the national park in the story?
Correct answer: B
A good Driving Question Board question starts with Why or How, is about the phenomenon and can be investigated. The other questions ask for simple facts that do not help explain the echoes.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: each learner draws an Initial Echo Model with a source, a surface and a returning path
Q7. Four learners draw their first echo models. Which drawing shows the most complete starting model of an echo?
  1. Only a person shouting, with no wall and no arrows
  2. A person, a wall or cliff, and arrows showing sound going to the wall and coming back, with labels
  3. Only a wall, with nothing showing where the sound comes from
  4. A person and a wall with no arrows or labels
Correct answer: B
The initial model should show at least the source, a surface and a returning path, with labels. The other drawings are missing one or more of these parts.
