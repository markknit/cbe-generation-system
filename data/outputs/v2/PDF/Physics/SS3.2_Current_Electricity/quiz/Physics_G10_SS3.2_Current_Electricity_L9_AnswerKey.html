Q1. In a mixed circuit, two identical 30 Ω bulbs are connected in parallel with each other. What is the equivalent resistance of this parallel pair?
  1. 60 Ω
  2. 45 Ω
  3. 15 Ω
  4. 30 Ω
Correct answer: C
For a parallel pair, 1/R = 1/30 + 1/30 = 2/30, so R = 15 Ω. Adding 30 + 30 = 60 Ω is the series rule, which is the common mistake.
Use after: Explain phase — STEP 2 and STEP 3: redraw the circuit, add to find total R, then find the main current
Q2. A 9 V battery is connected to a 30 Ω bulb in series with a parallel pair of 30 Ω bulbs. The parallel pair has an equivalent resistance of 15 Ω. What current does the battery supply?
  1. 0.20 A
  2. 0.12 A
  3. 0.15 A
  4. 0.30 A
Correct answer: A
Total R = 30 + 15 = 45 Ω, so I = V/R = 9/45 = 0.20 A. The value 0.12 A comes from wrongly adding 30 + 30 + 15 = 75 Ω.
Use after: Explain phase — STEP 4: find the p.d. across A
Q3. In a mixed circuit on a 9 V battery, a 30 Ω bulb A is in series with a parallel pair of bulbs B and C. The main current is 0.20 A. What is the p.d. across bulb A?
  1. 3.0 V
  2. 9.0 V
  3. 4.5 V
  4. 6.0 V
Correct answer: D
Bulb A carries the whole main current, so V = IR = 0.20 × 30 = 6.0 V. The remaining 3.0 V is across the parallel pair. The idea that every bulb gets 9 V is a misconception.
Use after: Observe phase — Unscrew bulb B and observe that C stays lit while A glows dimmer
Q4. Three identical 30 Ω bulbs are on a 9 V battery. Bulb A is in series with the parallel pair B and C. Bulb B is unscrewed. What does the ammeter in the main circuit now read?
  1. 0.30 A
  2. 0.15 A
  3. 0.20 A, because the current stays the same
  4. 0 A, because removing B puts out everything
Correct answer: B
Only A and C remain, in series: 30 + 30 = 60 Ω, so I = 9/60 = 0.15 A. C stays lit and glows brighter, while A glows dimmer.
Use after: Predict phase — Predict: which bulb will be brightest, A, B or C?
Q5. Which statement correctly describes the 3 bulbs in the mixed circuit (A in series with the parallel pair B and C) when they are first connected, with A having 6.0 V and 0.20 A, and B and C each 3.0 V and 0.10 A?
  1. Bulb A is dimmest because it is first in the circuit and uses up the current
  2. All three bulbs are equally bright because they are identical
  3. Bulb A is brightest because it has more p.d. and more current than B or C
  4. Bulbs B and C are brightest because they are in parallel
Correct answer: C
Bulb A has 6.0 V and 0.20 A, while B and C each have only 3.0 V and 0.10 A, so A is brightest. Current is not used up by the first bulb; it is the same all around the main loop.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Achieng's family uses a 12 V battery. How many 1.5 V cells in series would give 12 V?
Q6. Achieng's family solar home system in Kisumu uses a 12 V battery made of 1.5 V cells connected in series. How many cells are needed?
  1. 8
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 12
Correct answer: A
E.m.f. adds for cells in series, so the number of cells = 12 ÷ 1.5 = 8. Six cells give only 9 V.
Use after: Observe phase — Compare cells: two cells in parallel (1.5 V)
Q7. Two identical 1.5 V dry cells are connected in parallel to a bulb. Compared with one cell alone, what is the result?
  1. The e.m.f. is 3.0 V and the cells last longer
  2. The e.m.f. is 1.5 V and the cells last longer
  3. The e.m.f. is 1.5 V and the cells run out faster
  4. The e.m.f. is 3.0 V and the cells last the same time
Correct answer: B
Cells in parallel keep the same e.m.f. of 1.5 V, but each cell supplies only a share of the current, so the set lasts longer. Only cells in series add their e.m.f.
Use after: DQB phase — DQB: Kipchirchir's idea that the battery gets used up faster in parallel
Q8. On a 9 V battery, three identical bulbs in parallel drew 0.90 A, but the same three bulbs in series drew 0.10 A. Kipchirchir predicted that the battery is used up faster with bulbs in parallel. What do these currents show?
  1. He is partly right, because the battery supplies 9 times more current in parallel
  2. He is right because the p.d. across the bulbs is lower in parallel
  3. He is wrong, because the battery gives the same current in both circuits
  4. He is wrong, because the bulbs in series are dimmer and so drain the battery faster
Correct answer: A
The parallel bulbs draw 0.90 A against 0.10 A in series, so the battery supplies more current and is drained faster. The p.d. across each parallel bulb is higher (9 V), not lower.
Use after: Explain phase — KICD extension: dry cell with a voltmeter, with no load and then with a bulb connected; E = I(R + r) worked example
Q9. A real dry cell has e.m.f. 1.5 V and internal resistance 0.5 Ω. It is connected to an external resistor of 2.5 Ω. What is the terminal p.d. of the cell while it supplies current?
  1. 1.25 V
  2. 0.25 V
  3. 0.50 V
  4. 1.5 V
Correct answer: A
I = E/(R + r) = 1.5/(2.5 + 0.5) = 0.50 A. Terminal p.d. V = E - Ir = 1.5 - 0.50 × 0.5 = 1.25 V, which also equals IR = 0.50 × 2.5.
