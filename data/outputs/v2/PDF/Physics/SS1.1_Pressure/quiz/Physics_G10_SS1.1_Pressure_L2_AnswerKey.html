Q1. A 2 kg brick is placed gently on flour, first on its large face and then on its small face. Take g = 10 N/kg. What can you say about the force the brick presses on the flour with?
  1. It is less than 20 N on the large face because the force is spread out
  2. It is 20 N on the large face and more than 20 N on the small face
  3. It is 20 N on both faces, because the weight of the brick does not change
  4. It is greater on the large face because that face is bigger
Correct answer: C
Weight W = mg = 2 × 10 = 20 N. The brick's weight stays the same however it is placed, so the force on the flour is 20 N on every face. Only the area in contact changes.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: converting area from cm² to m² in the data table
Q2. The small face of a brick measures 10 cm by 5 cm. What is the area of this face in m², ready to use in P = F/A?
  1. 0.005 m²
  2. 0.0005 m²
  3. 0.05 m²
  4. 0.5 m²
Correct answer: A
Area = 10 × 5 = 50 cm². Since 1 m² = 10,000 cm², 50 cm² = 50 ÷ 10,000 = 0.005 m².
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: calculating P = F/A for each face of the brick
Q3. A 2 kg brick has weight 20 N. Its large face has an area of 0.02 m² and rests on flour. What pressure does the brick exert on the flour through this face?
  1. 400 Pa
  2. 0.001 Pa
  3. 20 Pa
  4. 1,000 Pa
Correct answer: D
P = F/A = 20 N ÷ 0.02 m² = 1,000 Pa. Dividing by a small area gives a large number.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: claim-evidence-reasoning on why the small face sinks deepest
Q4. The same 2 kg brick (weight 20 N) is placed on flour on its large, medium and small faces. The small face makes the deepest dent. Why?
  1. The brick becomes heavier when it stands on its small face
  2. The dents are all the same because the weight is the same
  3. The same 20 N acts on a smaller area, so the pressure is greater
  4. A bigger face always gives a bigger force, so the large face should sink deepest
Correct answer: C
The force is 20 N on every face, but the small face has the smallest area. 20 N over 0.005 m² gives 4,000 Pa, the greatest pressure, so the dent is deepest.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: writing a definition of pressure as P = F/A and stating the unit
Q5. Which statement correctly defines pressure and gives its unit?
  1. Pressure is the area covered by a force, measured in m²
  2. Pressure is the force acting at right angles per unit area, measured in pascals (Pa), where 1 Pa = 1 N/m²
  3. Pressure is the weight of an object, measured in kilograms (kg)
  4. Pressure is the same as force and is measured in newtons (N)
Correct answer: B
Pressure is force acting normally per unit area, P = F/A. Its unit is the pascal, with 1 Pa = 1 N/m². It is not the same as force, area or mass.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: quick calculation check using P = F/A
Q6. A force of 40 N acts at right angles on a surface of area 0.5 m². What is the pressure on the surface?
  1. 0.0125 Pa
  2. 80 Pa
  3. 40.5 Pa
  4. 20 Pa
Correct answer: B
P = F/A = 40 N ÷ 0.5 m² = 80 Pa. Dividing by 0.5 doubles the force value.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model Building: revising the knife model with 'same force' and small or large contact area
Q7. Mama Wanjiru pushes with the same 40 N force on a sharp knife and on a blunt knife. The sharp knife cuts the tomato easily. Which statement best explains this using what the brick on flour showed?
  1. The blunt knife has a smaller contact area, so it gives a greater pressure
  2. The sharp knife has a bigger force because it is sharper
  3. The sharp knife has a smaller contact area, so the same force gives a greater pressure
  4. Both knives give the same pressure, so the difference is only in the tomato
Correct answer: C
The force is the same for both knives, but the sharp edge touches the food over a much smaller area. Since P = F/A, the smaller area gives a greater pressure, as the small face of the brick did.
