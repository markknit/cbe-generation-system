Q1. Last lesson, a brick with the same weight was placed on flour on its large face, then on its small face. Which statement about the pressure on the flour is correct?
  1. The small face gives a larger pressure because the same force acts on a smaller area.
  2. The small face gives a smaller pressure because less of the brick touches the flour.
  3. The pressure is the same because the weight of the brick did not change.
  4. The pressure depends only on the weight, so the face does not matter.
Correct answer: A
Since P = F/A, the same force on a smaller area gives a larger pressure. This is why the brick's smallest face gave 4,000 Pa.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe Phase, Task 1: sharp knife contact area
Q2. Mama Wanjiru's sharp knife touches the tomato along a length of 0.05 m. The edge is 0.1 mm thick (0.0001 m). What is the contact area of the edge?
  1. 5 × 10^-3 m²
  2. 5 × 10^-5 m²
  3. 5 × 10^-6 m²
  4. 5 × 10^-7 m²
Correct answer: C
Area = length × thickness = 0.05 × 0.0001 = 0.000005 m², which is 5 × 10^-6 m². Learners must first convert 0.1 mm to 0.0001 m.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe Phase, Task 1: sharp knife pressure
Q3. Mama Wanjiru pushes the sharp knife with a force of 40 N. The contact area of the edge is 5 × 10^-6 m². What is the pressure under the edge?
  1. 8 kPa
  2. 0.8 MPa
  3. 8 MPa
  4. 80 MPa
Correct answer: C
P = F/A = 40 ÷ 0.000005 = 8,000,000 Pa, which is 8 MPa (1 MPa = 1,000,000 Pa).
Use after: Observe phase — Observe Phase, Task 2: blunt knife and comparison with 2 MPa
Q4. Mama Wanjiru pushes a blunt knife with 40 N. Its edge has a contact area of 5 × 10^-5 m². A tomato skin gives way at about 2 MPa. What is the pressure under the blunt edge, and what happens to the tomato?
  1. 0.8 MPa, so the tomato is squashed because the force is smaller than for the sharp knife.
  2. 8 MPa, which is more than 2 MPa, so the tomato is cut.
  3. 0.8 MPa, which is less than 2 MPa, so the tomato is only squashed.
  4. 0.8 MPa, which is less than 2 MPa, so the tomato is cut easily.
Correct answer: C
P = 40 ÷ 0.00005 = 800,000 Pa = 0.8 MPa. This is below the 2 MPa needed, so the food only squashes. The force is the same 40 N as for the sharp knife.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe Phase, Task 3: camel and person
Q5. A camel has a mass of 400 kg. Take g = 10 N/kg. It stands on four feet, each with an area of 0.05 m². What pressure does the camel exert on the sand?
  1. 2 kPa
  2. 5 kPa
  3. 80 kPa
  4. 20 kPa
Correct answer: D
Weight = 400 × 10 = 4,000 N. Total area = 4 × 0.05 = 0.2 m². P = 4,000 ÷ 0.2 = 20,000 Pa = 20 kPa.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain Phase: second prompt on why the camel does not sink much deeper than the student
Q6. A 400 kg camel is about 6.7 times heavier than a 60 kg student. On desert sand the camel exerts a pressure of 20 kPa and the student in school shoes exerts 15 kPa. Why does the camel not sink about 6.7 times deeper?
  1. The camel's wide feet spread its weight over a much larger area, so its pressure is only a little larger.
  2. The sand pushes back harder on a camel than on a student.
  3. The camel's weight is much smaller than the student's weight.
  4. Heavier animals always press less on the ground than lighter ones.
Correct answer: A
Pressure depends on both force and area. The camel's area (0.2 m²) is five times the student's (0.04 m²), which cancels most of the extra weight.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain Phase: claim-evidence-reasoning explanation 'same force, different area'
Q7. Mama Wanjiru pushes a sharp knife and a blunt knife with the same 40 N force. The sharp knife cuts a tomato, but the blunt knife only squashes it. Which explanation is best?
  1. The sharp knife is pushed with a bigger force.
  2. The sharp knife has a larger contact area, so it presses harder.
  3. The pressure is the same for both, but the sharp knife is stronger.
  4. The force is the same, but the sharp edge has a smaller area, so it gives a larger pressure.
Correct answer: D
Both knives have the same force (40 N). The sharp edge has 10 times less area, so by P = F/A its pressure (8 MPa) is above the 2 MPa needed to cut, while the blunt knife gives only 0.8 MPa.
