Use after: Observe phase — Step C: Data Strip (Lewis Glacier area in 1934, 2004 and 2023)
Q1. A data strip gives the area of Mount Kenya's Lewis Glacier as about 1.35 km² in 1934 and about 0.37 km² in 2004. By about how much did the glacier's area decrease between 1934 and 2004?
  1. 0.37 km²
  2. 1.15 km²
  3. 0.98 km²
  4. 1.72 km²
Correct answer: C
Decrease = 1.35 − 0.37 = 0.98 km². Adding the two values (1.72) or reading off a single value are common slips.
Use after: Observe phase — Step A: Visual Evidence, Observation T-chart (observe vs infer)
Q2. Students are filling in an Observation T-chart with the columns WHAT I OBSERVE (facts only) and WHAT I INFER (explanations). Which statement belongs in the WHAT I OBSERVE column?
  1. The Lewis Glacier covers a much smaller area in the recent satellite image than in the 1934 photograph.
  2. The farmer's rains are late because the glacier is melting.
  3. The glacier is shrinking because the Sun has moved closer to Earth.
  4. The glacier is shrinking because of a hole in the sky.
Correct answer: A
A fact that can be seen directly in the images goes in the observe column. The other statements give causes, so they are inferences.
Use after: Observe phase — Step C: Data Strip, describe the pattern in one sentence
Q3. The Lewis Glacier's area was about 1.35 km² in 1934, about 0.37 km² in 2004, and is estimated at less than 0.20 km² in 2023. Which sentence best describes the pattern in these three data points?
  1. The glacier's area grew between 2004 and 2023.
  2. The glacier's area has got smaller at each measurement.
  3. The glacier's area shrank only between 2004 and 2023.
  4. The glacier's area stayed about the same from 2004 to 2023.
Correct answer: B
1.35 > 0.37 > 0.20, so the area falls at every step. The other options contradict the numbers.
Use after: Observe phase — Step B: Farmer Testimony (round-robin reading of James Muriithi's account)
Q4. In the testimony written for this lesson, a fictional maize farmer in Embu County, James Muriithi, describes what has changed on his farm. Which statement matches what he reports?
  1. The long rains now arrive weeks earlier than before, and his maize and bean yields have risen.
  2. The long rains (March–May) now arrive weeks later than in his grandfather's time, and his maize and bean yields have fallen sharply.
  3. The long rains have stopped completely, but his yields are the same as before.
  4. The long rains arrive on time, but the glaciers have disappeared from his farm.
Correct answer: B
The testimony reports later long rains and sharply lower maize and bean harvests. This is a fact reported in the testimony, not an explanation of its cause.
Use after: Predict phase — Predict: Prediction Wall (what could cause a glacier to shrink AND rains to become unpredictable?)
Q5. Students know that ice melts when heat is added. They found it puzzling that the Lewis Glacier on Mount Kenya is shrinking even though the mountain has not moved closer to the Sun. What is the puzzle they are left with?
  1. Why does ice melt at all when heat is added?
  2. Why is Mount Kenya a volcanic mountain?
  3. Why is the Sun hotter at noon than in the morning?
  4. Where does the extra heat that melts the glacier come from, if the mountain is no nearer the Sun?
Correct answer: D
The anchoring puzzle is that melting needs added heat, yet the mountain has not moved closer to the Sun. The question is left open at this stage.
Use after: Observe phase — Step B: Farmer Testimony, discussion of why a glacier AND a farmer's testimony are shown together
Q6. The Lewis Glacier has shrunk since 1934, and farmers about 50 km away report late and unpredictable long rains. These changes are happening at the same time. Which statement is best supported by the evidence the class has seen so far?
  1. It is proven that the Sun has moved closer to Earth.
  2. The two places are 50 km apart, so the changes cannot possibly be linked.
  3. The changes happen at the same time, but this evidence alone does not show what causes them, so the class still has to investigate.
  4. It is proven that a hole in the sky is letting in extra sunlight.
Correct answer: C
The images, data and testimony show that both changes occur. They do not by themselves prove a cause, so the cause is left to investigate in the unit. A distance of 50 km does not rule out a shared cause either.
Use after: DQB phase — Driving Question Board: sorting question notes into clusters A, B, C and D
Q7. The class sorts its Driving Question Board notes into clusters: (A) causes of greenhouse gas increase, (B) how warming links to rainfall, (C) what can be done, (D) other physics questions. Which question belongs in cluster B?
  1. Which human activities add more gases to the air?
  2. How is infrared radiation different from visible light?
  3. What can Kenyan farmers and communities do to respond?
  4. How could a warmer climate change the timing of the long rains?
Correct answer: D
Cluster B is about how warming links to rainfall. The second question fits cluster A, the third fits cluster C and the fourth fits cluster D.
