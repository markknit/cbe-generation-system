Q1. A Grade 10 student sketches the greenhouse effect from memory. Which sequence of steps is scientifically correct?
  1. Sunlight is blocked by greenhouse gases, so Earth receives less energy and the lower atmosphere cools
  2. Sunlight passes through the atmosphere, Earth re-emits infrared radiation, and greenhouse gases absorb and re-radiate it, warming the lower atmosphere
  3. Greenhouse gases make the Sun give out more heat, and this extra heat warms Earth's surface directly
  4. Earth reflects all incoming ultraviolet radiation, and the ozone layer then traps it near the surface
Correct answer: B
Incoming shortwave solar radiation passes through, Earth gives out longwave infrared, and gases such as CO2, CH4 and N2O absorb and re-radiate that infrared, warming the lower atmosphere.
Use after: Predict phase — Predict phase: connecting glacier retreat and rainfall disruption to the same root cause
Q2. Mount Kenya's Lewis Glacier has retreated since 1934. At the same time, farmers in Embu County, about 50 km away, find the long rains harder to predict. Which statement best explains how both changes can be linked?
  1. Ozone layer depletion is the only cause of both changes, because it traps infrared radiation
  2. Mount Kenya has moved closer to the Sun, so the ice melts and the rains arrive late
  3. The enhanced greenhouse effect raises temperatures, which melts glacier ice and also changes evaporation and rainfall patterns
  4. The two changes are unrelated, because the glacier is on a mountain and the farm is on lower ground
Correct answer: C
Extra greenhouse gases trap more infrared energy and warm the planet. Warmer temperatures melt ice and alter the water cycle (evaporation and rainfall patterns), so one root cause can explain both observations.

Q3. Lake Turkana Wind Power produces electricity from the wind in northern Kenya. Is it a mitigation or an adaptation response to climate change, and why?
  1. Adaptation, because it helps people cope with warming that has already happened
  2. Mitigation, because it makes electricity without releasing greenhouse gases, so it acts at the first step of the causal chain
  3. Neither, because wind power does not change greenhouse gas emissions
  4. Adaptation, because it removes carbon dioxide from the air
Correct answer: B
Mitigation means cutting greenhouse gas emissions or removing CO2. Wind power replaces fuels that release greenhouse gases, so it acts at the first step of the chain (fewer greenhouse gases released).
Use after: Observe phase — Observe phase: sorting the Response Cards into MITIGATION and ADAPTATION and saying which step of the causal chain each acts on
Q4. A farmer in Embu plants drought-tolerant crops and uses rainwater harvesting. Why is this classed as adaptation and not mitigation?
  1. It reduces the amount of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere
  2. It stops infrared radiation from being absorbed by greenhouse gases
  3. It does not change the greenhouse effect, but it reduces the harm from changing rainfall
  4. It replaces charcoal with a cleaner fuel for cooking
Correct answer: C
Adaptation helps people cope with change already happening. These farming methods do not alter greenhouse gas levels; they reduce the damage from drought and unreliable rain.

Q5. Which statement correctly describes the role of the ozone layer, and how it differs from the greenhouse effect?
  1. The ozone layer traps infrared radiation from Earth and is the main cause of the greenhouse effect
  2. The ozone layer reflects visible light back to space and so keeps Earth cool
  3. The ozone layer absorbs UV-B radiation from the Sun; it is a separate process from greenhouse gases trapping infrared radiation
  4. The ozone layer produces CFCs, which then absorb infrared radiation
Correct answer: C
Ozone in the stratosphere absorbs harmful UV-B. The greenhouse effect involves gases absorbing and re-radiating infrared. CFCs are human-made chemicals that deplete ozone, not something the ozone layer produces.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain phase: Component B written explanation on why the long rains in Embu County have become harder to predict
Q6. A student writes: "Greenhouse gases are the only reason the long rains in Embu have become harder to predict." What is the best scientific response?
  1. Incorrect, because greenhouse gases have no effect on rainfall at all
  2. Correct, because natural cycles have no effect on Kenya's rainfall
  3. Incomplete, because natural cycles such as El Niño and the Indian Ocean Dipole also affect Kenya's rainfall; warming is a major contributor, not the only cause
  4. Correct, because El Niño and the Indian Ocean Dipole only affect rainfall in other countries
Correct answer: C
Warming changes the water cycle and rainfall patterns, but natural climate cycles like El Niño and the Indian Ocean Dipole also change rainfall from year to year. A strong explanation says warming is a major contributor, not the only one.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain phase: Component B, naming a Kenyan human activity (charcoal burning, Mau Forest deforestation) that contributes to the problem
Q7. Why does clearing and burning part of the Mau Forest add to the enhanced greenhouse effect?
  1. Trees give out infrared radiation, so fewer trees means less infrared escapes to space
  2. Trees reflect sunlight, so removing them lets more UV-B reach Earth and that causes the greenhouse effect
  3. Burning and clearing release CO2, and there are fewer trees left to absorb CO2 from the air
  4. Cutting trees thins the ozone layer directly, which warms the ground
Correct answer: C
Trees take in CO2 as they grow. Deforestation, especially by burning, releases stored carbon as CO2 and removes trees that would absorb it, so greenhouse gas levels rise.
Use after: End of lesson — Model phase: closing sentence "I can now explain the farmer's experience using physics because..."
Q8. Which one-sentence ending best explains the Embu farmer's experience using physics?
  1. Because the Sun is giving out more heat than it did in his grandfather's time, the rains have moved
  2. Because the ozone layer has stopped the rain clouds from forming over Embu
  3. Because the farmer has planted maize at the wrong time, so the rains are no longer reliable
  4. Because greenhouse gases from human activities trap more infrared energy, warming the planet and changing evaporation and rainfall patterns, so the rains are harder to predict
Correct answer: D
The physics link is the enhanced greenhouse effect: more trapped infrared raises temperatures, which alters the water cycle and rainfall timing. The other statements invent causes or blame the farmer.
