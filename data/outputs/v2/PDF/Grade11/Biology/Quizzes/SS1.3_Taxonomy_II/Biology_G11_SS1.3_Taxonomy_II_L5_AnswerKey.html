Q1. In a class activity, groups sorted eight animal pictures. One group sorted by size, another by 'animals we eat', and a third by habitat. A scientist in Nairobi and one in Kisumu need to agree on how to group animals. What is the main problem with these sorting rules?
  1. They depend on opinion or situation, so different people get different groups
  2. They cannot be written on manila paper
  3. They only work for animals that live on land
  4. They use too many animals at once
Correct answer: A
Size, food use and habitat vary with the person or place, so groupings differ. Scientists need shared, observable features that give the same result for everyone.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Expert A group (Annelida and Mollusca: earthworm, snail)
Q2. A learner says, 'A snail and an earthworm are both soft-bodied, so they must belong to the same phylum.' Which observation best shows the snail is NOT in the same phylum as the earthworm?
  1. The snail is smaller than the earthworm
  2. The snail is found in gardens, while the earthworm lives in soil
  3. The snail has a hard shell and an unsegmented body, while the earthworm has a body divided into many rings
  4. The snail moves more slowly than the earthworm
Correct answer: C
Snails (Mollusca) have a soft unsegmented body with a shell, while earthworms (Annelida) have a segmented body with rings. Size, speed and habitat are not phylum features.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Expert B group (Arthropoda: butterfly, mosquito, grasshopper)
Q3. A mosquito has three body parts, six jointed legs and a hard outer covering. Which phylum does it belong to?
  1. Chordata
  2. Arthropoda
  3. Annelida
  4. Mollusca
Correct answer: B
Jointed legs and a hard exoskeleton are features of Arthropoda. The mosquito has no backbone, no shell and no body rings.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Expert D and Expert E groups (Chordata: tilapia, lizard, chicken, goat)
Q4. A tilapia from Lake Victoria, a lizard, a chicken and a goat all look very different. Which feature do they ALL share that places them in the same phylum?
  1. Living in water
  2. A backbone
  3. Scales covering the body
  4. Wings for flying
Correct answer: B
Chordata in this lesson are the animals with a backbone (fish, reptiles, birds, mammals). Their body coverings, habitats and movement differ.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: writing Claim-Evidence-Reasoning (CER) statements for chosen animals
Q5. A learner writes this claim: 'The butterfly belongs to Phylum Arthropoda.' Which piece of evidence best supports the claim?
  1. It is found in the school garden
  2. It has jointed legs and an exoskeleton
  3. It is useful for pollinating flowers
  4. It is beautiful and colourful
Correct answer: B
Good evidence is an observable body feature that anyone can check. Jointed legs and an exoskeleton are Arthropoda features, while the other options are opinion, habitat or usefulness.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: comparing why the mosquito and goat are in different phyla
Q6. A mosquito feeds on blood and a goat feeds on grass, yet they are placed in different phyla. Which is the best reason?
  1. The mosquito can fly but the goat cannot
  2. The mosquito has jointed legs and an exoskeleton with no backbone, while the goat has a backbone
  3. The mosquito is smaller than the goat
  4. The mosquito feeds on blood but the goat does not
Correct answer: B
Phyla are decided by body features such as a backbone, jointed legs and exoskeleton. The mosquito is an arthropod and the goat is a chordate. Size, feeding and flight are not used to decide phylum.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: revising the class classification tree diagram with phyla and distinguishing features
Q7. A class is building a tree diagram of Kingdom Animalia. Which animal is placed correctly under its phylum?
  1. Butterfly under Chordata, because it can fly
  2. Lizard under Mollusca, because it is cold to touch
  3. Earthworm under Annelida, because its body is divided into rings and it has no legs
  4. Snail under Arthropoda, because it has a hard shell
Correct answer: C
The earthworm's segmented body with no legs places it in Annelida. The snail is Mollusca, the lizard is Chordata and the butterfly is Arthropoda.
