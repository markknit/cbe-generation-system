Q1. Wanjiru must roof a store with a square-based pyramid roof. The base is 6 m by 6 m and the vertical height is 4 m. She wants the area of one sloping triangular face. Which length should she use as the height of that triangle?
  1. The vertical height from the apex to the centre of the base, 4 m
  2. The full base side, 6 m
  3. The slant height, measured from the apex down the middle of the face to the midpoint of a base edge
  4. Half of the base side, 3 m
Correct answer: C
The height of a triangular face is measured along the face, perpendicular to its base edge, so it is the slant height. The vertical height runs inside the pyramid and gives the common error of 48 m2.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: building the 6 cm square-based pyramid net and checking 3² + 4² = 5²
Q2. A square-based pyramid has a base side of 6 m and a vertical height h = 4 m. The slant height l, the vertical height h and half the base side form a right-angled triangle. What is the slant height l?
  1. 4 m
  2. 7 m
  3. 5 m
  4. About 7.2 m
Correct answer: C
Half the base is 3 m, so l² = 4² + 3² = 16 + 9 = 25 and l = 5 m. Adding 4 + 3 gives the wrong 7 m, and using the full base of 6 m gives about 7.2 m.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: groups compare vertical height and slant height on the model and answer 'Why is the slant height longer than the vertical height?'
Q3. Learners built a square-based pyramid model and measured both heights. They found that the slant height is longer than the vertical height. Which statement explains why?
  1. The slant height is the longest side (hypotenuse) of the right-angled triangle formed by the vertical height, half the base side and the slant height
  2. The slant height is measured along an edge of the pyramid, so it is always longer
  3. The slant height is always exactly double the vertical height
  4. The slant height is longer because the base is a square and not a triangle
Correct answer: A
The vertical height and half the base are the two shorter sides of a right-angled triangle, and the slant height is its hypotenuse, so it is longer than either. For h = 4 and half-base = 3, l = 5, which is not double 4.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: worked example of the 60 m2 roof area (4 x 1/2 x 6 x 5)
Q4. A pyramid-shaped iron-sheet roof has a square base of 6 m by 6 m, a vertical height of 4 m and a slant height of 5 m. The roof has no floor. How many m2 of iron sheet cover the four sloping faces?
  1. 48 m2
  2. 120 m2
  3. 60 m2
  4. 96 m2
Correct answer: C
Each triangular face is 1/2 x 6 x 5 = 15 m2, and 4 faces give 60 m2. The base is not covered for a roof, so the 36 m2 base is not added. Using h = 4 gives the wrong 48 m2, and including the base gives the wrong 96 m2.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: pairs solve the Kajiado school hall roof problem (8 m x 8 m base, h = 3 m)
Q5. A school hall in Kajiado has a pyramid-shaped roof with a square base of 8 m by 8 m and a vertical height of 3 m. The roof has no floor. What is the area of iron sheet needed to cover the four sloping faces?
  1. 144 m2
  2. 48 m2
  3. 160 m2
  4. 80 m2
Correct answer: D
Half the base is 4 m, so l = √(3² + 4²) = 5 m. Roof area = 4 x 1/2 x 8 x 5 = 80 m2. Using h = 3 gives the wrong 48 m2, adding the 64 m2 base gives the wrong 144 m2, and forgetting the 1/2 gives the wrong 160 m2.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: teacher-supplied regular triangular pyramid (tetrahedron, edges 6 cm)
Q6. A regular triangular pyramid (tetrahedron) has all its edges equal to 6 cm. Each of its four triangular faces has an area of about 15.59 cm2. What is the total surface area of the pyramid?
  1. About 93.53 cm2
  2. About 46.77 cm2
  3. About 31.18 cm2
  4. About 62.35 cm2
Correct answer: D
Pyramid TSA = base area + areas of the triangular faces. A tetrahedron has 4 equal faces, so TSA = 4 x 9√3 ≈ 62.35 cm2. Using 3 faces gives the wrong 46.77 cm2 because the base is left out.
Use after: DQB phase — DQB: sticky notes linking surface area (covering material only) to the drum and box with the same 30,800 cm2
Q7. A metalsmith in Eldoret makes a cylindrical drum and a rectangular box from the same area of iron sheet, 30,800 cm2 each. The drum holds more maize than the box. Which statement best explains this?
  1. Surface area only tells us how much material covers a solid, and the shape decides how much the solid can hold
  2. Equal surface area always means equal volume, so the box must have been measured wrongly
  3. The drum must have used more iron sheet than the box
  4. The box has a larger surface area, so it should hold more
Correct answer: A
Surface area measures the covering material, while volume measures the space inside, and these are different quantities. Two solids with the same surface area can have different volumes because of their shapes.
