Use after: Predict phase — Predict: Wanjiru says the box has a surface area of 30,800 cm2, so does that tell us how much maize it holds?
Q1. Wanjiru made a rectangular box from 30,800 cm2 of iron sheet. She says this number tells us how much maize the box can hold. Is she right?
  1. No, because a rectangular box has a surface area but no volume.
  2. Yes, because cm2 can be changed directly into litres of maize.
  3. No, because surface area measures the material covering the outside, not the space inside the box.
  4. Yes, because the area of sheet is the same as the size of the box, so it gives the capacity.
Correct answer: C
Surface area is the amount of material covering a solid (cm2). Volume is the space inside (cm3), so the sheet area cannot tell us the capacity.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe, Pair 1 (prisms): rectangular prism and 3-4-5 triangular prism filled with sand
Q2. A triangular prism has right-angled triangle ends with sides 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm. The prism is 10 cm long. What is its volume?
  1. 60 cm3
  2. 120 cm3
  3. 6 cm3
  4. 150 cm3
Correct answer: A
The base is a right-angled triangle with perpendicular sides 3 cm and 4 cm, so base area = 1/2 x 3 x 4 = 6 cm2. Volume = 6 x 10 = 60 cm3, the same as the 5 x 4 x 3 cm rectangular prism.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe, Pair 2 (pyramid and prism): fill the pyramid and pour it into the prism
Q3. A hollow pyramid and a hollow prism have the same square base and the same vertical height. You fill the pyramid with sand and pour it into the prism, again and again, until the prism is full. How many pyramid-fuls of sand fill the prism?
  1. 3
  2. 2
  3. 1
  4. 4
Correct answer: A
The sand-fill investigation shows that 3 pyramid-fuls fill a prism with the same base and height. This is why the pyramid volume is 1/3 x base area x height.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain, Apply (1): Box from Mzee Kiplagat, V = 2,500 x 129
Q4. A closed box has a square base of 50 cm by 50 cm and a height of 129 cm. How many litres of maize can it hold when full? (1 litre = 1,000 cm3)
  1. 322.5 litres
  2. 3,225 litres
  3. 32.25 litres
  4. 322,500 litres
Correct answer: A
Base area = 50 x 50 = 2,500 cm2. V = 2,500 x 129 = 322,500 cm3. Dividing by 1,000 gives 322.5 litres.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain, Apply (2): Pyramid roof from Lesson 2, V = 1/3 x 36 x 4
Q5. A roof is a pyramid with a square base 6 m by 6 m. Its vertical height is 4 m and its slant height is 5 m. What is the volume of the pyramid?
  1. 144 m3
  2. 48 m3
  3. 24 m3
  4. 60 m3
Correct answer: B
Use the vertical height, not the slant height. V = 1/3 x 36 x 4 = 48 m3. Using the slant height of 5 m wrongly gives 60 m3.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain, exit question: prism with base area 40 cm2 and height 9 cm and a pyramid with the same base and height
Q6. A prism has a base area of 40 cm2 and a height of 9 cm. A pyramid has the same base and the same vertical height. What are the volumes of the prism and the pyramid?
  1. Prism 360 cm2 and pyramid 120 cm2
  2. Prism 360 cm3 and pyramid 120 cm3
  3. Prism 120 cm3 and pyramid 360 cm3
  4. Prism 360 cm3 and pyramid 180 cm3
Correct answer: B
Prism V = base area x height = 40 x 9 = 360 cm3. The pyramid holds one third of that: 1/3 x 40 x 9 = 120 cm3. Volume is in cm3, not cm2.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: revise the class model with columns 'Surface area (material, cm2)' and 'Volume (space inside, cm3)'
Q7. Learners are updating the class model with two columns: 'Surface area' and 'Volume'. Which entry is placed correctly for a box with base 50 cm by 50 cm and height 129 cm?
  1. Surface area 30,800 cm3 (material covering the box); Volume 322,500 cm2 (space inside the box)
  2. Surface area and volume are both 30,800 because the same sheet is used
  3. Surface area 322,500 cm3 (material covering the box); Volume 30,800 cm2 (space inside the box)
  4. Surface area 30,800 cm2 (material covering the box); Volume 322,500 cm3 (space inside the box)
Correct answer: D
Surface area is the material covering the solid and is measured in cm2. Volume is the space inside and is measured in cm3. Equal surface area does not mean equal volume.
