Q1. A Kenya Power engineer's tan button is broken. Her notebook says sin 50° = 0.766 and cos 50° = 0.643. Which value is closest to tan 50°?
  1. 0.49
  2. 0.84
  3. 1.41
  4. 1.19
Correct answer: D
tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ, so tan 50° = 0.766 ÷ 0.643 ≈ 1.19. The other values come from multiplying, adding, or dividing the wrong way round.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: pair table work, computing the 'sin θ ÷ cos θ' column for 30°, 40°, 50° and 60°
Q2. From mathematical tables, sin 30° = 0.500 and cos 30° = 0.866. What is sin 30° ÷ cos 30° to 3 decimal places?
  1. 1.366
  2. 1.732
  3. 0.577
  4. 0.433
Correct answer: C
0.500 ÷ 0.866 = 0.577 to 3 d.p., which matches tan 30° from the tables. Dividing the other way gives 1.732, multiplying gives 0.433, and adding gives 1.366.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: comparing the sin θ ÷ cos θ column with the tan θ column and writing the 'always / sometimes / never' sentence
Q3. A pair finds that sin θ ÷ cos θ matched tan θ for 30°, 40°, 50° and 60° in their tables. What is the best conclusion?
  1. It is likely a relationship that holds for any acute angle, and so needs a proof.
  2. It only works for angles bigger than 45°.
  3. It is probably a coincidence caused by rounding in the tables.
  4. It only works for these four angles because they are in the tables.
Correct answer: A
Agreement across several different angles suggests an identity, a relationship that is always true. The next step is to prove it from the definitions of the ratios, not to treat it as chance.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: teacher's board proof that tan θ = sin θ / cos θ
Q4. In a right-angled triangle, sin θ = O/H and cos θ = A/H, where O is the opposite side, A the adjacent side and H the hypotenuse. What is (O/H) ÷ (A/H) after simplifying?
  1. (O × A)/H²
  2. O/A
  3. O/H
  4. A/O
Correct answer: B
(O/H) ÷ (A/H) = (O/H) × (H/A). The H cancels, leaving O/A, which is the definition of tan θ.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Problem A, given sin θ = 3/5 find cos θ and tan θ
Q5. For an acute angle θ, sin θ = 3/5. Which pair of values for cos θ and tan θ is correct?
  1. cos θ = 4/5 and tan θ = 3/4
  2. cos θ = 2/5 and tan θ = 3/2
  3. cos θ = 4/5 and tan θ = 4/3
  4. cos θ = 3/5 and tan θ = 1
Correct answer: A
The triangle has opposite = 3 and hypotenuse = 5, so the adjacent side is 4 (3-4-5 triangle), giving cos θ = 4/5. Then tan θ = (3/5) ÷ (4/5) = 3/4.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Problem B, use the identity to find sin 35° and verify with a calculator
Q6. For an angle of 35°, tan 35° = 0.700 and cos 35° = 0.819. Using tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ, what is sin 35° to 3 decimal places?
  1. 1.170
  2. 0.855
  3. 0.573
  4. 1.519
Correct answer: C
Rearranging tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ gives sin θ = tan θ × cos θ = 0.700 × 0.819 ≈ 0.573. Dividing either way or adding gives values that are not valid sine values or do not fit the identity.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Problem C, finding tan 50° and the height of the Nakuru mast
Q7. The angle of elevation to the top of a mast is 50°, and the observer stands 40 m from its base. Using sin 50° = 0.766 and cos 50° = 0.643, what is the height of the mast to 1 decimal place? (Use height = 40 × tan 50°.)
  1. 25.7 m
  2. 30.6 m
  3. 47.7 m
  4. 33.6 m
Correct answer: C
tan 50° = 0.766 ÷ 0.643 ≈ 1.191, so height = 40 × 0.766 ÷ 0.643 ≈ 47.7 m. The other answers come from using sin or cos alone, or from dividing 40 by tan 50°.
