Use after: Observe phase — Part A: Diagram Construction (angle of depression diagram)
Q1. A tourist stands at the top of a coastal cliff and looks down at a boat on the sea. Which statement correctly describes the angle of depression?
  1. The angle measured between her line of sight and the vertical face of the cliff
  2. The angle measured upward from the sea level to her line of sight to the boat
  3. The angle measured downward from the horizontal line at her eye level to her line of sight to the boat
  4. The angle measured between the cliff face and the sea surface
Correct answer: C
An angle of depression is always measured downward from the horizontal through the observer's eye, not from the vertical cliff face.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: Worked Demonstration, Step 3 (identify the correct ratio)
Q2. A surveyor stands 40 m from the base of a mast and knows the angle of elevation to its top. She wants the height of the mast. The 40 m is the side next to the angle (adjacent) and the height is the side opposite the angle. Which ratio should she use?
  1. Cosine, because a horizontal distance is involved
  2. Sine, because it is the first ratio we learnt
  3. None of the ratios work, because only one side is known
  4. Tangent, because it links the opposite and adjacent sides
Correct answer: D
Tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent connects the known adjacent side and the unknown opposite side. The angle supplies the second piece of information needed.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: Worked Demonstration, Step 4 (Nakuru mast: h = 40 × tan 50°)
Q3. A student stands 40 m from the base of a Kenya Power mast in Nakuru. The angle of elevation to the top of the mast is 50°. Using tan 50° ≈ 1.1918, what is the height of the mast, to 1 decimal place?
  1. 30.6 m
  2. 33.6 m
  3. 62.2 m
  4. 47.7 m
Correct answer: D
tan 50° = h/40, so h = 40 × tan 50° = 40 × 1.1918 ≈ 47.7 m. Dividing by tan 50° would give 33.6 m, which is wrong because the height is the unknown opposite side.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: coastal cliff demonstration (cliff 20 m, angle of depression 35° to a dhow)
Q4. From the top of a 20 m high cliff, the angle of depression to a dhow at sea level is 35°. Using tan 35° ≈ 0.7002, how far is the dhow from the foot of the cliff, to 1 decimal place?
  1. 16.4 m
  2. 34.9 m
  3. 14.0 m
  4. 28.6 m
Correct answer: D
The 35° angle inside the triangle at the dhow is opposite the 20 m cliff, so tan 35° = 20/d. Then d = 20 ÷ tan 35° = 20 ÷ 0.7002 ≈ 28.6 m.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: coastal cliff demonstration (alternate angles between the two horizontal lines)
Q5. From the top of a cliff, the angle of depression to a boat at sea is 35°. What is the angle of elevation from the boat to the top of the cliff?
  1. 90°
  2. 55°
  3. 35°
  4. 145°
Correct answer: C
The horizontal at the cliff top and the sea level are parallel lines, so the alternate interior angles are equal. The angle of elevation from the boat equals the angle of depression from the top, which is 35°.
Use after: Explain phase — Problem 1 (Elevation — Nairobi): flagpole in Uhuru Park
Q6. A visitor stands 15 m from the base of a flagpole in Uhuru Park, Nairobi. The angle of elevation to the top of the flag is 62°. Using tan 62° ≈ 1.8807, what is the height of the flagpole, to 1 decimal place?
  1. 28.2 m
  2. 32.0 m
  3. 13.2 m
  4. 8.0 m
Correct answer: A
tan 62° = h/15, so h = 15 × tan 62° = 15 × 1.8807 ≈ 28.2 m. The answer of 8.0 m comes from wrongly dividing by tan 62°.
Use after: Explain phase — Problem 3 (Depression — coastal cliff): tourist and dhow
Q7. A tourist stands at the top of a coastal cliff 27 m above sea level. She measures the angle of depression to a dhow at sea level as 41°. Using tan 41° ≈ 0.8693, how far is the dhow from the base of the cliff, to 1 decimal place?
  1. 17.7 m
  2. 41.2 m
  3. 31.1 m
  4. 23.5 m
Correct answer: C
The cliff height 27 m is opposite the 41° angle (from alternate angles) and the distance d is adjacent. So tan 41° = 27/d, giving d = 27 ÷ 0.8693 ≈ 31.1 m. Writing tan 41° = d/27 gives the common wrong answer of 23.5 m.
