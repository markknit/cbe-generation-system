Q1. A lorry is 6 m in front of a small plane mirror held by a matatu conductor. How far apart are the lorry and its image in the mirror?
  1. The image is smaller, so less than 6 m
  2. 6 m
  3. 12 m
  4. 3 m
Correct answer: C
The image appears as far behind the mirror as the object is in front of it. The distance from the lorry to its image is 6 + 6 = 12 m.

Q2. Triangle ABC is reflected in the y-axis to give triangle A'B'C'. The only facts known are that AB = A'B', BC = B'C' and CA = C'A', because reflection preserves length. Which congruence test proves that the two triangles are congruent?
  1. AAS, because two angles and a side are equal
  2. SSS, because all three pairs of corresponding sides are equal
  3. RHS, because the triangles are right-angled
  4. They only look the same, so no test can prove it
Correct answer: B
Only the three pairs of equal sides are given, which is exactly the SSS test. RHS needs a right angle and AAS needs angles, and neither is given. Visual inspection is not a proof.
Use after: Observe phase — Evidence Pack discussion: SSS and opposite/indirect congruence
Q3. A triangle and its mirror image are congruent, yet the image cannot be slid on the page to fit exactly onto the object without being lifted off. What is the reason?
  1. Reflection reverses the orientation, so the congruence is opposite (indirect)
  2. The corresponding sides of the image are shorter than those of the object
  3. The angles of the image are different from those of the object
  4. The image is slightly larger than the object
Correct answer: A
Reflection keeps all lengths and angles, so the shapes are congruent. It reverses orientation (left-right), so the congruence is opposite or indirect, and the image must be flipped, which means lifting it off the page.

Q4. The point P(5, −2) is reflected in the line y = −x. What are the coordinates of its image P'?
  1. (−2, 5)
  2. (−5, 2)
  3. (2, −5)
  4. (−2, −5)
Correct answer: C
For reflection in y = −x, (a, b) → (−b, −a). So (5, −2) → (−(−2), −5) = (2, −5).
Use after: Explain phase — Final Explanation Poster, Section 3: The Mirror Line (midpoint calculation)
Q5. The point P(3, 4) is reflected to P'(−3, 4). The midpoint of PP' is (0, 4), and the segment PP' is horizontal. What is the equation of the mirror line?
  1. y = 4
  2. x = 0
  3. y = 0
  4. y = x
Correct answer: B
The mirror line passes through the midpoint (0, 4) and is perpendicular to the horizontal segment PP', so it is vertical. A vertical line through x = 0 is the y-axis, so the equation is x = 0.
Use after: Explain phase — Final Explanation Poster, Section 2: extension to mirror lines y = k
Q6. The point (3, 5) is reflected in the line y = 2, using the rule (a, b) → (a, 2k − b) with k = 2. What is the y-coordinate of the image?
  1. −5
  2. −3
  3. 9
  4. −1
Correct answer: D
Using y = 2k − b = 2(2) − 5 = −1, so the image is (3, −1). The point and its image are each 3 units from the line y = 2, on opposite sides.

Q7. Which statement correctly describes the mirror line in a reflection?
  1. It is parallel to the segment joining any object point to its image
  2. It passes through the object point but not through the image point
  3. It cuts the segment joining a point and its image at a slant, at its midpoint
  4. It is the perpendicular bisector of the segment joining any object point to its image
Correct answer: D
The mirror line cuts every object–image segment at its midpoint (bisects it) and at 90° (perpendicular). That is why the object and image are equally far from the mirror.

Q8. The word AMBULANCE is painted reversed on the front of an ambulance. Why does it read correctly in a driver's rear-view mirror?
  1. The mirror makes the image smaller, which corrects the word
  2. The mirror changes the angles of the letters, so the word changes
  3. The mirror image is a congruent copy that is flipped left to right, so reversed lettering is flipped back to read correctly
  4. The mirror rotates the image through 180°, which turns the letters upside down
Correct answer: C
A reflection keeps size and shape but reverses left and right (lateral inversion). Reversed lettering is therefore flipped back to normal, so it reads correctly in the mirror.
Use after: End of lesson — Exit ticket (1): the coordinate rule for reflection in y = x
Q9. The point (7, −2) is reflected in the line y = x. What are the coordinates of its image?
  1. (2, −7)
  2. (−7, −2)
  3. (−2, 7)
  4. (7, 2)
Correct answer: C
For reflection in y = x, (a, b) → (b, a). So (7, −2) → (−2, 7).
