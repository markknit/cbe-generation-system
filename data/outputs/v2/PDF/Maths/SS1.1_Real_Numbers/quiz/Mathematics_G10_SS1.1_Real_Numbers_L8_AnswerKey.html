Use after: Predict phase — Predict: 6 kg of unga into 3/4 kg packets
Q1. A shop has 6 kg of unga to pack into packets of 3/4 kg each. How many packets can be filled?
  1. 8 packets
  2. 4.5 packets
  3. 18 packets
  4. 2 packets
Correct answer: A
Dividing by 3/4 is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal 4/3, so 6 ÷ 3/4 = 6 × 4/3 = 8. Check: 8 × 3/4 = 6 kg.
Use after: Observe phase — Station 2: running pace (3 minutes 45 seconds per km)
Q2. A runner takes 3 minutes 45 seconds to run 1 km. Using the reciprocal of her pace in minutes per km, what is her speed in km per hour?
  1. 17.4 km per hour
  2. 16 km per hour
  3. 3.75 km per hour
  4. 0.2667 km per hour
Correct answer: B
3 minutes 45 seconds = 3.75 minutes per km. The reciprocal is 1/3.75 = 0.2667 km per minute, and 0.2667 × 60 = 16 km per hour (same as 60 ÷ 3.75). Writing 3 minutes 45 seconds as 3.45 minutes is the common error.
Use after: Observe phase — Station 3: fuel use of a boda-boda (35 km per litre)
Q3. A boda-boda travels 35 km on each litre of petrol. Taking the reciprocal of this rate, how much petrol does it use per kilometre?
  1. 0.2857 litres per km
  2. 35 litres per km
  3. 0.02857 litres per km
  4. 2.857 litres per km
Correct answer: C
The reciprocal of 35 km per litre is 1/35 = 0.02857 litres per km. Check: 35 km × 0.02857 litres per km is about 1 litre.
Use after: Observe phase — Station 4: exchange rate (shillings per dollar to dollars per shilling)
Q4. Ksh 130 buys USD 1. Ksh 1 buys 1/130 USD. Using this reciprocal, how many US dollars can Ksh 6 500 buy?
  1. 0.05 USD
  2. 845 000 USD
  3. 5 USD
  4. 50 USD
Correct answer: D
The reciprocal of the rate is 1/130 USD per shilling. So 6 500 × 1/130 = 50 USD. Check by multiplying back: 50 × 130 = 6 500.
Use after: Explain phase — Debrief of the stations: a ÷ b = a × 1/b and why dividing by a number below 1 increases the value
Q5. Why is the answer to 6 ÷ 3/4 bigger than 6?
  1. Because the reciprocal of 3/4 is 3/4 itself.
  2. Because 3/4 is less than 1, so 6 contains more than 6 groups of 3/4 (we multiply by 4/3, which is more than 1).
  3. Because dividing always gives a bigger answer than the number you start with.
  4. Because we divide 6 by 3 and then by 4 separately.
Correct answer: B
Dividing by a number less than 1 gives more than you started with, because 3/4 fits into 6 more than 6 times. This matches 6 × 4/3 = 8, where 4/3 is greater than 1.
Use after: Model Building phase — Exit ticket: The reciprocal of 28π is… It is rational/irrational because…
Q6. A calculator shows 28 × π = 87.96459430… with digits that never end or repeat. What is the reciprocal of 28π, and is it rational or irrational?
  1. About 0.01137; it is irrational because its decimal never ends or repeats, so it cannot be written exactly as a fraction of whole numbers.
  2. About 0.01137; it is rational because it is 1 divided by a number, which is a fraction.
  3. About 0.01137; it is rational because the calculator shows it ending after a few decimal places.
  4. About 0.0357; it is irrational because it contains π.
Correct answer: A
1/(28π) ≈ 0.01137 to 4 s.f. The calculator display is only an approximation. The reciprocal of an irrational number is irrational, so its decimal never terminates or repeats.
Use after: DQB phase — Closing the Driving Question Board: re-reading the driving question about numbers that go on forever
Q7. A sufuria has a diameter of exactly 28 cm. Its circumference is 28π cm, and a student cuts string 88 cm long, which leaves a tiny gap. Which statement best explains how we work accurately with such a number?
  1. The string should be 87 cm, because irrational numbers must always be rounded down.
  2. The string cannot be cut, because 28π does not exist as a number.
  3. 28π can never be written completely, so we choose and state an accuracy that suits the job, such as 87.96 cm to 2 decimal places.
  4. The circumference is exactly 88 cm, because 88 is the nearest whole number.
Correct answer: C
28π is irrational, so its decimal never ends and it cannot be written exactly. In practice we round to a stated accuracy suited to the job, for example 87.96 cm. The 88 cm string is only an approximation, which is why there is a gap.
