Use after: Observe phase — Group presentations: comparing totals from different decompositions of the Kericho heptagonal plot
Q1. Two groups find the area of the same seven-sided plot near Kericho. Group 2 splits it into five triangles. Group 4 splits it into two triangles and one trapezium. Both groups measure and calculate carefully. What should be true about their total areas?
  1. Group 2's total should be larger because it used more shapes
  2. The two totals should be almost the same, because the area of the plot does not depend on how it is split
  3. The totals must be different, because different formulae were used
  4. Group 4's total should be larger because a trapezium covers more land than triangles
Correct answer: B
Area is a property of the plot itself. Any correct decomposition into triangles and quadrilaterals gives the same total, apart from small rounding or measuring differences.
Use after: Observe phase — Group presentations: dividing the total area equally among the three farming families
Q2. The total area of the Kericho plot is 1 260 m². It must be divided into three sections of equal area for three farming families. What area should each family receive?
  1. 1 257 m²
  2. 630 m²
  3. 3 780 m²
  4. 420 m²
Correct answer: D
Equal division among three families means total ÷ 3, so 1 260 ÷ 3 = 420 m². Dividing by 2 would only suit two owners.
Use after: Model Building phase — Individual written assessment, Q1: triangular maize plot near Eldoret
Q3. A triangular maize plot near Eldoret has a base of 48 m and a perpendicular height of 35 m. What is its area?
  1. 420 m²
  2. 83 m²
  3. 840 m²
  4. 1 680 m²
Correct answer: C
Area of a triangle = ½ × base × height = ½ × 48 × 35 = 840 m². Choosing 1 680 m² forgets the ½.
Use after: Model Building phase — Individual written assessment, Q2: tea-drying platform in Kericho (trapezium)
Q4. A tea-drying platform in Kericho is a trapezium. Its parallel sides are 22 m and 14 m long, and the perpendicular distance between them is 9 m. What is its area?
  1. 324 m²
  2. 277 m²
  3. 162 m²
  4. 198 m²
Correct answer: C
Area = ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × height = ½ × (22 + 14) × 9 = 162 m². Leaving out the ½ gives 324 m².
Use after: Model Building phase — Individual written assessment, Q3(a): regular octagon community centre floor in Kisumu
Q5. A community centre floor in Kisumu is a regular octagon with side 6 m and apothem 7.24 m. Using area = ½ × perimeter × apothem, what is the area of the floor?
  1. 21.72 m²
  2. 43.44 m²
  3. 347.52 m²
  4. 173.76 m²
Correct answer: D
The perimeter is 8 × 6 = 48 m. Area = ½ × 48 × 7.24 = 173.76 m². Using 6 × 7.24 × ½ only covers one side, and skipping the ½ doubles the answer.
Use after: Model Building phase — Individual written assessment, Q3(b): regular heptagon garden
Q6. A garden in the shape of a regular heptagon has side 4 m and apothem 4.15 m. Using area = ½ × perimeter × apothem, what is the area of the garden?
  1. 8.3 m²
  2. 58.1 m²
  3. 116.2 m²
  4. 29.05 m²
Correct answer: B
A heptagon has 7 sides, so the perimeter is 7 × 4 = 28 m. Area = ½ × 28 × 4.15 = 58.1 m². Forgetting the ½ gives 116.2 m².
Use after: Model Building phase — Individual written assessment, Q5: solar panel parallelogram inside a rectangular roof in Mombasa
Q7. A school roof in Mombasa is a rectangle 30 m long and 20 m wide. Inside it, solar panels cover a parallelogram with base 18 m, perpendicular height 20 m and slanted side 22 m. What is the area of the roof NOT covered by solar panels?
  1. 204 m²
  2. 960 m²
  3. 240 m²
  4. 360 m²
Correct answer: C
Rectangle = 30 × 20 = 600 m². Parallelogram = base × perpendicular height = 18 × 20 = 360 m². Area not covered = 600 − 360 = 240 m². Using the slanted side gives 204 m², which is wrong.
