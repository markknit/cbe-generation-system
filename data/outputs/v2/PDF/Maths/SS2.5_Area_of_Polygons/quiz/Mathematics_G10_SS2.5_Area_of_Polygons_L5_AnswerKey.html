Use after: Observe phase — Task 1: Decomposition Observation (drawing lines from centre O to each vertex and measuring the central angle)
Q1. A regular octagon has centre O. Straight lines are drawn from O to each of its 8 vertices, dividing it into 8 congruent triangles. What is the angle at O in each triangle?
  1. 135°
  2. 60°
  3. 22.5°
  4. 45°
Correct answer: D
The angles around the centre add up to 360°, and they are shared equally among 8 triangles: 360° ÷ 8 = 45°. The value 135° is the interior angle of the octagon, not the central angle.
Use after: Explain phase — Part A: Decomposition Method (area of one isosceles triangle from the measured apothem)
Q2. A regular octagon of side 3 m is split from its centre into 8 identical isosceles triangles. Each triangle has base 3 m and height (apothem) 3.62 m. What is the area of ONE triangle, to 2 decimal places?
  1. 43.44 m²
  2. 4.50 m²
  3. 5.43 m²
  4. 10.86 m²
Correct answer: C
Area of a triangle = ½ × base × height = ½ × 3 × 3.62 = 5.43 m². Forgetting the ½ gives 10.86 m², and 43.44 m² is the area of all 8 triangles together.
Use after: Explain phase — Part B: Formula Method (substituting s = 3 m into A = 2(1 + √2)s²)
Q3. The area of a regular octagon with side length s is A = 2(1 + √2)s². Using this formula, what is the area of the Mombasa courtyard, a regular octagon with side 3 m? Give the answer to 2 decimal places.
  1. 43.46 m²
  2. 14.49 m²
  3. 86.91 m²
  4. 21.73 m²
Correct answer: A
A = 2(1 + √2) × 3² = 2 × 2.4142 × 9 ≈ 43.46 m² (43.45 m² if √2 is rounded to 1.414). Using 3 instead of 3² gives 14.49 m², and leaving out the factor 2 gives 21.73 m².
Use after: Explain phase — Part C: Verification (comparing the two answers and justifying why they are almost identical)
Q4. A group finds the area of a regular octagon (side 3 m) as 43.5 m² by decomposing it into 8 triangles. The formula A = 2(1 + √2)s² gives 43.45 m². Why are the two answers very slightly different?
  1. The decomposition method only works for hexagons, not octagons.
  2. The eight triangles are not exactly the same size.
  3. The apothem (3.62 m) was rounded or measured, so the decomposition answer is only approximate.
  4. The formula is for irregular octagons, so it cannot match.
Correct answer: C
Both methods describe the same octagon, since the formula comes from the decomposition. The small difference comes from rounding the apothem to 3.62 m. The triangles are congruent.
Use after: Explain phase — Part D: Tile and Cost Calculation (number of tiles)
Q5. The Mombasa courtyard has an area of 43.45 m². Each square tile measures 0.25 m × 0.25 m. How many tiles are needed to cover the courtyard, before any allowance for wastage? (Round to a whole number of tiles you must buy.)
  1. 696 tiles
  2. 174 tiles
  3. 695 tiles
  4. 173 tiles
Correct answer: A
One tile has area 0.25 × 0.25 = 0.0625 m². Number of tiles = 43.45 ÷ 0.0625 = 695.2, which must be rounded UP to 696 so the whole floor is covered. Dividing by 0.25 instead of 0.0625 gives about 174.
Use after: Explain phase — Part D: Tile and Cost Calculation (total cost including 10% wastage)
Q6. A contractor needs 696 tiles to cover a courtyard floor. She adds 10% for wastage, which makes 766 tiles in total (rounded up). Each tile costs Ksh 85. What is the total cost of the tiles she orders?
  1. Ksh 65,110
  2. Ksh 60,010
  3. Ksh 5,916
  4. Ksh 59,160
Correct answer: A
Total tiles = 696 × 1.10 ≈ 766. Cost = 766 × 85 = Ksh 65,110. Ksh 59,160 ignores wastage, and Ksh 60,010 wrongly adds 10 tiles instead of 10%.
Use after: Explain phase — Part F: Regular heptagon (decomposition with s = 4 m and apothem 4.15 m)
Q7. A regular heptagon has side 4 m. It is split from its centre into 7 congruent isosceles triangles, each with base 4 m and height (apothem) 4.15 m. What is the area of the heptagon?
  1. 58.1 m²
  2. 116.2 m²
  3. 8.3 m²
  4. 66.4 m²
Correct answer: A
One triangle = ½ × 4 × 4.15 = 8.3 m². The heptagon has 7 triangles, so 7 × 8.3 = 58.1 m². The value 8.3 m² is only one triangle, and 66.4 m² uses 8 triangles.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model Building: cumulative area model, timeline and note under the Kericho Plot box
Q8. A village plot has seven sides of different lengths, and no single area formula fits it. Which strategy from this lesson's work on octagons and heptagons could be used to find its area?
  1. Measure only the longest side and use the octagon formula.
  2. Divide the plot into triangles, find each triangle's area and add them up.
  3. Multiply the longest side by the shortest side.
  4. Say it is impossible to find the area of a shape with unequal sides.
Correct answer: B
Every polygon can be decomposed into triangles, whose areas can be found with ½ × base × height and then added. This is the main idea of the cumulative area model leading to the Kericho plot.
