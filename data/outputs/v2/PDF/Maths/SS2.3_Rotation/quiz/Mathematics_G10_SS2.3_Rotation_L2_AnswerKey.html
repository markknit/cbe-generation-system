Q1. The minute hand of the clock on the University of Nairobi Administration Block moves from 12:00 to 12:15. Which of these stays the SAME?
  1. The angle the hand makes with the 12 mark
  2. The direction the hand points
  3. The length of the hand and the distance of each point on it from the centre of the clock
  4. The position of the tip of the hand
Correct answer: C
The hand does not stretch or shrink, so its size, its shape and the distance of every point from the centre stay the same. Only its direction changes.
Use after: Observe phase — Part: Set-up and Q2 'Did the pin point O move at any point?'
Q2. A learner pins a triangle cutout ABC to a sheet of paper at point O and turns it 90° clockwise. What happens to the pin point O?
  1. It does not move, because it is the only fixed point (the centre of rotation)
  2. It moves to the middle of the image
  3. It moves 90° clockwise with the triangle
  4. It moves a short distance, then stops
Correct answer: A
The centre of rotation is invariant. The pin stays in the same place while every other point of the cutout turns about it.
Use after: Observe phase — Pair 1, Round 1 and analysis Q3: compare angle AOA′, BOB′ and COC′
Q3. Triangle ABC is rotated 90° clockwise about O. Vertex A is 8 cm from O and vertex B is 4 cm from O. A learner measures angle AOA′ as 90°. What should angle BOB′ be?
  1. 45°, because B is closer to O
  2. It cannot be known without measuring
  3. 180°, because B is half the distance
  4. 90°, because every point turns through the same angle about O
Correct answer: D
Every point on the object moves through the same angle about the centre, whatever its distance from O. So angle BOB′ is also 90°.
Use after: Explain phase — Gallery share and discussion: Property 1, congruence (analysis Q1)
Q4. A triangle has sides 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm. It is rotated 180° about a pin at point O. Which statement about the image is correct?
  1. The image has longer sides, because the triangle moved a long way round
  2. The image has the same sides but different angles
  3. The image has sides 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm, so it is congruent to the object
  4. The image is a mirror copy, so it is not congruent to the object
Correct answer: C
Rotation keeps lengths and angles unchanged, so the object and its image are congruent. The sides stay 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm.
Use after: Explain phase — Analysis Q4 and Property 4: comparing Image 1 (90° clockwise) with Image 3 (90° anticlockwise)
Q5. A learner rotates a triangle 90° anticlockwise about O. Which clockwise rotation about O gives exactly the same image?
  1. 270° clockwise
  2. 180° clockwise
  3. 360° clockwise
  4. 90° clockwise
Correct answer: A
A 90° anticlockwise turn is the same as 360° − 90° = 270° clockwise. It is not the same as 90° clockwise, which puts the image in a different position.
Use after: Explain phase — Gallery share and discussion: connecting the four properties to the posho mill millstone
Q6. The millstone at a posho mill in Kericho turns about its axle. Which statement describes the rotation correctly?
  1. The axle moves round in a circle, while the edge of the stone stays still
  2. The stone becomes bigger as it turns
  3. Points near the edge turn through a bigger angle than points near the axle
  4. The axle stays fixed, and every point of the stone turns through the same angle about it
Correct answer: D
The axle is the centre of rotation, so it is invariant. Every point of the millstone turns through the same angle, and the stone keeps its size and shape.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model building: revising the Lesson 1 clock sketch with 90° arcs drawn from several points on the minute hand
Q7. A learner draws the clock's minute hand at 12:00 and at 12:30 and adds arcs to show the movement. The tip of the hand turns through 180°. Through what angle does a point in the middle of the hand turn?
  1. 180°, because every point on the hand turns through the same angle
  2. 90°, because it is only halfway along the hand
  3. It turns through no angle, because it is close to the centre
  4. 360°, because it goes all the way round
Correct answer: A
From 12:00 to 12:30 the hand turns through 2 × 90° = 180°. Every point on the hand turns through this same angle, not just the tip.
