Q1. At 12:00 the minute hand of a clock points straight up, towards the numeral 12. Which is the best prediction for where the tip of the hand will point at 12:15?
  1. Towards the numeral 9, on the left
  2. Towards the numeral 3, on the right
  3. Straight up, towards the numeral 12
  4. Towards the numeral 6, at the bottom
Correct answer: B
A quarter of an hour later the minute hand has made a quarter turn clockwise, so it points to the 3. Learners who choose other positions are guessing the direction or the amount of turning.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: I Notice / I Wonder table and the class list of 'What stays the same' and 'What changed'
Q2. Four photographs show the minute hand of the University of Nairobi clock at 12:00, 12:15, 12:30 and 12:45. Which statement correctly describes what changed and what stayed the same?
  1. The hand turned into a mirror image of itself each time, and the centre moved
  2. The hand slid sideways across the clock face, and its direction stayed the same
  3. The length of the hand changed, and the direction it points stayed the same
  4. The direction the hand points changed, and the length of the hand stayed the same
Correct answer: D
The learners' I Notice lists record that the hand points in a different direction each time, while its length and its centre point stay the same. The hand does not stretch, flip or slide.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: groups write their most important 'Wonder' question on a sticky note for the Driving Question Board
Q3. Which of these is the best 'I Wonder' question to post on the Driving Question Board after watching the clock images?
  1. The clock is on the Administration Block.
  2. The minute hand moves.
  3. How much has the hand turned between 12:00 and 12:15?
  4. The hand is long and thin.
Correct answer: C
A good Driving Question Board entry is a real mathematical question. The other choices are statements of what can be seen, not questions.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: improvised clock kit investigation (measuring tip and halfway-dot distances before and after rotation)
Q4. A group pins the base of a 20 cm card arrow to a sheet of paper. They measure a straight line from the pin to the tip of the arrow and get 20 cm. They then turn the arrow clockwise by any amount and measure again. What should they find for the straight-line distance from the pin to the tip?
  1. 0 cm, because the pin does not move
  2. Less than 20 cm, because the arrow has turned away from where it started
  3. More than 20 cm, because the tip has moved further away
  4. About 20 cm, the same as before
Correct answer: D
Careful measurement in the improvised clock kit gives the same distance before and after turning, to within about 2 mm. This is an observation from the investigation; the lesson does not yet ask why it happens.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: improvised clock kit investigation (teacher checks for confusing distance from the centre with arc length)
Q5. In the card-arrow investigation, one learner measures the distance from the pin to the tip by running a string along the curved path that the tip traced while turning. Is this the distance from the centre to the tip?
  1. No, because the curve is only the distance for the halfway dot
  2. Yes, because any path from the pin to the tip gives the same distance
  3. Yes, because the tip travelled along the curve
  4. No, because the distance from the centre is the straight line from the pin to the point, not along the curve
Correct answer: D
The distance from the centre to a point is measured along a straight line from the pin to that point. The curved path is the arc traced by the tip, which is a different measurement.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: vocabulary introduced after the investigation (centre of rotation, angle of rotation, clockwise, anticlockwise)
Q6. In the card-arrow investigation, the pin holds the base of the arrow still while the arrow turns. In mathematics, what is the pin called?
  1. The angle of rotation
  2. The image
  3. The clockwise point
  4. The centre of rotation
Correct answer: D
The fixed point that the object turns about is called the centre of rotation. The amount of turning is the angle of rotation.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: Rotation Model Journal Page 1, Model Statement on what kind of motion the clock hand performs
Q7. The tip of a clock's minute hand traces a circular arc about a fixed point. The hand does not stretch, shrink or flip over. Which word best names this kind of motion?
  1. Translation, because the hand slides in a straight line
  2. Enlargement, because the hand gets longer
  3. Reflection, because the hand makes a mirror image
  4. Rotation, because the hand turns about a fixed point
Correct answer: D
Turning about a fixed centre without changing size or shape is a rotation. It is not a slide (translation) or a mirror flip (reflection).
