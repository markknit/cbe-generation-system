Q1. Mama Wanjiru's new bed has an area of x² + 8x + 15 square metres. Its width is (x + 3) metres. Earlier, Chebet saw that (x + 3)(x + 5) = x² + 8x + 15. What is the length of the bed?
  1. (x + 8) metres
  2. (x + 5) metres
  3. (x + 15) metres
  4. (x + 12) metres
Correct answer: B
The area is width × length, and (x + 3)(x + 5) expands to x² + 8x + 15, so the length is (x + 5) m. Choosing (x + 12) or (x + 15) comes from adding or copying numbers without using the expansion.
Use after: Observe phase — Task C: factor pairs of 108 for x² + 3x − 108
Q2. Learners factorise x² + 3x − 108 by listing factor pairs of 108 and looking for a pair that differs by 3. Which two numbers p and q give x² + 3x − 108 = (x + p)(x + q)?
  1. p = 108 and q = −1
  2. p = 12 and q = 9
  3. p = 12 and q = −9
  4. p = 9 and q = −12
Correct answer: C
The product must be −108, so one number is negative. The sum must be +3, so the bigger number (12) is positive and the smaller (9) is negative: 12 + (−9) = 3 and 12 × (−9) = −108. 9 and −12 give a sum of −3.
Use after: Observe phase — Task C: expanding (x + 12)(x − 9) to confirm
Q3. Using the same pattern, a learner expands (x + 12)(x − 9) to check the factorisation of x² + 3x − 108. Which result is correct?
  1. x² + 3x − 108
  2. x² + 21x − 108
  3. x² − 3x − 108
  4. x² + 3x + 108
Correct answer: A
(x + 12)(x − 9) = x² − 9x + 12x − 108 = x² + 3x − 108. The middle term is −9x + 12x = +3x and the last term is 12 × (−9) = −108.
Use after: Explain phase — Worksheet: applying the Factorising Recipe to x² + 2x − 15
Q4. Factorise x² + 2x − 15.
  1. (x + 5)(x + 3)
  2. (x − 5)(x + 3)
  3. (x + 15)(x − 1)
  4. (x + 5)(x − 3)
Correct answer: D
We need two numbers with product −15 and sum +2. 5 and −3 work: 5 × (−3) = −15 and 5 + (−3) = 2. (x − 5)(x + 3) gives a middle term of −2x, and (x + 15)(x − 1) gives +14x.
Use after: Explain phase — Teacher move: addressing the misconception that (x + 3)(x + 5) = x² + 15
Q5. A learner says that (x + 3)(x + 5) = x² + 15 because 3 × 5 = 15. Which statement is correct?
  1. The correct expansion is x² + 2x + 15, because the numbers are subtracted.
  2. The correct expansion is x² + 15x + 8, because the numbers swap places.
  3. The learner is right, because only the last numbers are multiplied.
  4. The correct expansion is x² + 8x + 15, because the x terms 3x and 5x also add to the middle term.
Correct answer: D
Expanding gives x² + 5x + 3x + 15 = x² + 8x + 15. The two x strips of 3 and 5 make the middle term 8x, which is why p + q = b.
Use after: Explain phase — Worksheet: applying the Factorising Recipe to x² − 9x + 20
Q6. Factorise x² − 9x + 20 and choose the correct answer.
  1. (x + 4)(x + 5)
  2. (x − 2)(x − 10)
  3. (x − 4)(x + 5)
  4. (x − 4)(x − 5)
Correct answer: D
We need two numbers with product +20 and sum −9. Both must be negative: (−4) × (−5) = 20 and (−4) + (−5) = −9. Expanding (x − 4)(x − 5) gives x² − 9x + 20.
Use after: DQB phase — Explain and DQB: how does (x + 12)(x − 9) connect to Mama Wanjiru's garden?
Q7. Mama Wanjiru's garden has an area of 108 m² and its length is 3 m more than its width. The expression x² + 3x − 108 factorises as (x + 12)(x − 9). Which statement best links the numbers in the brackets to the garden?
  1. The width is 9 m and the length is 12 m, because 12 − 9 = 3 and 12 × 9 = 108.
  2. The width is −9 m and the length is 12 m, because those are the numbers in the brackets.
  3. The width is 3 m and the length is 36 m, because 3 × 36 = 108.
  4. The width is 12 m and the length is 9 m, because 12 comes first.
Correct answer: A
9 m by 12 m has area 9 × 12 = 108 m², and the length 12 is 3 more than the width 9. A width of 12 m with a length of 9 m would not fit 'length is 3 m more', and a length cannot be negative.
Use after: Model Building phase — Exit task: factorise x² − 13x + 36 and check by expanding
Q8. Factorise x² − 13x + 36 and check by expanding. Which factorisation is correct?
  1. (x + 4)(x + 9)
  2. (x − 6)(x − 6)
  3. (x − 4)(x − 9)
  4. (x − 3)(x − 12)
Correct answer: C
We need two numbers with product 36 and sum −13, so both are negative: (−4) × (−9) = 36 and (−4) + (−9) = −13. Expanding (x − 4)(x − 9) gives x² − 9x − 4x + 36 = x² − 13x + 36.
