Q1. On a Cartesian grid, triangle ABC has vertices A(1, 1), B(3, 1) and C(3, 4). It is enlarged with centre of enlargement the origin O(0, 0) and linear scale factor 2. What are the coordinates of C', the image of C?
  1. (5, 6)
  2. (6, 8)
  3. (6, 4)
  4. (3, 8)
Correct answer: B
With the origin as centre, Image = k × Point, so C' = 2 × (3, 4) = (6, 8). Both coordinates double; shifting by 2 or doubling only one coordinate are the common errors.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Step 2 — Negative L.S.F. demonstration (L.S.F. = −2 on triangle ABC, centre O)
Q2. Point B(3, 1) is enlarged with centre of enlargement the origin O(0, 0) and linear scale factor −2. What are the coordinates of the image B'?
  1. (−6, 2)
  2. (−6, −2)
  3. (6, 2)
  4. (−5, −1)
Correct answer: B
Image = −2 × (3, 1) = (−6, −2). Both coordinates are multiplied by −2, so both change sign and double in size.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: observation prompt (3) — Where is the image relative to the centre when L.S.F. is negative?
Q3. A point is enlarged from a centre of enlargement using a negative linear scale factor. Which statement about its image is correct?
  1. The image is on the opposite side of the centre from the object, at |L.S.F.| times the object's distance from the centre.
  2. The image is on the opposite side of the centre, but at the same distance as the object.
  3. The image is in the same quadrant as the object but smaller.
  4. The image is on the same side of the centre as the object, but further away.
Correct answer: A
A negative L.S.F. places the image on the opposite side of the centre. Its distance from the centre is |L.S.F.| times the object's distance, so the image is turned through 180° about the centre.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Problem 1 (positive L.S.F.) — water tank enclosure P, Q, R, S with centre C(1, 2) and L.S.F. = 3
Q4. Rectangle PQRS has vertices P(1, 2), Q(1, 4), R(3, 4) and S(3, 2). It is enlarged with centre C(1, 2) and linear scale factor 3. What are the coordinates of R', the image of R?
  1. (9, 12)
  2. (7, 2)
  3. (7, 8)
  4. (5, 6)
Correct answer: C
Image = Centre + k × (Object − Centre) = (1, 2) + 3 × (2, 2) = (1 + 6, 2 + 6) = (7, 8). Multiplying from the origin would wrongly give (9, 12).
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Problem 2 (negative L.S.F.) — school gate triangle E, F, G with centre O and L.S.F. = −2, and verifying lengths
Q5. On the school plan, the gate E(2, 1) and F(4, 1) are two corners of triangle EFG. The triangle is enlarged with centre O(0, 0) and linear scale factor −2. What is the length of the image E'F' in grid units?
  1. −4
  2. 8
  3. 4
  4. 2
Correct answer: C
Corresponding lengths are in the ratio |L.S.F.| : 1. EF = 4 − 2 = 2 units, so E'F' = |−2| × 2 = 4 units. A length cannot be negative.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: adding the coordinate grid overlay to the Kisumu School Plan Model (1 grid unit = 2 m)
Q6. The Kisumu school plan has scale 1 : 200, and the class lets 1 cm on the plan equal 1 grid unit, so 1 grid unit stands for 2 m on the real compound. The staffroom is at grid point (5, 8) and the gate is the origin. How far is the staffroom from the gate in the x-direction, in metres?
  1. 16 m
  2. 10 m
  3. 1000 m
  4. 5 m
Correct answer: B
1 grid unit = 2 m, so the x-distance is 5 × 2 = 10 m. The 16 m is the y-distance (8 × 2).
Use after: End of lesson — End of lesson: General Rule — Image = Centre + L.S.F. × (Object − Centre), when the centre is not the origin and L.S.F. is negative
Q7. The point (5, 3) is enlarged with centre of enlargement (2, 1) and linear scale factor −2. What are the coordinates of the image?
  1. (−10, −6)
  2. (8, 5)
  3. (−1, −1)
  4. (−4, −3)
Correct answer: D
Image = Centre + k × (Object − Centre) = (2, 1) + (−2) × (3, 2) = (2 − 6, 1 − 4) = (−4, −3). Using the origin as centre gives (−10, −6), and using L.S.F. +2 gives (8, 5).
