Q1. In the biuret test, a learner adds dilute sodium hydroxide and then a few drops of dilute copper sulphate to a sample of bean paste. What result shows that protein is present?
  1. The mixture turns blue-black
  2. The mixture turns brick-red
  3. The mixture turns violet or purple
  4. A translucent spot forms on paper
Correct answer: C
A violet or purple colour in the biuret test shows protein. Blue-black is the iodine test for starch, brick-red is the Benedict's test for sugar, and a translucent spot is the grease-spot test for lipids.
Use after: Observe phase — Test B (i): grease-spot test on groundnuts, mandazi, milk and ugali
Q2. A learner rubs crushed groundnuts on a piece of brown paper, dries it gently and holds it up to the light. Which observation shows that lipids are present?
  1. A cloudy white layer forms on the paper
  2. The paper turns violet
  3. A translucent (see-through) spot is left on the paper
  4. The paper stays completely dry and opaque
Correct answer: C
Lipids leave a greasy, translucent spot on paper that lets light through. Violet is the biuret test for protein, and a cloudy white layer belongs to the emulsion test, which is done in water, not on paper.
Use after: Observe phase — Test B (ii): emulsion test on groundnuts, mandazi, milk and ugali
Q3. In the emulsion test, a learner shakes a food sample with 2 cm3 of ethanol and pours the clear liquid into 2 cm3 of cold water. What result shows that the food contains lipids?
  1. The water turns blue-black
  2. The water turns violet
  3. A cloudy white layer forms
  4. The water stays clear and colourless
Correct answer: C
Lipids dissolve in ethanol but not in water. When the ethanol is poured into water, tiny droplets of lipid form a cloudy white emulsion. A clear liquid means no lipid.
Use after: Observe phase — Learners add a 'carbohydrate' column using Lesson 3 results (ugali blue-black with iodine) to compare
Q4. A class tests ugali paste. With iodine it turns blue-black. With the biuret test it stays pale blue. With the grease-spot test it leaves no translucent spot. What can the class conclude?
  1. Ugali contains starch, protein and lipid in equal amounts
  2. Ugali contains protein and lipid but no starch
  3. Ugali contains starch but little or no protein or lipid
  4. Ugali contains none of the three food chemicals
Correct answer: C
Blue-black with iodine means starch is present. A pale blue biuret result and no grease spot mean protein and lipid are absent or only in very small amounts.
Use after: Explain phase — Comparison table of carbohydrates, proteins and lipids (building blocks)
Q5. Which row correctly matches each food chemical with its building blocks?
  1. Carbohydrates: amino acids; proteins: glucose units; lipids: fatty acids and glycerol
  2. Carbohydrates: glucose units; proteins: amino acids; lipids: fatty acids and glycerol
  3. Carbohydrates: glucose units; proteins: fatty acids and glycerol; lipids: amino acids
  4. Carbohydrates: fatty acids and glycerol; proteins: amino acids; lipids: glucose units
Correct answer: B
Starch and other carbohydrates are made of glucose units, proteins of amino acids, and lipids of fatty acids and glycerol.
Use after: Explain phase — Claim-Evidence-Reasoning: 'Carbohydrates, not proteins or lipids, are the main cause of the spike from 4.5 to 8.0 mmol/L because...'
Q6. Wanjiru ate a meal with about 70 g of carbohydrate (mostly starch in ugali), about 12 g of protein and about 10 g of fat from cooking oil. Her blood glucose rose from 4.5 to 8.0 mmol/L. Which claim is best supported by this evidence?
  1. Protein caused the spike because beans are sweet
  2. Carbohydrates were the main cause of the spike because the meal had far more of them, and starch is made of glucose units
  3. All three caused the spike equally because they were all in the same meal
  4. Fat caused the spike because the cooking oil was used in the meal
Correct answer: B
The meal had about 70 g of carbohydrate compared with 12 g of protein and 10 g of fat. Carbohydrates are made of glucose units, whereas proteins and lipids are made of different building blocks, so carbohydrates are the main cause of the glucose rise.
Use after: Model Building phase — Revise the class flow-diagram model: thin dotted arrows from proteins and lipids to 'growth, repair and energy storage'
Q7. In the class flow-diagram model, thick arrows link carbohydrates to 'glucose in blood', and thin dotted arrows link proteins and lipids to other boxes. Which pair of labels belongs on the thin dotted arrows from proteins and from lipids?
  1. Proteins: growth and repair of body tissues; lipids: energy storage and insulation
  2. Proteins: the cause of the glucose spike; lipids: removal of glucose from the blood
  3. Proteins: energy storage and insulation; lipids: growth and repair of body tissues
  4. Proteins: main source of blood glucose; lipids: main source of blood glucose
Correct answer: A
Proteins mainly support growth and repair of body tissues, and lipids mainly store energy and provide insulation. Neither is the main source of the blood glucose rise.
