Use after: Observe phase — Data questions: By how much did glucose rise from 0 to 60 min?
Q1. Wanjiru ate ugali, sukuma wiki and beans after fasting. Her blood glucose (mmol/L) was measured at 0, 15, 30, 60, 90, 120, 150 and 180 minutes: 4.5, 5.5, 7.0, 8.0, 6.5, 5.2, 4.7, 4.5. By how much did her blood glucose rise from the start (0 min) to the peak?
  1. 8.0 mmol/L
  2. 60 mmol/L
  3. 3.5 mmol/L
  4. 12.5 mmol/L
Correct answer: C
The peak is 8.0 mmol/L at 60 min and the start is 4.5 mmol/L, so the rise is 8.0 - 4.5 = 3.5 mmol/L. 8.0 is the peak value itself, not the rise.
Use after: Observe phase — Data questions: rise from 0 to 60 min, about 78% above the start
Q2. Wanjiru's blood glucose was 4.5 mmol/L before her meal and reached a peak of 8.0 mmol/L at 60 minutes, a rise of 3.5 mmol/L. About what percentage above her starting level was the peak?
  1. About 178%
  2. About 3.5%
  3. About 78%
  4. About 44%
Correct answer: C
Percentage rise = rise ÷ starting value × 100 = 3.5 ÷ 4.5 × 100 ≈ 78%. Dividing by 8.0 gives 44%, which is the wrong base.
Use after: Observe phase — Data questions: Which is faster, rise or fall?
Q3. Wanjiru's blood glucose rose by 3.5 mmol/L between 0 and 60 minutes. After the peak it fell by 3.5 mmol/L between 60 and 180 minutes. Which statement about the rise and the fall is correct?
  1. The fall was faster, because it took more minutes.
  2. The rise was faster, because the same change took 60 minutes compared with 120 minutes.
  3. The rise and fall were equally fast, because both changed by 3.5 mmol/L.
  4. The fall was faster, because it ended at the starting value.
Correct answer: B
The same change of 3.5 mmol/L took 60 min going up but 120 min coming down, so the rise was quicker. Equal change does not mean equal speed, because time must be considered.
Use after: Observe phase — Data questions: When did glucose return to the normal fasting range of 4.0 to 5.5 mmol/L?
Q4. A normal fasting blood glucose range is 4.0 to 5.5 mmol/L. Wanjiru's readings after the peak of 8.0 mmol/L at 60 min were: 6.5 (90 min), 5.2 (120 min), 4.7 (150 min) and 4.5 (180 min). At which measurement time after the peak was her blood glucose first back in the normal fasting range?
  1. 60 minutes
  2. 120 minutes
  3. 180 minutes
  4. 90 minutes
Correct answer: B
At 90 min the value of 6.5 mmol/L is still above 5.5. At 120 min the value of 5.2 mmol/L is within 4.0 to 5.5, so that is the first time. It reaches her starting 4.5 mmol/L only at 180 min.
Use after: Explain phase — Claim-Evidence-Reasoning organiser: 'What I am not sure about yet'
Q5. In a Claim-Evidence-Reasoning task, a group is asked: 'What makes Wanjiru's blood glucose fall after the peak at 60 minutes?' At this point in the unit, which response is the most scientific?
  1. Glucose is burnt off in the stomach, so it falls.
  2. Glucose just disappears with time, so nothing needs explaining.
  3. The data show that it falls, but we do not yet know how. We need to investigate and add this to our questions.
  4. Glucose falls only because Wanjiru stopped eating, so nothing in the body is involved.
Correct answer: C
The lesson leaves the cause unexplained. The honest conclusion from the data is that something lowers glucose, but the process is not yet known and should be investigated. The other options are untested ideas that the data do not support.
Use after: Observe phase — Meal composition card
Q6. The meal card for Wanjiru's lunch says: ugali has about 70 g carbohydrate; beans have about 9 g protein and 23 g carbohydrate; sukuma wiki has about 3 g protein plus fibre, vitamins and mineral salts; cooking oil gives about 10 g fat. Which statement is supported by this card?
  1. Sukuma wiki is the main source of carbohydrate in the meal.
  2. The meal contains several kinds of food chemicals, including carbohydrate, protein and fat.
  3. Beans contain only protein.
  4. Ugali has no carbohydrate, because it is not sweet.
Correct answer: B
The card lists carbohydrate, protein, fat, vitamins and minerals in the meal. Ugali has the most carbohydrate (about 70 g), beans contain both protein and carbohydrate, and sukuma wiki has little carbohydrate listed.
Use after: Model Building phase — Initial flow-diagram model: Food chemicals → ? → Glucose in blood → ? → Back to normal
Q7. A learner draws this initial model: Food chemicals → ? → Glucose in blood (8.0 mmol/L at 60 min) → ? → Back to normal (4.5 mmol/L at 180 min). What do the question marks show?
  1. Steps that do not happen in the body.
  2. Mistakes in the model that must be rubbed out.
  3. Steps the learner does not yet understand. They will become questions to investigate and revise the model.
  4. Places where the teacher must give the correct answer immediately.
Correct answer: C
An initial model shows current thinking. The question marks mark gaps, which go onto the Driving Question Board and are revised in later lessons.
