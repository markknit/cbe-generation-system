Use after: Observe phase — Station A: Nutrient data
Q1. Wanjiru's meal has ugali (about 70 g carbohydrate), beans (about 20 g protein and 15 g carbohydrate), sukuma wiki (about 3 g protein plus fibre) and cooking oil (about 10 g fat). What is the total mass of carbohydrate in the meal?
  1. 70 g
  2. 85 g
  3. 105 g
  4. 95 g
Correct answer: B
Carbohydrate comes from ugali and beans only: 70 g + 15 g = 85 g. The other values leave out the beans or wrongly add protein or fat.
Use after: Observe phase — Station C: Burn test demonstration
Q2. Mr Otieno heats a small piece of dry maize flour in a flame and it turns black. Table salt heated in the same flame does not turn black. What does the black residue show?
  1. The maize flour contains salt, which turns black when heated
  2. The maize flour contains carbon, which is present in organic compounds
  3. The maize flour contains iron, which turns black when heated
  4. The maize flour contains water, which turns black when heated
Correct answer: B
Blackening (charring) is the carbon left behind when an organic food burns. Salt is inorganic and has no carbon, so it does not blacken.
Use after: Observe phase — Station B: Element cards
Q3. Starch (a carbohydrate) is made of C, H and O. Which element is found in proteins, such as those in beans and milk, but is NOT found in carbohydrates like starch?
  1. Oxygen (O)
  2. Nitrogen (N)
  3. Hydrogen (H)
  4. Carbon (C)
Correct answer: B
Proteins contain nitrogen (and sometimes sulphur) in addition to C, H and O. Carbohydrates contain only C, H and O.
Use after: Explain phase — Classification Chart: Organic and Inorganic columns
Q4. Which list contains ONLY organic compounds found in Wanjiru's meal?
  1. Protein, calcium salts, fat, starch
  2. Starch, water, fat, vitamin C
  3. Water, iron salts, calcium salts, protein
  4. Starch, protein, fat, vitamin C
Correct answer: D
Carbohydrates (starch), proteins, lipids (fat) and vitamins are organic. Water and mineral salts such as calcium and iron salts are inorganic, so they spoil the other lists.
Use after: Explain phase — Classification Chart: why water goes in the inorganic column
Q5. Water is found in every living thing and in all foods, yet it is placed in the inorganic column. Why?
  1. Water is a liquid and organic compounds are always solids
  2. Water is not a chemical, so it cannot be organic
  3. Water is made of only hydrogen and oxygen and has no carbon bonded to hydrogen
  4. Water is not found inside cells
Correct answer: C
Organic compounds contain carbon bonded to hydrogen. Water (H and O only) has no carbon, so it is inorganic even though living things need it.
Use after: Explain phase — Claim-Evidence-Reasoning statement
Q6. Wanjiru's blood glucose rose after she ate a meal containing about 70 g carbohydrate in ugali, about 20 g protein in beans and about 10 g fat from cooking oil. Which claim and evidence is best supported by this data?
  1. Water is most likely the cause, because it is found in all foods
  2. Protein is most likely the cause, because beans contain 20 g of it
  3. Fat is most likely the cause, because cooking oil is added to the meal
  4. Carbohydrate is most likely the cause, because the meal contains about 70 g of it in ugali, more than any other nutrient
Correct answer: D
The largest amount of any nutrient is carbohydrate (about 70 g in ugali), so it is the most likely link to the glucose rise. The other claims are not supported by the quantities given.
Use after: Model Building phase — Class model flow diagram: filling in the first box, Food chemicals
Q7. In the class model, the first box 'Food chemicals' is filled in, but the box 'Glucose in blood' is left as a question mark. Why?
  1. We know the kinds of chemicals in the lunch, but we do not yet know which one becomes glucose or how
  2. Carbohydrates have no link to the glucose rise
  3. Glucose is never found in blood
  4. The model is finished and no more boxes are needed
Correct answer: A
Models grow only with evidence. This lesson gives evidence about the groups of chemicals in food, not how they change into glucose, so that box stays open for later lessons.
