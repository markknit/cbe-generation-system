Q1. Before the diagram was shown, some learners predicted that animal cells also contain chloroplasts. Which statement is correct about chloroplasts?
  1. Chloroplasts are found only in animal cells because they need extra energy
  2. Chloroplasts are found in plant cells, where they capture sunlight to make food; animal cells such as cheek cells do not have them
  3. Chloroplasts release energy from glucose in both plant and animal cells
  4. Chloroplasts are found in both plant and animal cells because all cells need to make food
Correct answer: B
Chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis and are found in plant cells, not in animal cells, which get their food by eating. Releasing energy from glucose is the job of mitochondria.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Light vs Electron microscope two-row table
Q2. A learner looks at a plant cell through a school light microscope at about ×400. Which structure would they NOT be able to see clearly, and why?
  1. The whole cell, because light microscopes cannot magnify cells
  2. The nucleus, because it is too small to be separated by a light microscope
  3. Ribosomes, because they are too small to be separated by a light microscope
  4. The large central vacuole, because light cannot pass through water
Correct answer: C
A light microscope has limited resolution (about 0.2 micrometres), so it shows cells, the nucleus and the vacuole but not tiny ultrastructure such as ribosomes. An electron microscope is needed for those.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Light vs Electron microscope two-row table
Q3. Which pair correctly describes the difference between magnification and resolution?
  1. Magnification is how close two points can be and still be seen as two; resolution is how many times bigger the image is
  2. Magnification and resolution both mean how clear the colours are in the image
  3. Magnification is the brightness of the image; resolution is the size of the specimen
  4. Magnification is how many times bigger the image is; resolution is how close two points can be and still be seen as two separate points
Correct answer: D
Magnification is enlargement of the image. Resolution is the ability to show two close points as separate, which is why an electron microscope shows more detail.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Maize Farm Metaphor Table, Mitochondria line and misconception check
Q4. In the Rift Valley maize farm metaphor, the 'farm generator' is the organelle that releases energy from glucose. Which organelle is this, and does a plant cell need it?
  1. Golgi apparatus; plant cells need it to release energy from glucose
  2. Mitochondria; plant cells do not need them because chloroplasts already make their energy
  3. Chloroplast; plant cells need it because it releases energy from glucose
  4. Mitochondria; plant cells need them because chloroplasts make glucose but mitochondria release its energy
Correct answer: D
Chloroplasts make glucose using light, while mitochondria release energy from glucose through respiration. Plant cells need both, for example a seedling growing in the dark still needs energy.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Organelle Job Card matching task
Q5. A plant cell has a rigid structure outside its cell membrane that gives the cell shape, support and protection, like the perimeter fence of a farm. What is this structure?
  1. Rough endoplasmic reticulum
  2. Cell wall
  3. Large central vacuole
  4. Golgi apparatus
Correct answer: B
The cell wall lies outside the cell membrane and provides shape, support and protection. The membrane is the 'farm gate' that controls what enters and leaves.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Organelle Job Card matching task
Q6. A cell makes proteins that must be sorted, modified and sent to other parts of the cell, like goods in a packaging and dispatch depot. Which organelle plays this depot role?
  1. Ribosome
  2. Mitochondrion
  3. Golgi apparatus
  4. Nucleus
Correct answer: C
The Golgi apparatus sorts, modifies and ships proteins and lipids. Ribosomes build the proteins and the nucleus holds the DNA instructions.
Use after: DQB phase — DQB: connecting line about plant cells and Amina's epidermal cells (also the phenomenon reconnect in Explain)
Q7. The epidermal cells that grew back over a cut finger are animal cells with no chloroplasts. What is the best explanation?
  1. They do not need any energy to rebuild the skin, so they have no organelles
  2. They lost their chloroplasts because the cut damaged them
  3. They have chloroplasts but these are too small to see
  4. They do not photosynthesise because they get energy from food, so chloroplasts would be wasteful: structure matches function
Correct answer: D
Animal cells get energy from food and do not photosynthesise, so they have no chloroplasts. A cell has the organelles its job demands.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: drawing Version 2 – the Plant Cell
Q8. When drawing the Version 2 plant cell model, which drawing is correct?
  1. The cell membrane outside the cell wall, with a small vacuole at the edge
  2. No cell wall, a large central vacuole and chloroplasts only in the nucleus
  3. The cell wall outside the cell membrane, a large vacuole in the centre, and chloroplasts as oval structures inside the cell
  4. The cell wall inside the nucleus, with chloroplasts outside the cell
Correct answer: C
In a plant cell the cell wall is outside the membrane, the vacuole is large and central, and chloroplasts are distinct oval structures in the cytoplasm.
Use after: End of lesson — Model: red/green colour-coding of plant-only and shared organelles
Q9. Which organelle is found in plant cells but NOT in animal cells such as the human cheek cell?
  1. Ribosome
  2. Mitochondrion
  3. Nucleus
  4. Chloroplast
Correct answer: D
Chloroplasts (like the cell wall and large central vacuole) are plant features. Nucleus, mitochondria and ribosomes are shared with animal cells.
