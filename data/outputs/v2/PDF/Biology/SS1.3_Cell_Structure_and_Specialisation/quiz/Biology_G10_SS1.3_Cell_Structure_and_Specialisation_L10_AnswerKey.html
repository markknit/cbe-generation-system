Q1. Under a microscope, many flat epidermal cells of the same type are packed tightly together in layers at the edge of a cut finger. What do we call this group of similar cells working together to do one job?
  1. An organ system
  2. A single specialised cell
  3. An organ
  4. A tissue
Correct answer: D
Cells of the same type that group together to do one job form a tissue. Many epidermal cells together form epithelial tissue.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Side B Kenyan school analogy and ARES Observation Table
Q2. In the school analogy, one Grade 10 student is like one cell, and a Grade 10 class of 42 students is like a tissue. Which biological level matches Mwangaza Secondary School as a whole, with all its classes, teachers and departments working together?
  1. An organ system
  2. A cell
  3. An organ
  4. A tissue
Correct answer: C
A school is made of several different groups working together, like an organ made of different tissues. A class of similar students matches a tissue, not the whole school.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: Healing Cells strip on Side A of Evidence Card Set 10
Q3. Which cell in the healing cut finger is long and thin, has a large nucleus and plenty of rough endoplasmic reticulum, and makes collagen to rebuild the dermis?
  1. Epidermal cell
  2. Fibroblast
  3. Neutrophil
  4. Platelet
Correct answer: B
The fibroblast is the elongated cell that makes and secretes collagen to rebuild the dermis. Platelets help clotting, neutrophils engulf bacteria, and epidermal cells close the surface.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: teacher builds the five-level hierarchy diagram and discusses why skin is an organ
Q4. Why is the skin called an organ and not just a tissue?
  1. Because it is a group of organs, including hair, nails and sweat glands
  2. Because it is made only of epidermal cells packed in layers
  3. Because it is made of a very large number of cells
  4. Because it is made of more than one tissue, such as epithelial tissue and connective tissue, working together
Correct answer: D
An organ is made of different tissues working together. The epidermis is epithelial tissue and the dermis (rebuilt by fibroblasts) is connective tissue, with blood vessels and nerves running through them.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: hierarchy diagram and the prompt card on organ versus organ system
Q5. Which of these is an ORGAN SYSTEM?
  1. A fibroblast
  2. The epidermis
  3. The integumentary system (skin, hair, nails and sweat glands)
  4. The skin
Correct answer: C
An organ system is a group of organs working together. The integumentary system combines the skin with hair, nails and sweat glands. The skin alone is one organ.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: individual 5-sentence written explanation of why a single epidermal cell cannot heal a wound on its own
Q6. A Grade 10 student says: 'A single epidermal cell can heal a cut on a finger by itself.' Which response best corrects this claim?
  1. Each epidermal cell has different DNA, so they cannot work together
  2. Only the whole organism can heal, and cells, tissues and organs play no part
  3. Healing needs different cell types, such as platelets, neutrophils, fibroblasts and epidermal cells, working together within tissues, the skin and the body
  4. Epidermal cells have no nucleus, so they cannot divide
Correct answer: C
No single cell does every job. Different specialised cells work inside tissues, tissues form the skin, and the systems of the organism work together to restore the finger. Epidermal cells do have an active nucleus and divide to close the wound.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: Version 4 hierarchy diagram nesting cells inside tissue, organ, organ system and organism
Q7. A learner is drawing Model Version 4 to show how an epidermal cell in a cut finger fits into the levels of organisation. Which nesting shows the correct order from smallest to largest?
  1. Epidermal cell → epithelial tissue → skin → integumentary system → human body
  2. Epithelial tissue → epidermal cell → skin → human body → integumentary system
  3. Epidermal cell → skin → epithelial tissue → integumentary system → human body
  4. Epidermal cell → epithelial tissue → integumentary system → skin → human body
Correct answer: A
The order is cell, tissue, organ, organ system, organism. So the epidermal cell forms epithelial tissue, which is part of the skin (organ), then the integumentary system, then the whole human body.
