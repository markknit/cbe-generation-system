Use after: Predict phase — Think-Pair-Share on the blank animal cell outline and the Prediction Wall (confident vs unsure organelles)
Q1. Which of these organelles is found in BOTH plant cells and animal cells?
  1. Large central vacuole
  2. Cell wall
  3. Chloroplast
  4. Mitochondrion
Correct answer: D
Mitochondria release energy in both plant and animal cells. Chloroplasts, the cell wall and the large central vacuole are found only in plant cells.
Use after: Observe phase — Three-column Comparison Table (plant vs animal organelles) completed in groups of 4
Q2. A group fills in its Comparison Table and writes a tick for 'large central vacuole' in the animal cell column. What is the correct statement?
  1. Animal cells have a large central vacuole but no small ones.
  2. Animal cells have a large central vacuole, just like plant cells.
  3. Animal cells have only small, temporary vacuoles. The large central vacuole belongs to plant cells.
  4. Animal cells have no vacuoles of any kind.
Correct answer: C
Animal cells have small, numerous vacuoles. The large central vacuole stores water and maintains turgor in plant cells, so it should be marked absent in animal cells.
Use after: Explain phase — Guided explanation: return to the phenomenon, 'What organelle makes that digestion possible?' (lysosomes)
Q3. When a Grade 10 student cuts a finger, neutrophils arrive at the wound and engulf bacteria. Which organelle in the neutrophil digests the engulfed bacteria?
  1. Mitochondrion, which releases energy from glucose
  2. Lysosome, a membrane-bound sac of digestive enzymes
  3. Centriole, which organises spindle fibres
  4. Ribosome, which joins amino acids to make proteins
Correct answer: B
Lysosomes contain digestive enzymes that break down engulfed bacteria. The other organelles have different jobs: energy release, protein making and cell division.
Use after: Explain phase — Guided explanation: centrioles and epidermal cells dividing to close the wound
Q4. Epidermal (skin) cells divide rapidly to close the surface of a cut finger. Which organelle found in animal cells helps organise the spindle fibres during this cell division?
  1. Chloroplast
  2. Centriole
  3. Golgi apparatus
  4. Lysosome
Correct answer: B
Centrioles organise the spindle fibres during animal cell division. Lysosomes digest material, chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis, and the Golgi apparatus processes and packages substances.
Use after: Explain phase — Guided explanation: cell wall, 'Could it do that with a rigid cell wall?'
Q5. A neutrophil squeezes through the wall of a capillary to reach a cut on the finger. Why is it important that animal cells have NO cell wall?
  1. Without a cell wall, the cell can make its own food by photosynthesis.
  2. Without a cell wall, the cell does not need a cell membrane.
  3. Without a cell wall, the cell can change shape and move through narrow gaps.
  4. Without a cell wall, the cell can store large amounts of water for support.
Correct answer: C
Animal cells are flexible because they lack a rigid cell wall, so a neutrophil can change shape to pass through the capillary wall. Photosynthesis and water storage are not linked to the absence of a cell wall, and every cell still has a membrane.
Use after: Explain phase — Written 3-sentence Functional Justification: why an animal cell does not need a chloroplast
Q6. Skin cells in a person's finger have no chloroplasts. What is the best explanation?
  1. Skin cells get energy from glucose carried in the blood, so they do not photosynthesise and do not need chloroplasts.
  2. Skin cells are too small to hold chloroplasts.
  3. Chloroplasts are destroyed when the skin is cut.
  4. Skin cells get their energy directly from sunlight using mitochondria.
Correct answer: A
Animal cells do not make their own food by photosynthesis. They obtain glucose from the bloodstream and release its energy in mitochondria, so chloroplasts would serve no purpose.
Use after: Model Building phase — Version 3 side-by-side Plant vs Animal model with colour-coding (blue animal-only, red plant-only, green shared)
Q7. In a Version 3 model, a learner colours the organelles found ONLY in animal cells (not in plant cells) blue. Which pair of organelles should be coloured blue?
  1. Ribosomes and large central vacuole
  2. Nucleus and mitochondria
  3. Lysosomes and centrioles
  4. Chloroplasts and cell wall
Correct answer: C
Lysosomes and centrioles are the organelles unique to animal cells. Chloroplasts, the cell wall and the large central vacuole are plant-only, while the nucleus, mitochondria and ribosomes are shared.
