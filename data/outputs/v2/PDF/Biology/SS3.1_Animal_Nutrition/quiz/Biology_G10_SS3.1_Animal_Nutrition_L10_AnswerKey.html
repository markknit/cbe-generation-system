Q1. On a maize farm in Kakamega, a mosquito can drink a person's blood but a grasshopper cannot. Which explanation is best?
  1. The mosquito has a piercing-sucking proboscis that reaches liquid blood, while the grasshopper has chewing mandibles made for solid plant tissue.
  2. Both insects have the same mouthparts, but the mosquito is lighter and so can reach blood.
  3. The grasshopper has no stomach, so it cannot digest any food.
  4. The mosquito has sharp teeth like a human, so it can bite through skin.
Correct answer: A
Mouthpart structure decides what food an insect can reach. The mosquito's proboscis pierces skin and sucks liquid blood, while the grasshopper's mandibles chew solid leaves.

Q2. At Lake Nakuru, a flamingo sweeps its bent beak sideways through the water. A fish eagle on a branch tears a fish apart with its hooked beak. What best explains why their beaks are so different?
  1. The flamingo's beak is hooked at the tip for catching fish, while the eagle's beak has lamellae for filtering water.
  2. The flamingo's beak has lamellae that filter tiny food from water, while the eagle's hooked beak is adapted for tearing flesh.
  3. Both birds eat the same food, so their beaks only look different.
  4. Beak shape is random and has no link to the food an animal eats.
Correct answer: B
Each beak is a structural adaptation to diet and feeding strategy. Lamellae filter small food from water, and a hooked beak tears flesh.
Use after: Observe phase — Gallery Walk: checking that models show the aphid stylet linked to structure, function and nutrient type
Q3. Aphids cluster on maize stems. The leaves stay whole, but the plant wilts. Which statement best explains this?
  1. The aphids chew the stem with strong mandibles and swallow solid pieces.
  2. The aphids use needle-like stylets to pierce the stem and suck out plant sap, so the plant loses fluid and nutrients without leaf tissue being chewed.
  3. The aphids filter tiny particles from the air using lamellae.
  4. The aphids drink blood from the plant's roots using a proboscis.
Correct answer: B
The aphid's stylet is a needle-like mouthpart that taps sap, so it takes liquid food without making ragged chewing holes like the grasshopper.

Q4. What is the main role of villi in the human small intestine?
  1. They greatly increase the surface area so digested nutrients are absorbed into the bloodstream quickly.
  2. They make stomach acid that kills germs in food.
  3. They absorb water only from undigested waste.
  4. They churn food to break it into smaller pieces by mechanical digestion.
Correct answer: A
Villi are finger-like folds that increase the surface area of the small intestine. This lets digested nutrients be absorbed into the blood efficiently.
Use after: Explain phase — Part A: Final Explanation (CER+Evidence), reasoning from food type to digestion to absorption to survival
Q5. A learner eats githeri (maize and beans). Which statement correctly describes what happens to the protein in the beans?
  1. Protease enzymes digest the protein into amino acids, which villi absorb into the bloodstream for growth and cell repair.
  2. The protein is absorbed in the mouth, so no enzymes are needed.
  3. The protein is changed into glucose in the large intestine and used only for energy.
  4. Teeth chew the protein into small pieces, and these whole pieces are absorbed directly into the blood.
Correct answer: A
Chemical digestion by protease enzymes breaks protein into amino acids, which are small enough to be absorbed through the villi into the blood. Chewing only breaks food into smaller pieces.
Use after: Explain phase — Part B: Kenyan Food Cost-Nutrition Analysis (githeri, sukuma wiki, ugali, mandazi, nyama choma)
Q6. A family in Nairobi has a small budget. Approximate prices are: githeri KES 30 per plate, sukuma wiki KES 10 per bunch, ugali KES 20 per plate, mandazi KES 5 per piece, nyama choma KES 150 per portion. They want a meal with protein and also iron and vitamins. Which choice is best?
  1. Nyama choma only, because the most expensive food must be the most nutritious overall.
  2. Githeri with sukuma wiki, because together they give energy, plant protein, iron and vitamins at a low cost.
  3. Mandazi only, because it is the cheapest and is a complete food.
  4. Ugali with mandazi, because they give the most energy for the least money.
Correct answer: B
No single food is complete. Githeri gives energy and plant protein, and sukuma wiki is a cheap source of iron and vitamins, so the pair costs only KES 40 and is better value than the other options.
Use after: DQB phase — Driving Question Board closure: Key Inquiry Question 1 and the question 'What is the unifying principle across ALL of these?'
Q7. The class has described the aphid stylet, grasshopper mandibles, mosquito proboscis, flamingo lamellae, fish eagle beak and human teeth. Which unifying idea links all of them?
  1. Only animals with teeth can obtain nutrients from their food.
  2. Each structure is adapted to the animal's diet and feeding strategy, helping it get the nutrients it needs to survive.
  3. Feeding structures vary at random and are not linked to food type.
  4. All animals have the same mouthparts, which only look different in size.
Correct answer: B
The unit's unifying principle is that structure enables nutrient acquisition. Different structures suit different diets, but all serve the same purpose of getting nutrients for survival, growth and energy.
