Q1. A customer at Otieno's kiosk asks whether a packet of old biscuits is still fine to eat. Which item on the packet label would best tell the customer if the biscuits may no longer be safe?
  1. The picture of biscuits on the front
  2. The weight of the packet
  3. The expiry date
  4. The name of the factory
Correct answer: C
The expiry date shows when a product may no longer be safe or effective, which is what the customer needs to know. The other items do not tell how long the biscuits stay safe.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: label kit inspection, column 'KEBS Diamond Mark?'
Q2. A learner inspecting a juice carton finds the KEBS Diamond Mark printed on it. What does this mark tell the buyer?
  1. The juice is cheaper than other brands
  2. The juice contains no preservatives
  3. The juice will never go bad
  4. The product has met quality standards set by the Kenya Bureau of Standards
Correct answer: D
The KEBS Diamond Mark shows that a product has met quality standards. It does not mean the product lasts forever, and it says nothing about price or about preservatives.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: comparing food labels (maize flour, juice) with medicine labels in the label observation table
Q3. Learners compare the label on a maize flour packet with the label on a paracetamol 500 mg box. Which information would they expect to find on the paracetamol box but NOT on the maize flour packet?
  1. A list of ingredients
  2. The dose and a warning that too much can damage the liver
  3. An expiry date
  4. The name of the manufacturer
Correct answer: B
Food labels list ingredients, preservatives and nutritional information. Medicine labels carry the active ingredient, dose and cautions. Both types can have an expiry date and a manufacturer name.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: calculating the daily maximum for paracetamol (8 x 500 mg = 4000 mg = 4 g)
Q4. The paracetamol label says: 'Adult dose: 1 to 2 tablets of 500 mg every 4 to 6 hours. Maximum 8 tablets in 24 hours.' What is the greatest total mass of paracetamol an adult may take in 24 hours, in grams?
  1. 4 g
  2. 8 g
  3. 0.5 g
  4. 40 g
Correct answer: A
8 tablets x 500 mg = 4000 mg. Since 1000 mg = 1 g, this is 4 g in 24 hours.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: answering Otieno's kiosk question about giving a small child 2 tablets
Q5. A customer wants to give a small child 2 tablets of paracetamol 500 mg, because 2 tablets is within the adult dose. Why is this unsafe?
  1. A child's dose is worked out from body weight and is much smaller, so an adult dose can overload a child's liver
  2. Paracetamol only works for adults, so it does nothing in a child
  3. The tablets will not dissolve in a child's stomach
  4. Two tablets is always too few to have any effect
Correct answer: A
A child's dose depends on body weight and is much smaller than an adult dose. An adult dose can overload the child's liver, so the buyer should ask a health worker or pharmacist.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: why antacid F and paracetamol G need different labels
Q6. A shopkeeper mixes up an antacid tablet and a paracetamol tablet because both look like plain white tablets. Why is this a problem?
  1. Paracetamol neutralises stomach acid and the antacid relieves pain
  2. Only the antacid can be harmful if taken in excess
  3. The two tablets do the same job, so the mix-up only wastes money
  4. Antacid neutralises excess stomach acid for heartburn, while paracetamol relieves pain and fever, so the wrong tablet will not treat the problem and too much of either can harm the body
Correct answer: D
The two tablets have different jobs and different cautions, which is why each needs its own label. An antacid taken too often upsets the body's salts, and too much paracetamol can damage the liver.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model Building: adding the layer 'Labels and consumer protection' to class model v2
Q7. Learners add 'Labels and consumer protection' to their class model. On the paracetamol label, what does the warning 'maximum 8 tablets in 24 hours' connect?
  1. The colour of the tablet to its price
  2. The taste of the tablet to the way it is made
  3. The factory's address to the expiry date
  4. The property of the substance in the body (too much can damage the liver) to a safe amount for the consumer
Correct answer: D
The model links a property of the substance (excess damages the liver) to label information (the maximum dose) and then to safe use by the consumer.
