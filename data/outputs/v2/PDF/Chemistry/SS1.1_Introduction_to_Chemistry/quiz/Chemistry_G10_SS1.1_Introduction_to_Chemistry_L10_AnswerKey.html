Q1. Mixture M had 3 g of iron filings, 3 g of sand and 3 g of salt (9 g in all). A group separated it and recovered 2.9 g of iron, 2.8 g of sand and 2.7 g of salt. How much mass was lost during the separation?
  1. 0.6 g
  2. 0.4 g
  3. 0.9 g
  4. 8.4 g
Correct answer: A
Total recovered = 2.9 + 2.8 + 2.7 = 8.4 g, so the loss = 9 - 8.4 = 0.6 g. The loss is from spillage and from material sticking to equipment.
Use after: Observe phase — Part A: evidence review (which property lets you separate the iron from mixture M)
Q2. A mixture contains iron filings, sand and salt. Which property of iron lets us remove it from the mixture first, before using water?
  1. Iron is attracted by a magnet
  2. Iron dissolves in water
  3. Iron melts easily when heated
  4. Iron fizzes in vinegar
Correct answer: A
Iron is magnetic, so a magnet pulls it out of the dry mixture. Sand and salt are not attracted by a magnet.
Use after: Observe phase — Part A: evidence review (which property tells you the salt is still in the water)
Q3. Iron has been removed from a mixture of sand and salt. Water is added and the mixture is stirred and filtered. Sand stays on the filter paper and salt passes through with the water. Why?
  1. Salt is magnetic but sand is not
  2. Both sand and salt dissolve, but sand is heavier
  3. Sand dissolves in water but salt does not
  4. Salt dissolves in water but sand does not dissolve
Correct answer: D
Salt dissolves in water, so it passes through the filter paper as a solution. Sand does not dissolve, so it is trapped on the paper. Salt is then recovered by evaporation.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: safe and healthy environment (maximum paracetamol dose of 8 x 500 mg = 4 g in 24 hours)
Q4. The label on a paracetamol packet says the maximum dose for an adult is 8 tablets of 500 mg in 24 hours. What is this maximum in grams?
  1. 40 g
  2. 8 g
  3. 0.4 g
  4. 4 g
Correct answer: D
8 x 500 mg = 4000 mg. Since 1000 mg = 1 g, the maximum is 4 g in 24 hours.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: rights and responsibilities brainstorm and gallery presentation (linking to lab safety rules)
Q5. Which responsibility of a learner helps keep a school laboratory safe and healthy?
  1. Tasting substances to find out what they are
  2. Following lab rules such as wearing goggles and not tasting substances
  3. Taking off goggles once the heating has started
  4. Heating substances alone when the teacher is away
Correct answer: B
Safety rules such as wearing goggles, not tasting and heating only with supervision are the learner's responsibilities. They protect the learner and others in the laboratory.
Use after: Explain phase — Step 2: explain the medicine puzzle (Wanjiru's question about the antacid and paracetamol)
Q6. Why does an antacid tablet relieve heartburn while a paracetamol tablet relieves pain?
  1. Both tablets neutralise stomach acid, but paracetamol does it more slowly
  2. The antacid acts on pain and fever, while paracetamol neutralises stomach acid
  3. The antacid neutralises excess stomach acid, while paracetamol acts on pain and fever and does not neutralise acid
  4. They act in the same way, but the antacid is a bigger tablet
Correct answer: C
The antacid (calcium carbonate) reacts with excess stomach acid, which is why it fizzes in acid such as vinegar. Paracetamol has a different action on pain and fever and does not fizz in acid.
Use after: Explain phase — Step 1: Claim-Evidence-Reasoning (CER) chart on why A-H behave differently
Q7. Salt, sand, sulphur and iron filings all look like powders, but they behave very differently in water, in heat and near a magnet. What is the best explanation?
  1. Each substance has its own properties because of what it is made of
  2. They behave differently only because their colours are different
  3. They behave differently because they were bought from different shops
  4. They behave differently only because the grains are different sizes
Correct answer: A
Properties such as solubility, magnetism and melting behaviour come from what each substance is made of. This is the claim and reasoning in the Final Explanation, and it is why chemists can choose or separate materials by their properties.
Use after: Explain phase — Step 2-3: probe on a friend taking six paracetamol tablets at once (using the label and dosage)
Q8. A friend says: "I took six paracetamol tablets (500 mg each) at once because my head hurts a lot." What is the best advice?
  1. Follow the label dose of 1 to 2 tablets every 4 to 6 hours with a maximum of 8 tablets in 24 hours, because too much can harm the liver
  2. It is fine because paracetamol is sold in shops, so it is safe in any amount
  3. Children and adults can take the same dose, so there is nothing to worry about
  4. Take even more tablets, because a larger dose works faster
Correct answer: A
The label gives the correct dose. Taking too much paracetamol (over 4 g in 24 hours) can damage the liver, and being sold in a shop does not make a medicine safe in any amount.
Use after: Model Building phase — Step 2: redraw the final model, part (a) what the substances are made of
Q9. Which statement correctly classifies the substances from the Kisumu tray?
  1. Sulphur and iron are elements, while table salt and water are compounds
  2. Sulphur and iron are compounds, while table salt and water are elements
  3. Salt is an element because it dissolves in water
  4. Mixture M of iron, sand and salt is a compound because it was separated
Correct answer: A
Sulphur and iron are elements. Table salt, water and sugar are compounds. Mixture M (iron, sand and salt) is a mixture whose parts can be separated by their properties.
