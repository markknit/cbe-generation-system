Q1. Learners divided sin θ by cos θ for θ = 20°, 30°, 40°, 50°, 60° and 70°, and compared each answer with the tan θ value in their table. Every row matched, apart from tiny rounding differences. What is the best conclusion from this pattern?
  1. sin θ ÷ cos θ is never equal to tan θ, but the values are close
  2. sin θ ÷ cos θ is sometimes equal to tan θ, depending on the angle
  3. sin θ ÷ cos θ is equal to tan θ for every acute angle tested, so it looks like an identity
  4. sin θ ÷ cos θ is equal to cos θ ÷ sin θ for these angles
Correct answer: C
All six rows agreed, which suggests sin θ ÷ cos θ = tan θ is always true for acute angles. The lesson then proves this algebraically.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: teacher's board proof of tan θ = sin θ / cos θ
Q2. In a right-angled triangle, sin θ = O/H and cos θ = A/H, where O is the opposite side, A the adjacent side and H the hypotenuse. What is (O/H) ÷ (A/H) after simplifying?
  1. O/A
  2. (O × A)/H
  3. A/O
  4. O/H
Correct answer: A
(O/H) ÷ (A/H) = (O/H) × (H/A). The H cancels, leaving O/A, which is the definition of tan θ.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Problem C, finding tan 50° from sin 50° and cos 50°
Q3. A Kenya Power engineer's calculator has a broken tan button. Her notebook says sin 50° = 0.766 and cos 50° = 0.643. Using tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ, what is tan 50° to 3 decimal places?
  1. 0.839
  2. 1.191
  3. 1.409
  4. 0.492
Correct answer: B
tan 50° = sin 50° ÷ cos 50° = 0.766 ÷ 0.643 ≈ 1.191.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Problem C, computing the Nakuru mast height with distance 40 m
Q4. The angle of elevation to the top of a mast is 50°, and the horizontal distance from the observer to the base is 40 m. Using tan 50° = sin 50° ÷ cos 50° = 0.766 ÷ 0.643 and height = tan θ × horizontal distance, what is the height of the mast to 1 decimal place?
  1. 47.7 m
  2. 25.7 m
  3. 33.6 m
  4. 30.6 m
Correct answer: A
Height = 40 × (0.766 ÷ 0.643) = 40 × 1.1913 ≈ 47.7 m. Multiplying 40 by sin alone, or by cos alone, or dividing 40 by tan, gives the wrong answers.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Problem A, given sin θ = 3/5, find cos θ and tan θ
Q5. θ is an acute angle in a right-angled triangle and sin θ = 3/5. Without a calculator, what are cos θ and tan θ?
  1. cos θ = 4/5 and tan θ = 3/4
  2. cos θ = 4/5 and tan θ = 4/3
  3. cos θ = 2/5 and tan θ = 3/2
  4. cos θ = 3/5 and tan θ = 1
Correct answer: A
With opposite 3 and hypotenuse 5, the adjacent side is 4 (3-4-5 triangle), so cos θ = 4/5. Then tan θ = (3/5) ÷ (4/5) = 3/4.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Problem B, given tan 35° and cos 35°, find sin 35°
Q6. For an angle of 35°, tan 35° = 0.700 and cos 35° = 0.819. Using tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ, what is sin 35° to 3 decimal places?
  1. 1.519
  2. 0.855
  3. 0.573
  4. 1.170
Correct answer: C
Rearranging tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ gives sin θ = tan θ × cos θ = 0.700 × 0.819 ≈ 0.573.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model Building: extension task on a mast twice as tall at the same distance
Q7. A mast is 95.4 m tall, and an observer stands 40 m from its base. Using tan θ = height ÷ distance, what is tan θ for the angle of elevation θ to the top?
  1. 2.385
  2. 135.4
  3. 55.4
  4. 0.419
Correct answer: A
tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent = height ÷ distance = 95.4 ÷ 40 = 2.385. Dividing distance by height gives the inverted value 0.419.
