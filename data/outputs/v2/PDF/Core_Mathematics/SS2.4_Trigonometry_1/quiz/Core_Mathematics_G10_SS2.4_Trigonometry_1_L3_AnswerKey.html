Q1. Using mathematical tables, a pair finds that sin 25° = 0.4226. Which of these must also be equal to 0.4226?
  1. cos 25°
  2. cos 65°
  3. sin 65°
  4. tan 25°
Correct answer: B
25° and 65° add up to 90°, so they are complementary and sin 25° = cos(90° − 25°) = cos 65°. The others are different ratios or different angles.

Q2. Which pair of angles is complementary, so that the sine of one angle equals the cosine of the other?
  1. 20° and 160°
  2. 42° and 138°
  3. 35° and 65°
  4. 42° and 48°
Correct answer: D
Complementary angles add to 90°, and 42° + 48° = 90°. The other pairs add to 180° or 100°, so they are not complementary.

Q3. In a right-angled triangle ABC, the right angle is at C, angle A = θ and angle B = 90° − θ. Side a is opposite angle A, side b is opposite angle B, and side c is the hypotenuse. Which ratio equals sin(90° − θ)?
  1. b/c
  2. a/b
  3. b/a
  4. a/c
Correct answer: A
From corner B, the opposite side is b, so sin(90° − θ) = b/c. This is the same ratio as cos θ, because b is adjacent to angle A.

Q4. Which of these is equal to cos 70°?
  1. sin 20°
  2. sin 110°
  3. sin 35°
  4. sin 70°
Correct answer: A
cos 70° = sin(90° − 70°) = sin 20°. Writing sin 70° ignores the change to the complementary angle.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: Practice set (B) Solve for θ: sin(2θ + 10°) = cos(θ + 20°)
Q5. Given that sin(2θ + 10°) = cos(θ + 20°), what is the value of θ?
  1. 30°
  2. 20°
  3. 10°
  4. 40°
Correct answer: B
The angles must be complementary, so (2θ + 10°) + (θ + 20°) = 90°. This gives 3θ + 30° = 90°, so 3θ = 60° and θ = 20°.

Q6. Which statement about sine and cosine of complementary angles is correct?
  1. cos 65° = cos 25°
  2. sin 65° = cos 65°
  3. sin 65° = sin 25°
  4. sin 65° = cos 25°
Correct answer: D
sin θ = cos(90° − θ), so sin 65° = cos 25°. The relationship swaps sine and cosine; it does not keep the same ratio for both angles.

Q7. A mast stands on level ground. From a point 40 m from its base, the angle of elevation to the top is 50°. In the right-angled triangle formed, the angle at the top of the mast is 40°. Why can an engineer use either the 50° angle or the 40° angle to work out the mast height?
  1. Because sin 50° = sin 40° in the same triangle
  2. Because the tangent of 50° and the tangent of 40° are equal
  3. Because 50° and 40° are equal angles
  4. Because the two angles are complementary, so the sine of one equals the cosine of the other and both give the same triangle relationships
Correct answer: D
The two acute angles add up to 90°, so they are complementary. This means sin 50° = cos 40° and cos 50° = sin 40°, so either angle describes the same triangle.
