Use after: Predict phase — Predict: Think-Pair-Share on the Nakuru mast (40 m, about 50°)
Q1. A student stands 40 m from the base of a mast and measures the angle of elevation to the top as 50°. Let h be the height of the mast in metres. The 40 m side is next to the 50° angle, and h is opposite it. Which equation should be used to find h?
  1. sin(50°) = h/40
  2. tan(50°) = 40/h
  3. cos(50°) = h/40
  4. tan(50°) = h/40
Correct answer: D
The known side (40 m) is adjacent to the angle and the unknown height is opposite it. The ratio that links opposite and adjacent is tangent, so tan(50°) = h/40.
Use after: Observe phase — Part A: Diagram Construction (elevation and depression diagrams)
Q2. Which statement correctly describes an angle of depression?
  1. The angle measured downward from the horizontal to the line of sight
  2. The angle between the ground and the observer's feet
  3. The angle measured between the line of sight and the vertical line through the observer
  4. The angle measured upward from the horizontal to the line of sight
Correct answer: A
An angle of depression is measured downward from the observer's horizontal line to the line of sight to an object below. The upward version is the angle of elevation.
Use after: Observe phase — Part B: Worked Demonstration (Fort Jesus cliff depression example, 20 m and 35°)
Q3. A person stands at the top of the Fort Jesus cliff in Mombasa, 20 m above sea level. The angle of depression to a dhow at sea level is 35°. What is the horizontal distance from the base of the cliff to the dhow, to 1 decimal place?
  1. 34.9 m
  2. 28.6 m
  3. 24.4 m
  4. 14.0 m
Correct answer: B
The cliff height (20 m) is opposite the 35° angle and the distance d is adjacent. tan(35°) = 20/d, so d = 20 ÷ tan(35°) = 20 ÷ 0.7002 ≈ 28.6 m.
Use after: Explain phase — Problem 1 (Elevation — Nairobi): flagpole in Uhuru Park
Q4. A visitor at Uhuru Park, Nairobi stands 15 m from the base of a flagpole. The angle of elevation to the top of the flagpole is 62°. What is the height of the flagpole, to 1 decimal place?
  1. 28.2 m
  2. 13.2 m
  3. 8.0 m
  4. 7.0 m
Correct answer: A
The distance of 15 m is adjacent and the height is opposite, so h = 15 × tan(62°) = 15 × 1.8807 ≈ 28.2 m.
Use after: Explain phase — Problem 2 (Elevation — Kisumu): surveyor and five-storey building
Q5. A surveyor in Kisumu stands 42 m (horizontal distance) from a five-storey building. The angle of elevation to the top of the building is 38°. What is the height of the building, to 1 decimal place?
  1. 53.8 m
  2. 32.8 m
  3. 25.9 m
  4. 33.1 m
Correct answer: B
The 42 m is adjacent to the angle and the height is opposite, so h = 42 × tan(38°) = 42 × 0.7813 ≈ 32.8 m.
Use after: Explain phase — Problem 3 (Depression — Mombasa): tourist at Fort Jesus cliff and dhow
Q6. A tourist stands at the top of a cliff 27 m above sea level. She sees a dhow at sea level at an angle of depression of 41°. Let d be the horizontal distance from the base of the cliff to the dhow. Which working correctly finds d?
  1. d = 27 ÷ sin(41°) ≈ 41.2 m
  2. d = 27 × tan(41°) ≈ 23.5 m
  3. d = 27 ÷ tan(41°) ≈ 31.1 m
  4. d = 27 × cos(41°) ≈ 20.4 m
Correct answer: C
The cliff height (27 m) is opposite the 41° angle and d is adjacent. tan(41°) = 27/d, so d = 27 ÷ tan(41°) = 27 ÷ 0.8693 ≈ 31.1 m. Writing tan(41°) = d/27 is the common error.
Use after: Model Building phase — Cumulative right-triangle model: depression inset labelled 'Angle of Depression = Angle of Elevation (alternate interior angles)'
Q7. From the top of a cliff, the angle of depression to a boat on the sea is 41°. What is the angle of elevation from the boat to the person at the top of the cliff?
  1. 139°
  2. 90°
  3. 49°
  4. 41°
Correct answer: D
The horizontal line at the cliff top and the horizontal line at sea level are parallel. Alternate interior angles are equal, so the angle of elevation from the boat equals the angle of depression from the cliff top, which is 41°.
