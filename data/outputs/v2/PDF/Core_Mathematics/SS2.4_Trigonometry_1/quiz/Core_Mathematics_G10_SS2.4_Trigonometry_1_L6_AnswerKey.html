Q1. A ramp at Kisumu County Referral Hospital makes a 12° angle with the ground. The horizontal distance along the ground is 6 m. You want the length of the ramp surface. Which ratio should you use?
  1. Cosine, because the known side is adjacent to 12° and the ramp is the hypotenuse
  2. Sine, because the ramp is the side opposite the 12° angle
  3. Tangent, because the ground distance is the side opposite the 12° angle
  4. Tangent, because a ramp is a slope
Correct answer: A
The 6 m ground distance is adjacent to the 12° angle and the ramp surface is the hypotenuse. Adjacent and hypotenuse means CAH, so cosine is used.
Use after: Predict phase — Predict: Diagram C, the Garissa telecom mast support wire
Q2. A telecom mast in Garissa is 45 m tall and stands at a right angle to the ground. A support wire is anchored on the ground 45 m from the base of the mast. What angle does the wire make with the ground?
  1. 45°
  2. 30°
  3. 60°
  4. 90°
Correct answer: A
The known sides are opposite (45 m) and adjacent (45 m), so tan θ = 45/45 = 1. The angle whose tangent is 1 is 45°, a special angle.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: worked example 'WORKED EXAMPLE — Nakuru Mast'
Q3. A student stands 40 m from the base of the Nakuru transmission mast. The angle of elevation to the top is 50°. Using tan 50° ≈ 1.1918, what is the height of the mast?
  1. 47.7 m
  2. 33.6 m
  3. 30.6 m
  4. 25.7 m
Correct answer: A
The known side (40 m) is adjacent and the height is opposite, so tan 50° = height/40. Then height = 40 × 1.1918 ≈ 47.7 m.
Use after: Observe phase — Observe: second demonstration, the Kisumu ramp co-constructed with the teacher
Q4. A ramp makes a 12° angle with the ground. The horizontal distance along the ground is 6 m. Using cos 12° ≈ 0.9781, what is the length of the ramp surface, to 2 decimal places?
  1. 1.28 m
  2. 6.13 m
  3. 28.88 m
  4. 5.87 m
Correct answer: B
cos 12° = adjacent/hypotenuse = 6/ramp, so ramp = 6 ÷ 0.9781 ≈ 6.13 m. Multiplying by cos 12° is a rearrangement error and gives a value shorter than 6 m, which is impossible for a hypotenuse.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: pair work, Problem 1 (Mombasa port ramp)
Q5. A ramp at Mombasa port makes a 15° angle with the horizontal. The ramp is 8 m long. What is the vertical height the ramp rises, to 2 decimal places?
  1. 2.07 m
  2. 2.14 m
  3. 30.90 m
  4. 7.73 m
Correct answer: A
The height is opposite 15° and the ramp is the hypotenuse, so sin 15° = height/8. Height = 8 × sin 15° ≈ 8 × 0.2588 ≈ 2.07 m.
Use after: Explain phase — Explain: pair work, Problem 3 (Garissa tower shadow) and the link back to the Nakuru mast
Q6. A surveyor in Garissa measures that a telecom tower casts a shadow 32 m long on flat ground when the sun's angle of elevation is 62°. What is the height of the tower, to 1 decimal place?
  1. 17.0 m
  2. 28.3 m
  3. 15.0 m
  4. 60.2 m
Correct answer: D
The shadow (32 m) is adjacent to 62° and the tower height is opposite, so tan 62° = height/32. Height = 32 × 1.8807 ≈ 60.2 m.
Use after: DQB phase — Driving Question Board: 'What does solving a right-angled triangle completely mean — and can we now do it?'
Q7. After solving a right-angled triangle, a learner writes: 'I know one acute angle and one side. Now I can find the other two sides and the other angle.' What does 'solving a right-angled triangle completely' mean?
  1. Finding every unknown side and angle from one known side and one acute angle
  2. Finding the height of the triangle only
  3. Finding only the hypotenuse
  4. Finding the three ratios sin, cos and tan for the 90° angle
Correct answer: A
Solving completely means finding all unknown sides and angles. With one side and one acute angle, the ratios give the other sides, and the angle sum of 180° gives the remaining angle.
Use after: Model Building phase — Model: writing the decision rule box (SOH if O and H, CAH if A and H, TOA if O and A)
Q8. For a named acute angle in a right-angled triangle, you know the length of the side opposite it and you want the length of the hypotenuse. Which rule from SOH-CAH-TOA should you use?
  1. SOH, because it uses opposite and hypotenuse
  2. CAH, because it uses the adjacent side
  3. TOA, because it uses the opposite side
  4. TOA, because tangent is the ratio to use whenever an angle is given
Correct answer: A
Opposite and hypotenuse are involved, so SOH applies: sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse. Tangent only links opposite and adjacent.
