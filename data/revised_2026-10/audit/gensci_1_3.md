## Final Explanation reviewer findings (unverified; some are false alarms)
- **Part 2(c) exemplar and prompt**: The exemplar says ugali turned blue-black with iodine and that 'glucose and ripe banana' gave brick-red with Benedict's. This is fine, but the exemplar also includes tests the learner is told to pick from. It gives only one correct example for each nutrient, so no problem is introduced. However, the prompt requires 'at least two nutrients' while the exemplar covers four, which is acceptable.
- **Part 4(a) prompt and exemplar**: The exemplar says amino acids from protein and fatty acids 'are absorbed into the bloodstream' through villi. Fatty acids are mainly absorbed into the lacteals (lymph), not directly into the blood capillaries. The exemplar mentions lacteals but then says fatty acids go into the bloodstream, which is inconsistent. Also Part 5 says all digested nutrients 'are absorbed through the villi and microvilli into the blood'.
- **Part 3(a) exemplar**: The exemplar claims protein digestion 'takes longer than starch digestion' because it starts later and involves more sequential steps. Part 3(b) and Lesson 5 also say fat digestion happens later than starch and protein. The exemplar for (b) states fat digestion happens later than starch only, omitting protein, which is a minor mismatch with Lesson 5, which says it is delayed compared to starch and protein.
