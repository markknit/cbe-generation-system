## Final Explanation reviewer findings (unverified; some are false alarms)
- **Part 4 exemplar, wilting explanation**: The exemplar says the leaf wilts because it loses water, then says that 'without water the chloroplasts cannot carry out photosynthesis' and glucose falls. It never states the sequence clearly: the sun/water-loss chain is accepted, but 'the vacuole pushes the cell membrane against the cell wall' is the correct mechanism and the exemplar does not mention that cell death follows from organelle failure beyond this one step. The causal chain is thin, though the main claim is not wrong.
- **Part 1 exemplar (c) vs Lessons 1, 2, 4**: The exemplar names the 'dark oval bodies' as mitochondria and the 'large fluid-filled space' as the vacuole, but the lessons never taught this identification in the phenomenon (Lesson 1 left them as unexplained structures). This is consistent with later lessons, so it is acceptable, but 'folded membranes' is left unidentified while the exemplar adds thylakoid detail.
- **Part 2 exemplar (c) and Part 3 exemplar**: The exemplar says centrioles or lysosomes are found in a human cheek cell but not in the plant cell. Lysosomes are also present in some plant cells, and this claim follows the lessons only as a simplification. The Part 2 exemplar (b) lists the vacuole as absent in animal cells, though animal cells can have small vacuoles.
