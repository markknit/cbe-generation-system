The exemplar does not apply the stated rule consistently.

The prompt asks what the pattern says about trusting estimates from long records, but the coin's 0.5 comes from theory taught in Lesson 4, after Lessons 1-2 which taught only the stabilising of relative frequency. The claim also implies the changes 'became smaller' when the differences are 0.05, 0, 0.025, which are not monotonically decreasing (0.05 then 0 then 0.025).
