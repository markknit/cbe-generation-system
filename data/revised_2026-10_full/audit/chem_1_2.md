## Final Explanation reviewer findings (unverified; some are false alarms)
- **Part 1 exemplar vs Lesson 1 (and Part 5 exemplar)**: The exemplar says that in the Kisumu laboratory potassium permanganate gave violet, and Part 5 says 'potassium permanganate also gave violet, which fits with potassium being the source'. Lesson 1 had the teacher demonstrate the flame tests in class, not in a Kisumu lab. Also, the violet colour of the permanganate solution itself is a confound that the lessons treat as 'not the colour of the solution'. The exemplar uses it as evidence of potassium without noting this.
- **Part 2 exemplar**: The exemplar says the fall that gives sodium's yellow line is 'from the excited level back to the level of the outermost electron'. Bohr's shell 3 is the ground level, so it is vague. It also says only 'a maximum of 2 in shell 1 and 8 in shell 2' with no shell-3 capacity, though the arrangement 2,8,1 is given. This is a minor gap, not an error.
- **Part 3 exemplar (b) and (c)**: The exemplar writes the filling order as '1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, and so on' and then uses 4s before 3d for potassium without stating that 4s fills before 3d. The potassium configuration depends on an ordering rule the lessons never taught.
