Tangent and the Sine-Cosine-Tangent Relationship: Solving the Mango-Tree Problem
Teacher reference only. Keep this open in a separate window or tab while presenting. Do not project.

Asked after: Derivation of tan θ
Q1. In a right-angled triangle, which ratio equals tan θ?
  1. Opposite / Hypotenuse
  2. Adjacent / Hypotenuse
  3. Opposite / Adjacent
  4. Hypotenuse / Opposite
Correct: C
tan θ = O/A. O/H is sin θ and A/H is cos θ. It follows from sin θ ÷ cos θ with H cancelling.
Asked after: Verification table (60° row)
Q2. sin 60° = 0.8660 and cos 60° = 0.5000. Use tan θ = sin θ / cos θ to find tan 60°.
  1. 0.5774
  2. 1.7320
  3. 0.4330
  4. 1.3660
Correct: B
0.8660 ÷ 0.5000 = 1.7320, which matches tan 60° in the tables (1.7321 to four decimal places). 0.5774 is the reciprocal (cos ÷ sin); 0.4330 is the product.
Asked after: Discussion: labelling the mango-tree triangle
Q3. Relative to the 53° angle, the 9.0 m shadow is the triangle’s …
  1. Opposite side
  2. Adjacent side
  3. Hypotenuse
  4. It depends on how tall the tree is
Correct: B
The shadow lies next to the 53° angle and is not the hypotenuse, so it is the adjacent side. The tree height is opposite; the sun ray is the hypotenuse.
Asked after: Solving the mango-tree height
Q4. tan 53° = h / 9.0. Which step correctly finds h?
  1. h = 9.0 ÷ tan 53°
  2. h = 9.0 × tan 53°
  3. h = tan 53° ÷ 9.0
  4. h = 9.0 + tan 53°
Correct: B
Multiply both sides by 9.0 to get h = 9.0 × tan 53°. The lesson plan flags h = 9.0 ÷ tan 53° as the common error.
Asked after: Apply it: flagpole problem
Q5. A flagpole casts a 10 m shadow when the sun angle is 40°. tan 40° = 0.8391. How tall is the flagpole?
  1. 0.84 m
  2. 8.39 m
  3. 10.84 m
  4. 11.92 m
Correct: B
h = 10 × tan 40° = 10 × 0.8391 = 8.39 m. 11.92 m comes from dividing (10 ÷ 0.8391), the same error as in Quick Check 4.
