Asked after: The outer layers (cuticle and epidermis)
Q1. Why is it useful that the cuticle is both waxy and transparent?
  1. It lets light in while reducing water loss
  2. It traps carbon dioxide inside the leaf
  3. It makes food for the leaf
  4. It carries water to the leaf cells
Correct: A
Transparent lets light reach the chloroplast-rich cells below; waxy slows water loss. The cuticle does not photosynthesise or transport water.
Asked after: Palisade and spongy mesophyll
Q2. Which layer is packed tightly with chloroplasts to capture the most light?
  1. Lower epidermis
  2. Palisade mesophyll
  3. Spongy mesophyll
  4. Cuticle
Correct: B
Palisade cells are tall and tightly packed under the upper epidermis, where light is strongest. Spongy mesophyll is loosely packed for gas movement.
Asked after: Stomata and guard cells
Q3. What controls how wide a stoma opens?
  1. The cuticle
  2. The two guard cells around it
  3. The palisade cells
  4. The xylem vessels
Correct: B
Each stoma is surrounded by two guard cells that change shape to open or close the pore. HOW they do this is left as a DQB question for later in the unit.
Asked after: Vascular bundles (xylem and phloem)
Q4. Which tissue carries water from the roots up into the leaf?
  1. Phloem
  2. Xylem
  3. Palisade mesophyll
  4. Epidermis
Correct: B
Xylem carries water and minerals up from the roots. Phloem carries sugars made in the leaf to the rest of the plant.
Asked after: Putting it together (the wilting explanation)
Q5. Using today’s evidence, why does the sukuma wiki wilt at midday even though the soil is moist?
  1. The soil dries out faster in strong sunlight
  2. Water vapour leaves through open stomata faster than xylem can replace it
  3. The roots stop absorbing water when it is hot
  4. Chlorophyll breaks down in direct sunlight
Correct: B
The lesson's payoff: open stomata in bright light lose water vapour faster than the xylem resupplies it, so cells lose water and the leaf droops. The soil stays moist, so A and C do not fit the phenomenon.
